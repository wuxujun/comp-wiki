# PBL-S02-自然密码-CS Outlines

green dot icon

**Level 1 (TOEFL 60 / G5-G6) 发现并描述自然中的分形，理解自相似的概念。**

**关键词：** 基础入门、观察描述、简单运用

## Session 1 : The Magic Question: Can a Snowflake Go Around the World? / 神奇问题：一片雪花能绕地球一周吗？

### (1) Learning Objectives 学习目标:

* Understand what fractals are, and observe the self-similar patterns in nature.

* 了解什么是分形，观察自然界中的自相似图案。

### (2) Student Task 学生任务:

* Discuss the natural fractals that you like or have seen, and describe their fractal patterns.

* 讨论你喜欢或见过的大自然中的分形，并描述它的分形模式。

### (3) Homework 作业:

* Find 2-3 fractal images in nature. Write a short paragraph for each, describing how they are self-similar, as the first part of your final digital portfolio.

* 找到 2-3 张自然分形图片。为每张图片写一小段话，描述它们如何自相似，作为你的最终数字作品集的第一部分。

## Session 2 : Drawing the Koch Snowflake: The Answer is in the Steps / 画科赫雪花：答案藏在每一步里

### (1) Learning Objectives 学习目标:

* Learn the concept of iteration and practice drawing the classic Koch Snowflake to understand its structure.

* 学习“迭代”的概念，并通过绘制科赫雪花来理解它的结构。

### (2) Student Task 学生任务:

* Follow the steps to manually draw the first three iterations of the Koch Snowflake.
* 手动绘制科赫雪花的前三步迭代。

** (3) Homework 作业: **

* Complete the forth iteration of the Koch Snowflake. Write a short description of the steps you took, and save this image and text for your final digital portfolio.
* 完成科赫雪花的第四步迭代。写一小段话描述你的绘制步骤，将这张图和文字保存到你的最终数学作品集。

---

# Session 3 : Uncovering the Math Code: The Rule of the Snowflake / 揭示数学密码：雪花的规则

** (1) Learning Objectives 学习目标: **

* Discover the mathematical patterns in the number of segments and total length of a fractal.
* 发现分形图案中线段数量和总长度的数学规律。

** (2) Student Task 学生任务: **

* Create a table to track how the number of segments and total length change with each iteration of the Koch Snowflake.
* 制作一个表格，记录科赫雪花每一轮迭代后线段数量和总长度的变化。

** (3) Homework 作业: **

* Write a short section for your report, explaining the math patterns you found in the Koch Snowflake. Use the table you created to show the number sequences.
* 为你的报告写一小段，解释你在科赫雪花中发现的数学规律。用你创建的表格来展示这些数列。

---

# Session 4 : Beyond the Snowflake: The Superpowers of Fractals / 超越雪花：分形的超能力

** (1) Learning Objectives 学习目标: **

* Learn about the interesting applications of fractals in the real world.
* 了解分形在现实世界中的有趣应用。

** (2) Student Task 学生任务: **

* Choose one of the discussed applications that interests you most and share a reason why you think the fractal shape is important for it.
* 选择一个你最感兴趣的应用，并分享一个你认为分形形状对其很重要的理由。
**(3) Homework 作业:**

* Write the "Applications of Fractals" section of your portfolio, explaining one or two applications and why their fractal shape is key.

* 撰写作品集中的“分形的应用”部分，解释一到两个应用案例以及为什么分形形状是关键。

---

# Session 5：My Fractal Portfolio: From Parts to Whole / 我的分形作品集：从部分到整体

**(1) Learning Objectives 学习目标:**

* Understand the structure of a good report and organize previous work into a complete digital portfolio.

* 了解一份好的报告的结构，并将之前的作业整合为一个完整的数字作品集。

**(2) Student Task 学生任务:**

* In class, with teacher guidance, organize your findings from Sessions 1-4.

* 课堂上，在老师的指导下，将第 1-4 课的发现进行整理。

**(3) Homework 作业:**

* Finalize and polish your complete digital portfolio, and practice your oral presentation for the final session.

* 完成并润色你的完整数字作品集，并为最后一课的口语展示进行练习。

---

# Session 6：Young Mathematician Showcase Day 小小数学家展示日

**(1) Learning Objectives 学习目标:**

* Confidently give an oral presentation and share your project findings.

* 自信地进行口语展示，分享你的项目发现。

**(2) Student Task 学生任务:**

* Give a 5-7 minute oral presentation, using your digital portfolio to showcase your favorite discoveries and analyses.

* 进行 5-7 分钟的口语展示，用你的数字作品集来展示你最喜欢的发现和分析。

**(3) Extension Activity 拓展活动:**

* **内容: 分形维度的初探。**在学生完成展示后，老师将通过直观的例子和比喻（例如，线的维度是 1，平面的维度是 2），引导学生讨论分形维度的概念。通过比较科赫雪花和直线的“粗糙度”，让学生理解非整数维度的含义。

* **Content: An Introduction to Fractal Dimension.** After the student's presentation, the teacher will use intuitive examples and analogies (e.g., a line has
dimension 1, a plane has dimension 2) to lead a discussion on the concept of fractal dimension. By comparing the "roughness" of the Koch Snowflake and a straight line, students will understand the meaning of a non-integer dimension.

# Level 2 (TOEFL 80 / G7-G8)：系统性地分析分形属性，理解迭代和序列的代数表达

**关键词**：数学计算、代数表达、结构化写作

---

## Session 1：The Magic Question: Can a Snowflake Go Around the World? / 神奇问题：一片雪花能绕地球一周吗？

*   **(1) Learning Objectives 学习目标:**
    

    *   Understand the core concept of fractals through an intriguing question.
    

    *   通过一个有趣的问题，了解分形的核心概念。

*   **(2) Student Task 学生任务:**
    

    *   Discuss the title question and make a guess, then analyze a fractal example and describe its mathematical features.
    

    *   讨论这个标题问题并做出你的猜想，然后分析一个分形例子，并描述其数学特征。

*   **(3) Homework 作业:**
    

    *   Draft the introduction of your digital portfolio. Clearly define fractals and state your key observations about their mathematical properties.
    

    *   撰写你的数字作品集的引言部分。清晰定义分形并提出你的核心观察。

---

## Session 2：The Infinite Path: A Math Journey into the Koch Snowflake 无穷之路：科赫雪花的数学旅程

**(1) Learning Objectives 学习目标:**

*   Understand the iterative rules of the Koch Snowflake and describe its sequences using algebraic expressions.
*   理解科赫雪花的迭代规则，并用代数式描述其序列。

**(2) Student Task 学生任务:**
* Record the number of segments and total length of the Koch Snowflake at each iteration in a table and express the change using algebraic expressions.
* 制作一个表格，记录科赫雪花每一轮迭代的线段数量和总长度，并用代数式表示其变化。

### (3) Homework 作业:

* Write the first part of your portfolio. Write the "Mathematical Analysis of the Koch Snowflake" section, including your table and algebraic expressions.

* 撰写你的数字作品集的第一部分。写“科赫雪花的数学分析”部分，附上你的表格和代数表达式。

## Session 3 : The Fractal Paradox: Area vs. Perimeter 分形悖论：面积与周长

### (1) Learning Objectives 学习目标:

* Understand the paradox of infinite perimeter and finite area in fractals.

* 理解分形中“无限周长，有限面积”的悖论。

### (2) Student Task 学生任务:

* Orally explain your understanding of the area and perimeter paradox of the Koch Snowflake.

* 口头阐述你对科赫雪花面积和周长悖论的理解。

### (3) Homework 作业:

* Write the second part of your portfolio. Write the "Fractal Paradox" section, using clear text and calculations to explain this phenomenon.

* 撰写你的数字作品集的第二部分。写“分形悖论”部分，用清晰的文字和计算来解释这一现象。

---

## Session 4 : The Code of Roughness: The Concept of Fractal Dimension 粗糙度的密码：分形维度的概念

### (1) Learning Objectives 学习目标:

* Understand the concept of fractal dimension and its importance.

* 理解分形维度的概念及其重要性。

### (2) Student Task 学生任务:

* Discuss the dimension values of several simple fractals (e.g., a line is 1D, the Koch curve is 1.26D) and explain their meaning.

* 讨论几个简单分形的维度值（例如，线是 1 维，科赫曲线是 1.26 维），并解释其含义。

### (3) Homework 作业:

* Write the third part of your portfolio. Write the "Concept of Fractal Dimension" section, explaining why fractal dimensions are often non-integers and giving a few examples.

* 撰写你的数字作品集的第三部分。写“分形维度的概念”部分，解释为什么分形维度通常是
非整数，并举几个你感兴趣的例子。

# Session 1：Fractal Theory: The Beauty of Math 分形理论：数学之美

(1) Learning Objectives 学习目标:

Build a systematic understanding of fractal geometry and its fundamental mathematical principles.

建立对分形几何的系统性认知，并了解其基本数学原理。

(2) Student Task 学生任务:

Understand the basic mathematical theories of fractal geometry, and describe the mathematical characteristics of a fractal pattern in a structured paragraph.

了解分形几何的基本数学理论，并用结构化段落描述一个分形图案数学特征。

# Session 2：Iteration & Dimension: Quantitative Analysis of Classic Fractals 迭代与维度：经典分形的量化分析

(1) Learning Objectives 学习目标:

Master the construction of classic fractals and perform mathematical dimension calculations.

掌握经典分形的构建方法，并进行维度的数学计算。

(2) Student Task 学生任务:

Manually derive and calculate the dimension of the Koch Snowflake and Sierpinski Triangle.

手动推导并计算科赫雪花和谢尔宾斯基三角形的维度。

# Session 3：The Beauty of Limits: Infinite Perimeter, Finite Area 极限之美：无限周长与有限面积

(1) Learning Objectives 学习目标:

Rigorously prove a mathematical paradox in fractals.

严谨地证明分形中的数学悖论。

(2) Student Task 学生任务:

Explain your understanding of the area and perimeter paradox of the Koch Snowflake.
阐述你对科赫雪花面积和周长悖论的理解。
