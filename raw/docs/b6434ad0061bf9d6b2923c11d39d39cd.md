# Session 1 – What Is an Argument? Claims, Reasons, Evidence

## 认识议论文：主张、理由与证据

**Learning Goals 学习目标**

* Understand what argumentative writing is and how it differs from facts or opinions

* Identify claims, reasons, and evidence in short texts

* Learn how these parts work together to form an argument

* 理解什么是议论文，以及它与事实、个人感受的区别

* 能识别文本中的主张、理由和证据

* 理解论证结构的基本逻辑关系

**Student Task 学生任务**

* Identify the claim, reason, and evidence in given examples

* Complete short exercises sorting sentences into claim / reason / evidence

* 标注范例中的主张、理由与证据

* 完成句子分类练习，巩固论证结构理解

**Homework after Session 1：**

Write one short paragraph stating a claim and one supporting reason.

# Session 2 – Writing Strong Claims

## 如何写出清晰、有力、可辩论的主张

**Learning Goals 学习目标**

* Distinguish between weak and strong claims

* Learn how to make claims clear, specific, and debatable

* Practice refining vague or overly general claims

* 区分弱主张与强主张

* 学习让主张更清晰、具体、可辩论

* 练习修改模糊或过于宽泛的观点

**Student Task 学生任务**
* Revise weak claims into stronger versions
* Discuss whether sample claims are debatable and focused
* 将弱主张改写为强主张
* 讨论主张是否具有可争论性与明确立场

**Homework after Session 2 :**

Write 2–3 revised claims on a chosen topic by applying different thinking strategies.

# Session 3 – Developing Reasons

## 展开理由

**Learning Goals 学习目标**

* Learn how to develop reasons that directly support a claim from different perspectives
* Understand why undeveloped or repeated reasons weaken arguments
* Study how different reasons can work together as sub points for a main claim
* 学习如何从不同角度展开与主张直接相关的理由
* 理解理由空泛或重复的问题
* 练习如何用不同的理由构架主论点的分论点

**Student Task 学生任务**

* Generate multiple reasons for one claim using different categories
* Organize the reasons to form an outline of the main argument and its sub points
* 从不同角度为同一主张构思理由
* 将不同理由整合到统一的论述大纲下

**Homework after Session 3 :**

Write at least 3 reasons (from at least 3 different categories) as three sub points for your claim about a chosen topic.

# Session 4 – Using Evidence Effectively Part 1

## 如何选择与分析证据

**Learning Goals 学习目标**

* Learn different types of evidence (data, facts, logic)
* Distinguish relevant evidence from irrelevant or weak evidence
* Understand why explanation and analysis matter
* 学习不同类型的证据（数据、事实、逻辑、个人经历）
* 判断证据是否相关、有力
* 理解“分析解释”在说服中的重要性

**Student Task 学生任务**

* Match evidence types to claims and reasons
* Practice searching for and filtering factual evidence
* 为主张与理由匹配合适证据
* 练习查找并筛选事实证据

**Homework after Session 4：**

Use three pieces evidence (data, facts, or logical reasoning) to support your claim on a chosen topic.

# Session 5 – Using Evidence Effectively Part 2

## 完整议论文结构与整合

**Learning Goals 学习目标**

* Learn to use anecdotes as evidence
* Distinguish relevant evidence from irrelevant or weak evidence
* Understand the structure of a full argumentative essay
* 学会使用叙事作为论据
* 判断证据是否相关、有力
* 理解完整议论文的结构

**Student Task 学生任务**

* Match narrative evidence to the claims
* Master how to write effective anecdotes
* 为论点匹配事件论据
* 学会写作有效的事件论据

**Homework after Session 5：**

Write a complete argumentative essay using skills from Sessions 1–5.
# Session 6 – Argumentative Writing Workshop & Feedback

**议论文修改工作坊：反馈与提升**

## Learning Goals 学习目标

* Recognize common persuasive writing problems

* Strengthen clarity, depth, and insight in arguments

* Learn how to revise writing based on feedback

* 识别议论文中的常见问题

* 提升论证的清晰度、深度与洞察力

* 学习如何根据反馈进行修改

## Workshop Focus 常见问题聚焦

* Vague or overly broad claims

* Reasons that are listed but not developed

* Evidence that is irrelevant or insufficient

* Lack of explanation, analysis, or insight

* 主张模糊或范围过大

* 理由未展开、仅停留在表面

* 证据不相关或支撑不足

* 缺乏分析与深层思考

## Student Task 学生任务

* Revise key sections (claim, body paragraph, evidence explanation)

* Reflect on one major improvement made

* 修改关键部分（主张、段落、证据分析）

* 反思自己最大的写作提升点

## Final Outcome 课程最终成果

* One revised argumentative essay demonstrating clear persuasion

* A visible progression from opinion-based writing to structured argument

* 一篇经过修改的完整议论文

* 从“表达观点”到“有效说服”的能力跃迁
