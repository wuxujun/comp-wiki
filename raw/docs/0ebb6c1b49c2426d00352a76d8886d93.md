# 历史人物分析：用戏剧重现历史人物
**Historical Figure Theatre Course: Recreating Historical Figures’ Dilemmas and Eras through Theatre Arts**
9课时 / 9 sessions

## 课程概述 Overview

本课程旨在引导学生独立研究一位历史人物的生平与时代背景，通过戏剧写作与艺术表达，展现对人物内心世界与时代背景的理解。

This course guide students to independently research the life and historical context of a historical figure, and through theatrical writing and creative expression, explore and present their personal interpretation of the character's inner world and legacy.

## 适合学员 Suitable for Students

* 热爱历史、戏剧、艺术和写作的学生

* 未来计划申请人文、历史、写作和戏剧相关专业的学生

* 渴望提升创意表达、跨学科能力和背景提升

* Passionate about history, theatre, arts and writing

* Future applicants to history, social sciences, writing, or arts programs

* Those eager to enhance creative expression, interdisciplinary skills and improve personal activities

## 课程模块 Syllabus

### 01 历史主题剧本赏析 Historical Drama Script Analysis
1课时 1 lesson

通过经典历史改编剧本案例，解析作者在人物塑造、情节编排与冲突设计上的艺术处理手法。
Analyze historically adapted scripts, focusing on the author's techniques in characterization, plot structure, and conflict design.

### 02 戏剧创作技巧学习 Script Writing Techniques
2课时 2 lessons

系统讲解剧本核心要素（如对白、舞台指示）与写作规范，掌握创作格式与技巧。
Introduce script writing elements (e.g. dialogue, stage directions) and techniques.

### 03 题材与大纲 Theme & Outline
2课时 2 lessons

指导学生选定创作题材，探讨多视角叙事对故事的影响；研究历史人物行为动机，构建立体戏剧角色。
Guide students in selecting themes and exploring how narrative perspectives shape stories. Analyze historical figures' motivations to develop layered characters.

### 04 作品创作与修改 Script Drafting & Revision
3课时 3 lessons

学生完成剧本初稿，完善剧情逻辑与角色设定。
Students draft their scripts, refine plot and character development.

### 05 剧本终稿 Finish Script
1课时 1 lesson

学生完成剧本终稿。
Finish Script.

## 课程亮点 Course Highlights

### Key Deliverables & Student Development

* 学生将围绕感兴趣的历史人物进行独立创作，通过深度调研与艺术表达，完成具有个性化和原创性的戏剧项目，体现个人创意与学术思辨能力。
Students will independently create theatre project based on historical figures of their choice. Through in-depth research and artistic expression, they will showcase personal creativity and critical thinking.

* 完成一个5页的短剧剧本，围绕人物的心理挣扎或历史抉择展开。
Students will develop a 5-page play script focusing on a major emotional or moral dilemma in the character's life.

### Engaging & Exploratory

* 以导演和编剧的视角，深入挖掘历史人物的关键抉择、情感动机及时代背景。
Adopt the perspective of a drama director and playwright to explore a historical figure's pivotal choices, emotional motivations, and historical background.

* 通过观看历史题材话剧片段，直观感受舞台如何重构历史，包括服装、布景、台词对时代氛围的暗示。
Observe historical plays to analyze how staging reconstruct history, examining how costume, set design, and dialogue can demonstrate the atmosphere of the era.

### Enhance Creativity and Writing Skills

* 通过跨学科创作（历史 + 戏剧），学生将培养独立调研、批判性思考与艺术表达能力。
By combining history and theatre, students will build interdisciplinary skills in independent research, critical thinking, and creative performance.

* 最终成果可作为学术或升学作品集的重要组成部分。
The final project can serve as a valuable piece for academic or portfolio use.
