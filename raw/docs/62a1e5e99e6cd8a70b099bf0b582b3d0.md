# 一周生存预算挑战：住在美国不同州要花多少钱？
How Much to Survive?
A One-Week Budget Challenge Across U.S. States

7课时 / 7 sessions

Illustration of U.S. landmarks including Statue of Liberty and Golden Gate Bridge

## 项目简介 Project Summary

学生将模拟自己即将搬去美国某个州（如德州、加州、纽约、北卡等）生活，了解并比较各州在住房、食品、交通和娱乐等方面的生活成本，制作可视化预算对比图，并用英文阐述自己的选址理由。

Students will role-play as teenagers planning to live in different U.S. states (e.g., Texas, California, New York, North Carolina). They will research the cost of living --- housing, food, transportation, and entertainment- and create a visualized budget comparison chart. Finally, they'll present tehir ideal location to live in English.

## A Message to Parents

这项PBL课题旨在帮助学生：
* 理解预算与生活成本的现实意义
* 训练英文演讲与表达自信
* 运用逻辑句型表达选择与比较理由
* 初步接触经济学与个人财务知识

This project builds real-world financial awareness and critical thinking skills. Students will:

* Learn the basics of budgeting and cost of living

* Develop presentation and speaking confidence in English

* Practice using logical structures for preference and comparison

* Gain exposure to economics and personal finance concepts

## 适合年级 Target Age

* 难度将根据不同年龄层灵活调整
* 高年级学生将融入更加深度的经济分析

The level of difficulty will be flexibly adjusted based on age.

Older students will engage in more advanced economic analysis.
