# 082225 旋律时光机：我的音乐故事播客 PBL Syllabus V2 from 李昕怡

## Level 1 – 基础表达者（TOEFL ~60）

## Session 1 – 我的旋律记忆

学习目标：

* 理解“回忆录（Memoir）”与“播客（Podcast）”的含义，认识音乐与记忆的关联

* 能从样例中标出五句框架（Hook/When-Where/Detail/Feeling/Closing

* 能说出与熟悉歌曲相关的 1–2 个小记忆

写作任务：

* 句型框架：When I hear [song], I [reaction]. In [time/place], [who] [action]. I felt [feeling].

* 使用五句框架完成 3–5 句初步草稿（60 词内，偏口语，便于朗读）

* 样例赏析脚本（朗读/播放，约 85 词）：

“When I hear the first drum beat of ‘We Will Rock You’, my feet start to tap. In fifth grade, our school bus driver played it every Friday after the last bell. The whole bus clapped the rhythm on the seats, and even the shy kids smiled. That song felt like a small party after a long week; it made me feel brave and included. Now the beat is my shortcut back to those bright, noisy rides.”

互动提示：

* “有一首歌一响起，你脑中会跳出哪个画面？先说 3 个关键词（人/地点/情绪）。”

* “第二遍听样例，请给每句打标：H / WW / D / F / C，并说出理由。

* “回忆录是什么？播客是什么？为什么用音频讲这段记忆更打动人？”

* 播客微技能：手机距口 5–10cm，找软装角落降回声；逗号轻停、句尾降调更自然。
# Session 2 – 建立音乐回忆框架

## 学习目标：

* 学会用五句结构+时间连接词（first/then/next/finally）组织故事顺序

* 能画出“事件时间线”，用口头 4 句复述样例

* 完成一段含“顺序+感受变化”的回忆（60–80 词）

## 写作任务：

* 在 Session 1 草稿上补充至少 2 个顺序词和 1 句“感受变化”（At first..., later...）

* 草拟第一篇音乐记忆短文（60–80 词）

* 样例赏析脚本（约 90 词）：

“The first time I heard ‘A Thousand Miles’, I was in my aunt’s car. First, the piano notes fell like soft rain on the window. Then my aunt sang, and I tried to follow her words. Next, we laughed when I mixed the chorus. I felt warm and safe, as if the car was a tiny moving home. Finally, when we parked, I wished the song could start again.”

## 互动提示：

* “圈出样例中的所有顺序词，用箭头连出事件顺序线。”

* “用‘先…再…然后…最后…’把样例口头复述成 4 句。”

* “你的故事里情绪有变化吗？加一句：At first I felt..., but later I felt...”

* 播客微技能：目标语速每分钟 110–130 词；长句断为短句再读，清晰度更高。

# Session 3 – 学会表达我的感受

## 学习目标：

* 学习并应用 10 个基本情绪词（happy, calm, excited, proud, safe, sad, nervous, relaxed, loved, nostalgic）

* 会用一个明喻（It was like...）和一个五感细节让情绪可感

* 完成 100 词 Memoir #1 最终稿（便于朗读录音）

## 写作任务：
* 在草稿中加入 2-3 个情感细节、1 个五感细节、1 个明喻；完成 100 词最终版 Memoir #1

* 样例赏析脚本（约 85 词）：

“When my grandmother played her lullaby on the old radio, I felt calm. Her hands moved slowly, and the room smelled like tea and soap. I felt peaceful, loved, and a little nostalgic, as if time walked backward. When the music stopped, the house became too quiet. Even now, that simple tune is like a warm blanket, and it brings me comfort on hard days.”

## 互动提示：

* “圈出样例里的情绪词（≥3 个）与五感细节（声音/气味/触感各 1 处）。”

* “把‘I felt happy.’升级为更具体的一句（加入原因或五感，如 because... / It sounded like...）。”

* “试写一个明喻：It was like [sunlight/rain/blanket/heartbeat]。”

* 播客微技能：微笑声线更温暖；同一句读两遍（带/不带微笑）对比效果。

# Session 4 – 让语言更生动

## 学习目标：

* 识别“平淡句”，用具体动词/形容词替换（good→bright/gentle；feel→grow/sense）

* 至少添加 1 个明喻+1 个五感描写润色文本

* 能听辨润色前后在画面感和情绪传达上的差别

## 写作任务：

* 选自己稿子中的 3 句“平淡句”，完成“具体化替换”；产出 Memoir #1 润色版

* 样例赏析脚本（前后对比）：

Before（约 45 词）：“The song was good. We listened in the gym. I felt happy. My friends sang. I liked it.”

After（约 70 词）：“The chorus rose like sunlight through the high windows. In the gym, the wooden floor echoed under our feet. Our voices sounded bright, and I felt proud and excited, as if the morning opened just for us.”

## 互动提示：
* “高亮样例 After 里的升级点：更具体的动词/形容词/五感/明喻各 1 处。”

* “把你文中的一句普通句，用样例的方法升级一次（至少改 2 处词/加 1 处细节）。”

* “朗读 Before 与 After 各一次：你听到了哪些差别（节奏/画面/情感）？”

* 播客微技能：读到逗号轻停，句末降调。

## Session 5 – 独立写作第二篇

学习目标：

* 在新话题中再次运用五句结构与情感/五感/明喻等工具

* 完成 Memoir #2 草稿并进行 45–60 秒的首次录音

写作任务：

* 选择一个音乐记忆话题，自由写作 80–100 词；含 2 个顺序词、2 个情绪词、1 个五感或明喻

* 初录 Memoir #2 音频（含开场欢迎与结尾致谢）

* 样例选题提纲（可仿写）：

- 体育馆加油歌：Hook “When the drum started, my hands clapped by themselves.” / Context “At the school game last winter…”

- 厨房哼唱：Hook “When Mom hummed in the kitchen, the air felt warm…” / Detail “The pan sizzled; the room smelled like ginger.”

互动提示：

* “先写五句大纲，再扩展为完整句（每句 12–15 词内，便于朗读）。”

* “加入一处‘感觉变化’句：At first…, but later…”

* “录前在稿子上标注停顿点（逗号/句号）。”

* 播客微技能：开场/收束模板——“Welcome to My Melody Memoir… Thanks for listening.”

## Session 6 – 反思与润色

学习目标：
* 反思表达与写作的成长，完成两篇最终音乐回忆录文本
* 选择一篇完成入门播客成品音频（清晰、自然、有情感）

写作任务：

写一段学习反思（3–4 句）：我学会了…；最难的是…；下次我会…

提交：Memoir #1 最终稿 + Memoir #2 最终稿 + 其中一篇的最终音频（30–60 秒）+ 标题

反思样例句型：

“I learned to use simple similes and sense words to make my story clear. The hardest part was slowing down my voice. Next time, I will mark pauses and smile while I read.”

互动提示：

* “回顾两篇短文，哪一处修改最有效？为什么（更具体/更有画面/更有情绪）？”

* “选一句‘金句’，读 3 遍，选择最自然的一次作为成品片段的中心句。

* “自评清单：结构完整？细节≥2？情绪明确？音量/语速合适？”

* 播客微技能：正式录音前留 3 秒环境底噪，读完后留 1 秒静音，便于剪辑与淡出。

# Level 2 – 进阶讲述者（TOEFL ~80）

## Session 1 – 配乐瞬间：隐喻点亮记忆

学习目标：

* 探索音乐如何唤起记忆和情感

* 理解反思性个人故事的结构

* 练习使用隐喻/比喻描述情感反应

写作任务：

* 选择一首有意义的歌曲，使用修辞语言写出与其相关的记忆（3-5 句）（承接 L1：可先用五句框架快速列出 When-Where/Detail/Feeling，再凝练为 3–5 句并加入比喻）

互动提示：

* “你们有没有一首歌曲，能让你瞬间回到某个特定的时刻？那个时刻是什么样的？”（可先说
出 L1 里的 3 个关键词：人/地点/情绪）

* “尝试用隐喻或比喻来描述你听到这首歌时的感受，比如‘这首歌像一阵温暖的阳光’。”

* “在你的记忆中，这首歌的旋律和歌词如何影响了你的情绪？”（可先用 L1 的因果句型：I felt... because...）

## Session 2 – 多感官叙事：场景更立体

**学习目标：**

* 学习将五感融合进叙事写作

* 能指出在草稿中添加感官语言的位置

**写作任务：**

* 重写记忆片段，加入听觉、视觉、嗅觉等感官细节（承接 L1：先按时间顺序标注“first/then/next/finally”，再在关键节点叠加 2–3 种感官）

**互动提示：**

* “回忆一下你当时的环境，你能听到什么声音？看到什么颜色？”（先锁定 L1 里的 When/Where 句位）

* “在重写时，尝试加入一些你能闻到的气味，比如食物的香气或花香。”

* “你的故事中有哪些细节可以通过感官来增强？比如描述某个场景的气氛？”（避免只说‘好听/好看’，换成可被感知的细节）

## Session 3 –对话与心声：人物更鲜活

**学习目标：**

* 使用对话展现情感与人物性格

* 加入内心独白加深个人反思

**写作任务：**

* 给故事加入 2 句对话和 1 句内心独白（承接 L1“展示而非说明”：用对白与动作替代直白情绪说明，如 Not “I was nervous” → “我搓着手说：‘没事。’”）
**互动提示：**

* “想象一下你和谁在一起，能用对话展示你们的情感吗？”（在 L1 的 Detail 句位插入对白）

* “在你的故事中，加入一个内心独白，表达你当时的真实感受。”

* “对话如何帮助你更好地展示人物性格和情感变化？”（可配一个动作节拍，如停顿/目光/手势）

## Session 4 – 开场与收束：结构更有力

**学习目标：**

* 构建有吸引力的开头和令人满意的结尾

* 学习如何“聚焦”和“拉远”镜头进行叙述

**写作任务：**

* 修改故事首尾句，增强结构（承接 L1：用 Hook 位置做“在场感”开头；用 Closing 位置回响前文意象或情绪，形成呼应）

**互动提示：**

* “你的故事开头是否足够吸引人？试着用一个引人入胜的场景开始。”（把 L1 的 Hook 从总结句升级为画面句）

* “结尾部分如何能够让读者感到满足？你想传达什么样的情感？”（回扣开头的物象/声音/一句歌词）

* “在叙述中，如何有效地使用‘聚焦’和‘拉远’的技巧来增强故事的深度？”（聚焦=具体细节；拉远=一句抽象领悟）

## Session 5 – 稿件到配音：第二篇完成

**学习目标：**

* 将完整叙述技巧运用于新故事

* 练习有表现力和清晰度的配音

**写作任务：**

* 完成第二篇 100 词 Memoir 并准备配音稿（承接 L1：保留顺序词与情绪变化句，新增停
顿/重读标注以提升口播表现）

互动提示：

* “选择一个新的故事主题，确保它与你的个人经历相关联。”（沿用 L1：先起五句大纲，再扩展到 100 词）

* “在准备配音稿时，考虑如何通过语调和情感来增强你的叙述。”（标出逗号/句号处的停顿）

* “你希望听众在听到你的配音时感受到什么？如何通过声音传达这些情感？”（挑 1 句“核心句”做重读）

# Session 6 – 制作与回望：音频双成品

**学习目标：**

* 反思讲述技巧与音频呈现成长

* 完成两个音频回忆录

**写作任务：**

* 提交两篇配乐录音 Memoir，作为结课展示（承接 L1 反思：补充 3–4 句自评，说明最有效的修改与朗读策略）

互动提示：

* “在制作音频时，你觉得哪些技巧最有效？为什么？”（从 L1 到 L2：具体到某个比喻/感官/停顿的效果）

* “回顾你的创作过程，你认为自己在哪些方面有了成长？”

* “你希望听众从你的音频中获得什么？你的故事传达了怎样的情感？”（可引用一处你在结尾设置的“回响”）

# Level 3 – 高阶表达者 (TOEFL ~100)
# Session 1 – 音乐与身份：确立个人主张

## 学习目标：

* 建立清晰的“身份主张”（我是谁/我属于哪里/我坚持什么），将回忆提升为主题表达

* 通过语境化（时代/家庭/社群/语言）与控制性意象贯穿叙述

## 写作任务：

* 选一首影响你身份认同的歌，写 120–150 词短文；包含：一个明确主张、至少一个文化或社群细节、一个贯穿性意象（文中出现≥2 次）

## 互动提示：

* “这首歌如何让‘我是谁’更清晰？用‘因为…所以…’写出你的主张。”

* “加入一个文化线索（语言/节日/地点/服饰/家族规训）支撑主张。”

* “若把你的身份比作一个意象，这首歌在哪个瞬间让它‘发光’？”

# Session 2 – 体裁探索：结构放大主题

## 学习目标：

* 理解并评估非常规 memoir 结构（书信体、播放清单注释、双时间线、碎片化段落/脚注等）

* 选择一种体裁服务主题，并设置清晰的结构信号（小标题/编号/时间戳/歌词引子）

## 写作任务：

* 三选一开写：a) 写给过去/未来自己的信；b) “我的人生播放清单”（3 首歌+注释）；c) 双时间线（过去/现在交错）

* 完成结构提纲+首段约 120 词草稿，并明确结构标记

## 互动提示：

* “你选的体裁在哪一点最能放大主题？举‘结构×内容’互相加分的例子。”

* “转场如何被‘看见/听见’？试用时间戳、歌词、地点名或编号提示。”

* “播放清单里的每首歌分别承担了什么叙事功能（开端/转折/回响）？”

# Session 3 – 语调与距离：提升可信度与影响力

## 学习目标：

* 调整叙述者与“当年的我”的叙事距离，平衡当时感受与现在理解

* 校准语调与受众定位，增强真实感与说服力

* 用暗示与对照替代直陈，增加表达层次
写作任务：

* 将草稿中 3 处直说情绪/观点改为间接呈现（动作/细节/对照）

* 新增 2–3 句“现在的我”的反思以框住主题；写 2 句作者说明，明确目标读者与希望被记住的核心句

互动提示：

* “哪一句太像结论？能否用细节或对照让读者自己得出？”

* “你的目标读者是谁？为这个读者应补充或删减哪些背景信息？”

* “现在的我如何评价当年的我——是拉近理解，还是保持距离？”

# Session 4 – 节奏与精炼：打造高质量文本

学习目标：

* 通过句式节奏（长短错落、重复回环）塑造情绪并提升可读性

* 精简语言：删除冗余、替换弱动词与空洞形容

* 用“重复线索”贯穿全文：用同一物品/声音/颜色反复出现，连接段落与情感。

写作任务：

* 将稿件精炼至 120–150 词：删减≥15%冗余；用更具体的动词/名词替换≥3 处弱词（如 be/very/really）

* 确保文中至少出现一处回环句

互动提示：

* “读稿时哪些词删掉不损失意义？先剪口头语与套话。”

* “能否用一个重复意象或短语把段落‘粘’起来？”

* “把一处长句切成两句试读，节奏是否更有‘呼吸感’？”

# Session 5 – 声音与表达：朗读策略与呈现

学习目标：

* 设计朗读策略（停顿、重读、语速与语调）让主题被“听见”

* 将文字结构转化为清晰自然的口头表达（无需多媒体剪辑）

写作任务：

* 准备 60–90 秒朗读稿，在文本中标注停顿与重读；进行两次对比朗读（平稳版/情绪版），自选更合适的一版（可用手机录音留存，非必选）
互动提示：

* “哪一句是核心句？前后各留多少‘呼吸’让它更突出？”

* “哪些词需要重读？哪些从句可放慢以帮助理解？”

* “删掉哪些口头赘词，朗读会更清晰自然？”

**Session 6 – 发布与洞见：终期反思**

学习目标：

* 综合呈现“身份主题×体裁选择×表达策略”的成果

* 以受众意识与伦理意识反思创作影响

写作任务：

* 提交最终文本（120–150 词），课堂朗读呈现；撰写 80–120 词英文反思，说明受众定位、体裁选择理由与表达策略的调整（如停顿/重读）

互动提示：

* “哪一处修订最有效？它如何改变了读者的理解或情绪？”

* “若作品涉及他人，你如何处理细节以兼顾真实性与尊重？”

* “下一步你最想精进哪项能力（主题塑造/体裁驾驭/语调与距离/朗读表达）？”

# 073025 Melodies and Momories PBL Syllabus by Xinyi Li

**Level 1 – Basic Expressers (TOEFL ~60)**

**Session 1 – My Melodic Memories**

Learning Objectives:

* Understand the meaning of "memoir" and recognize the connection between music and memory.

* Be able to share small memories related to familiar songs.

Writing Task:

* Use sentence frameworks to describe musical memories.
* Complete a preliminary draft of 3-5 sentences.

Instructor Prompts:

1. “Do you have a special song that reminds you of a particular moment? Can you share it?”

2. “What is the first image that comes to your mind when you hear this song?”

3. “What is a memoir? How can we record our memories through music?”

## Session 2 – Building a Framework for Musical Memories

Learning Objectives:

* Learn to organize memories using a five-sentence structure.

* Learn basic time conjunctions to organize the sequence of events.

Writing Task:

* Complete a memory segment using a “sequence + feeling” structure.

* Draft the first music memory short essay (60–80 words).

Instructor Prompts:

1. “Can you recall the first time you heard this song? Where were you? What happened?”

2. “What time conjunctions can we use to connect these events? For example, ‘then,’ ‘next,’ ‘finally,’ etc.”

3. “How do your feelings change over time in your story?”

## Session 3 – Learning to Express My Feelings

Learning Objectives:

* Learn and apply 10 basic emotion words.

* Express inner emotions through sentence structures.

Writing Task:

* Add 2-3 emotional details to the first short essay.

* Complete the final version of Memoir #1 (100 words).
Instructor Prompts:

1. “What words can you use to describe how you feel when you listen to this song? For example, happy, sad, nostalgic, etc.”

2. “In your short essay, add a specific emotional detail that helps the reader feel your emotions.”

3. “What impact has this piece of music had on you? Can you share?”

## Session 4 – Making Language More Vivid

Learning Objectives:

* Learn to add sensory descriptions and similes.

* Strengthen the use of verbs and adjectives.

Writing Task:

* Replace and polish language.

* Add at least 1 simile and 1 sensory description.

Instructor Prompts:

1. “What adjectives can you use to describe the sound of the music? Is it soft, passionate, or harsh?”

2. “Imagine the environment when you listen to music. Can you describe it using your senses? What do you hear? What do you smell?”

3. “Use a simile to enhance your description, such as ‘This song is as warm as sunshine.’”

## Session 5 – Independent Writing of the Second Piece

Learning Objectives:

* Reapply writing tools in a new topic.

* Develop independent writing skills.

Writing Task:

* Choose a music memory topic and write freely.
* Draft and revise the second 100-word Memoir.

**Instructor Prompts:**

1. “Choose a song that has special meaning to you. Why is it important?”

2. “During the writing process, use the structures and emotional vocabulary you learned earlier to ensure your story is organized and vivid.”

3. “What do you want to express most in this short essay? What should your readers feel?”

## Session 6 – Reflection and Polishing

**Learning Objectives:**

* Reflect on growth in expression and writing.

* Complete two final music memoirs.

**Writing Task:**

* Write a short reflection on your learning.

* Submit Memoir #1 + Memoir #2 + Title.

**Instructor Prompts:**

1. “Looking back at your two short essays, where do you think you have improved?”

2. “Did you encounter any difficulties during the writing process? How did you overcome them?”

3. “Which essay do you like the most? Why? What part of it are you most satisfied with?”

# Level 2 – Advanced Narrators (TOEFL ~80)

**Learning Objectives:**
* Explore how music evokes memories and emotions.
* Understand the structure of reflective personal stories.
* Practice using metaphors/similes to describe emotional responses.

Choose a meaningful song and write a memory related to it using rhetorical language (3-5 sentences).

Instructor Prompts:

2. “Try to use a metaphor or simile to describe how you feel when you hear this song, such as ‘This song is like a warm ray of sunshine.’”

## Session 2 – Layering the Senses in Writing

Learning Objectives:

* Learn to integrate the five senses into narrative writing.
* Identify where to add sensory language in drafts.

Writing Task:

* Rewrite the memory segment, adding auditory, visual, olfactory, and other sensory details.

Instructor Prompts:

1. “Recall the environment you were in. What sounds could you hear? What colors did you see?”

2. “When rewriting, try to include some smells you might have noticed, like the aroma of food or flowers.”

3. “What details in your story can be enhanced through sensory descriptions? For example, describing the atmosphere of a scene.”
# Session 3 – Dialogue and Inner Voices

*   Add inner monologues to deepen personal reflection.

# Session 4 – Engaging Beginnings and Clear Endings

# Session 5 – Creating the Second Memoir and Voice Practice

# Level 3 – Advanced Expressers (TOEFL ~100)
# Session 1 – Theme: Music and Identity

Learning Objectives:

* Analyze how music reflects personal or cultural identity.
* Begin to identify themes in personal experiences.

**Writing Task:**

Choose a song that has influenced your sense of identity and write a memory related to it (about 100 words).

Instrutor Prompts:

1. “How has this song impacted your sense of identity? Can you elaborate?”

2. “What emotions or experiences are connected to this song in your memory?”

3. “How does the lyrics or melody of this song express your cultural background?”

# Session 2 – Structural Experimentation

Learning Objectives:

* Learn examples of non-traditional memoir structures (e.g., letters, playlists, dual timelines).
* Choose a structure to begin drafting.

**Writing Task:**

Select a form and start creating, such as “a letter to your younger self” or “the playlist of my life.”

Instrutor Prompts:

1. “How does the form you chose help you better express the theme? Why?”

2. “In the letter to your younger self, what important life experiences do you want to convey?”

3. “What special significance does each song in your playlist hold for your life?”
# Session 3 – Revision Workshop I: Tone and Impact

Learning Objectives:

* Learn to revise tone and authenticity.
* Peer review style and clarity with others.

Writing Task:
Exchange drafts with peers/teachers and provide feedback on emotion and tone.

Instrutor Prompts:
1. “Does the tone in your draft align with the theme? How can you adjust it to enhance the effect?”
2. “Provide specific feedback on your peer’s work, pointing out where emotion is well expressed and where it needs improvement.”
3. “What did you learn from your peer’s feedback? How will you apply those suggestions to your own work?”

# Session 4 – Revision Workshop II: Conciseness and Polishing

Learning Objectives:

* Strengthen language conciseness and rhythm.
* Prepare the final memoir draft of 100-150 words.

Writing Task:
Use a checklist to refine your draft to within 150 words.

Instrutor Prompts:
1. “During the polishing process, did you find redundant parts? How can you streamline the language?”
2. “How is the rhythm of your story? Are there areas that need adjustment to enhance narrative flow?”
3. “In the final draft, which words or sentences best convey your emotions?”
# Session 5 – Audio and Multimedia Integration

Learning Objectives:

* Learn basic audio editing techniques (using Audacity or mobile apps).
* Experiment with integrating music, sound effects, and visual synchronization.

Writing Task:
Record and edit an audio version of your memoir, adding background music.

Instrutor Prompts:

1. “In audio editing, how do you want to use music and sound effects to enhance the emotional impact of your story?”

2. “Which sound effects best represent your story’s theme? How do you choose appropriate background music?”

3. “When recording, how can you adjust your tone and pace to ensure the audience’s attention?”

# Session 6 – Presentation and Final Reflection

Learning Objectives:

* Present and reflect on the creative process and growth.
* Submit the multimedia memoir project.

Writing Task:
Submit the final draft and write a paragraph reflecting on the creative process in English.

Instrutor Prompts:

1. “When presenting your work, what key moments in the creative process do you want to highlight?”

2. “Reflecting on the entire creative process, what do you consider your greatest takeaway?”

3. “In your reflection, how do you summarize your growth and suggest areas for future improvement?”
