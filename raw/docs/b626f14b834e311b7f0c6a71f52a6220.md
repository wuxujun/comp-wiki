# 《真实故事的呈现 - 线上纪实剧创作营》

*Real Voices On Stage: A Remote Documentary Theatre Lab*

**AA Instructor 指导老师:**

Sara Wu

**Original PBL topic 原始题目:**

新闻演绎剧场 | Documentary Theatre Lab

**Project Summary 项目简介:**

In this remote-friendly theatre project, students become “story collectors.” They will choose a social or school-based topic (such as social pressure or family routines), interview people around them, and turn these real voices into a mini documentary-style script. They’ll learn to turn real speech into stage-ready dialogue, explore multiple perspectives, and collaboratively edit a group scene for online performance or reading.

本项目专为远程线上学习设计，学生将化身“故事采集者”，围绕一个与校园或青少年生活相关的议题进行采访，并将访谈内容整理为一段群像式英文戏剧脚本。他们将学习如何将真实语言转化为舞台表达，呈现多种声音与观点，并以线上朗读或演绎形式进行展示。

**Parent Introduction 家长说明:**

This project helps students build empathy, active listening, and real-world English writing skills. By transforming interviews into dramatic scripts, students learn how to express personal and social voices in powerful, meaningful ways—perfect for remote learning and online sharing.

该项目通过“访谈+改编+表演”形式，帮助孩子练习倾听、表达与英文写作技巧，同时增强社会意识与多角度理解力。全部任务可线上完成，适合 5–8 年级学生在家远程参与。

**Target Age Grade 适合年级:**

Grades 5–10

5–10 年级

**Subjects Integrated 跨学科整合:**

Social Studies + Interview & Writing Skills + Theatre + Public Speaking

社会议题研究 + 英语采访与写作 + 剧本创作 + 英语表达训练

**Final Products 项目成果:**
* One short ensemble scene adapted from real interviews
* Online video reading or voice performance
* 一段改编自真实访谈的英文戏剧剧本
* 一次朗读或线上演绎展示

**English Learning Outcomes 英语学习关联:**

* Practice listening, note-taking, and question formation in English
* Learn how to adapt real language into stage-ready English dialogue
* Develop empathy-based speaking and writing
* 练习英文倾听与访谈技巧，训练提问与信息整合能力
* 学习将真实话语转化为戏剧剧本形式的文字，并通过朗读和表演的形式表现英文台词
* 培养基于情境的英文表达与写作能力

**English Deliverables 英文产出:**

* Script
* Performance reading video or audio
* 英文剧本
* 戏剧演绎片段（视频或音频可选）
