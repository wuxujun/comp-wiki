# 太阳神传说：跨文化神话探秘之旅
Sun Gods Across Cultures: A Mythology & Storytelling Adventure

Illustration of sun gods Apollo, Ra, and Xihe

## 项目简介 Project Summary

该项目为全程线上 1 对 1 形式，学生将踏上一段跨文化的神话探索之旅，了解世界各大文明如何通过神话塑造和崇拜太阳。在互动式故事讲述、视觉化资料和创意活动的引导下，学生将认识三位传奇太阳神：希腊的阿波罗、古埃及的拉以及中国的羲和。课程将帮助学生掌握神话相关词汇、描述与比较句型，并提升叙事写作能力，同时加深跨文化认知。学生不会被动阅读神话，而是积极创作：绘制“太阳神身份证”卡片，概述神祇的形象、起源与能力；用简洁的英文撰写神话故事摘要；制作对比图表，分析不同文化的价值观异同。

In this fully online 1-on-1 project, students will embark on a cross-cultural journey to uncover how civilizations across the world imagined and celebrated the Sun through mythology. Guided by interactive storytelling, visual resources, and creative tasks, they will explore three legendary figures: Apollo from Greek mythology, Ra from ancient Egypt, and Xihe from Chinese legend. Students will master myth-related vocabulary, descriptive and comparative sentence structures, and develop narrative writing skills, while also deepening their cultural awareness.

Instead of passively reading myths, students will actively create: designing "Sun God ID Cards" to summarize each deity's appearance, origin, and powers; crafting concise English summaries of each myth; and building comparison charts to analyze similarities and differences in cultural values.

## <mark>A Message to Parents</mark>

本项目为学生提供一次将文学、历史、艺术与英语学习相结合的独特体验。通过神话探索，他们不仅会扩充词汇、提升写作能力，还会培养跨文化比较、分析性思维与创意表达的能力。研究、设计和展示的过程将强化学生有逻辑地组织信息与清晰表达观点的能力。最终的数字成果将成为其学术成长与艺术创造力的有形记录。

This project offers a rare opportunity for students to combine literature, history, art, and English language learning in a single, engaging experience. Through myth exploration, they will not only expand vocabulary and improve writing but also develop skills in cultural comparison, analytical thinking, and creative expression. The process of researching, designing, and presenting will strengthen their ability to structure information logically and communicate ideas clearly. The final digital products will serve as a tangible record of their academic growth and artistic creativity.

**"Cultural Reflection Essay"**
