# 领袖思维训练营：从乔布斯到马斯克
Future Leaders' Thinking Camp: From Jobs to Musk

<mark>6课时(CT) + 1课时(NCT)</mark>

Illustration of Steve Jobs and Elon Musk

## 项目简介 Project Summary

本项目通过阅读乔布斯、马斯克等创新领袖的英文传记，帮助学生掌握信息提炼、逻辑思维和创造性表达能力。课程将带领学生分析创新者的生平和关键决策，制作创新者档案和思维导图，最终完成对比分析报告，提升阅读理解、批判性思维和英文写作水平。

This project guides students to explore the lives of innovators such as Steve Jobs and Elon Musk through English biographies. Students will develop skills in information extraction, logical thinking, and creative expression. The course includes creating an Innovator Profile, designing a Mind Map, and writing a Comparative Analysis Report, building competencies in reading comprehension, critical thinking, and academic writing.

## <mark>A Message to Parents</mark>

该课程不仅帮助学生提高英文阅读与写作，还培养他们逻辑思维、信息整合和批判性分析，这是国际课程和海外院校高度重视的核心素养。最终成果可作为学生的学术作品集，为未来背景提升和申请加分。

This course enhances students' English reading and writing while developing essential skills such as logical thinking, information synthesis, and critical analysis—competencies highly valued by international curricula and overseas universities. The final products can become part of a student's academic portfolio, strengthening future applications.

## 课程分级体系

**Level 1**  TOEFL 60

基础英文传记+关键词+Mind Map，适合英语起步但有留学计划的学生

**Level 2**  TOEFL 80

阅读400词传记，写结构化英文段落，掌握逻辑表达

**Level 3**  TOEFL 100

阅读原版片段，完成200-250词批判性分析，输出高质量作品集
