# 041026 PBL Topic - Personal

# Storytelling from Sara

**Personal Storytelling**

**AA Instructor:**

Sara McCartney

**Project Summary:**

Students learn to craft, outline, and perform outloud a personal narrative story about a first-hand experience of personal growth.

**Parent Introduction:**

This project introduces students to the foundations of narrative structure and practice their written and verbal English, while reflecting on a memorable experiennce in their life.

**Target Age Grade:**

Grades 6-10

**Subjects Integrated:**

Creative Writing + Personal Narrative + Speaking and Performance Skils

**English Learning Outcomes:**

Improve spoken English and presentation skills, structure and craft a story, construct personal reflections
