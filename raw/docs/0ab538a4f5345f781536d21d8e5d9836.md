# Level 1: Hooking the Audience with Platform-Specific Storytelling

第一阶梯：短视频平台上的故事开端（TOEFL 60）

**Format:** 线上一对一 Online 1-on-1

**Theme:** Learn how to tell platform-optimized stories using hooks, structure, and pacing

---

## Tier Goal 阶段目标

Students explore how cinematic storytelling techniques are reshaped for short-form video platforms like TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels. Through analyzing viral content and platform styles, they develop and write a 3-episode mini-series with clear narrative goals and emotional appeal.

学生将学习如何将电影叙事技巧转化为短视频平台上的表达形式。通过分析热门视频与平台风格，构思并撰写一个三集短视频系列，突出故事目标与情绪感染力。

---

## 6-Class Breakdown – 线上 1 对 1 课程结构（6 节课）

---

### Session 1: What Makes a Story Work?

第一课：爆款故事有什么魔力？

**Learning Goal 学习目标：**

Analyze how short videos hook viewers through structure, pacing, and emotional appeal

分析短视频如何通过结构、节奏与情绪吸引观众 **Tasks 任务内容：**

* Watch 2–3 viral short videos with the teacher (from different platforms)

* 与教师一起观看来自不同平台的爆款短视频 Break down: visual hook, story rhythm, ending structure

* 拆解视频的视觉吸引点、叙事节奏与结尾设计 Fill out “Story Hook & Structure” worksheet

完成“故事吸引力与结构分析表”

---

### Session 2: Platform Aesthetics & Story Shapes

第二课：不同平台，不同的“故事脸”

**Learning Goal 学习目标：**

Understand how different platforms shape content tone, pacing, and framing

理解不同平台如何影响内容风格、节奏和画面构图 **Tasks 任务内容：**

* Compare TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Reels: vertical framing, music use, tone

* 比较 TikTok、YouTube Shorts、Instagram Reels 的画面风格、配乐、整体语气
Review and annotate a short web series: what changes across episodes?

* 分析一个短视频系列的多集结构：每集如何变化？Choose one platform for your own series

选择一个作为创作目标的平台

# Session 3: Design Your Digital Series

第三课：设计你自己的短视频系列

## Learning Goal 学习目标：

Design a 3-episode mini-series based on platform norms and personal theme

基于平台规范与个人兴趣，设计一个三集短视频系列 **Tasks 任务内容：**

* Complete the “Mini-Series Concept Sheet” (title, theme, hook, platform, audience)

* 完成“短视频系列创意表”：标题、主题、吸引点、发布平台、目标受众 Outline each episode’s hook + ending in 1 sentence

* 用一句话概述每集的“吸引点+结尾”Discuss tone, format (vlog, dramatized, narrated, montage)

讨论整体风格：Vlog、剧情、旁白、蒙太奇等形式

# Session 4: Script It Short, Script It Sharp

第四课：短剧本，大内容

## Learning Goal 学习目标：

Write 3 concise, engaging video scripts (30–60 seconds) with visual thinking

撰写 3 个简洁、吸引人的短视频剧本，注重视觉呈现 **Tasks 任务内容：**

* Write 6–8 line English scripts for each episode

* 为每集撰写 6–8 句英文台词或旁白 Add shot suggestions or text overlay notes per line

* 每句标注镜头建议或文字特效提示 Use the “Script + Shot Card” template to format content

使用“剧本+画面提示卡”模板组织剧本

# Session 5: Performance and Voiceover Practice

第五课：表演语调+试录音

## Learning Goal 学习目标：

Build expressive voice skills for narration or on-screen dialogue

提升口语表现力，用于旁白或镜头内台词 **Tasks 任务内容：**

* Read all 3 scripts aloud with emotional variation and pacing

* 有语调地朗读三个脚本，注意节奏与情绪变化 Teacher gives feedback on pauses, emphasis, tone

* 教师提供关于停顿、重音、语气的反馈 Record draft audio for one episode (optional video if student is ready)

录制其中一集的试配音（可选视频演练）
# Session 6: Final Script + Creator Reflection
第六课：剧本定稿+创作者反思

## Learning Goal 学习目标：

Finalize scripts and reflect on how storytelling matches platform + audience
完善剧本并用英文反思：自己的故事如何与平台风格和观众匹配 **Tasks 任务内容：**

* Revise all 3 scripts for clarity, tone, and hook strength
* 修改三个剧本，提升清晰度、情绪表达与吸引力 **Write 3–5 sentence creator statement in English:**

“I chose this format because... / I want the audience to...”
* 用英文撰写 3–5 句创作者声明：“我选择这种形式是因为… / 我希望观众…”(Optional)

Plan post-course recording or editing schedule
（可选）讨论课后视频制作时间表

---

## Final Products 项目成果 (Level 1)

✔️ “Mini-Series Concept Sheet” 英文创意大纲一份

✔️ 3 Draft Scripts with Visual Notes 三个剧本草稿（含画面提示）

✔️ 1 Optional Voice Recording or Test Video 一段试录音或视频草稿

✔️ English Creator Statement 英文创作者反思短文（3–5 句）



# Level 2: Voice, Aesthetic, and Impact

第二阶梯：风格设计、语调掌握与观众影响（TOEFL 80）
**Format:** 线上一对一 Online 1-on-1
**Theme:** Deepen your creative voice and shape how stories *feel* on screen

---

## Tier Goal 阶段目标

Students refine their digital storytelling by revising scripts, building performance tone, and designing visual rhythm for short-form platforms. They deepen their understanding of editing choices, platform aesthetics, and emotional pacing while preparing at least one fully edited episode.

学生将进一步打磨脚本、调整表现语气，并学习为短视频平台设计节奏与画面风格。他们将在理解平台美学与观众心理的基础上，完成至少一集精剪视频。

---

## 6-Class Breakdown – 线上 1 对 1 课程结构（6 节课）

---
# Session 1: How Platform Shapes Feeling

第一课：平台风格如何影响故事感受

**Learning Goal 学习目标：**

Understand how editing, transitions, and layout influence tone and pacing
理解剪辑节奏、转场与布局如何塑造内容的语气与观感 **Tasks 任务内容：**

* Watch 3 platform-native videos: analyze cuts, rhythm, overlay, sound

* 观看 3 个不同平台的原生短视频，分析剪辑、节奏、特效与配音 Use “Aesthetic Audit” form to describe how the story *feels*

* 使用“风格观察表”描述每段视频带来的感受 Identify 1–2 techniques to use in your own work

选出 1–2 个想借用的风格元素

---

# Session 2: Rewrite with Rhythm

第二课：节奏为王——重写脚本

**Learning Goal 学习目标：**

Revise scripts to enhance pacing, emotional flow, and platform fit
调整剧本，使节奏更流畅、情绪更清晰、风格更贴近平台需求 **Tasks 任务内容：**

* Review and revise 3 scripts from Tier 1 with rhythm cues

* 回顾并修改第一阶段的剧本，加上节奏提示（快/慢/停顿）Teacher introduces “Jump Cut Rhythm” and “Breath Beat” techniques

* 教师介绍“跳剪节奏”与“呼吸节奏”两种常用剪辑感知方式 Apply edits: trim words, rewrite lines, test pacing aloud

应用技巧进行语言精简、节奏调整和口头演练

---

# Session 3: Voiceover, Text, or Talking Head?

第三课：旁白、字幕还是出镜？

**Learning Goal 学习目标：**

Choose the best delivery style for your story and practice performing it
根据故事内容选择最佳表达方式，并进行语音或视频表演练习 **Tasks 任务内容：**

* Explore 3 delivery formats: voiceover narration, text-on-screen, and speaking to camera

* 了解三种表达方式：配音、屏幕文字、镜头前发言 Decide episode-by-episode delivery format

* 为三集内容分别选择表达形式 Practice reading 1 episode script in selected format
练习一集剧本的实际朗读/出镜/旁白

---

# Session 4: Visual Design Lab

第四课：画面语言设计实验

**Learning Goal 学习目标：**

Plan visual elements: shot composition, text overlay, filters, transitions

设计镜头语言、文字提示、滤镜风格与转场逻辑 **Tasks 任务内容：**
* Fill out "Visual Design Worksheet" with shot ideas per script line

* 使用“画面设计表”填写每句话的画面构想 Discuss lighting, framing, and mobile setup with teacher

* 与教师探讨光线、构图与拍摄设备布置 Optional: select music or sound for one scene 可选任务：为一集选择背景音乐或音效

# Session 5: Draft + Test Feedback

第五课：视频草稿制作+反馈测试

**Learning Goal 学习目标：**

Create and share one rough-cut video to receive feedback on clarity and impact 制作一集粗剪视频并接受关于表达清晰度和感染力的反馈 **Tasks 任务内容：**

* Student presents one full episode (first rough cut)

* 学生展示一集完整粗剪版本 Teacher gives targeted feedback on visual pacing, tone, and hook

* 教师提供有关节奏控制、语气设定与开头吸引力的反馈建议 Student notes edits to apply for final version

学生记录修改方向，为后续精剪准备

# Session 6: Reflect Like a Creator

第六课：创作者反思与风格总结

**Learning Goal 学习目标：**

Reflect on how personal style and platform choice shape audience experience 反思创作者风格与平台选择如何共同影响观众体验 **Tasks 任务内容：**

* Write a 100–120 word English creator statement: "This series works best on      because..." / "I wanted the viewer to feel..."

* 撰写英文创作者陈述（100–120 词）：“我选择    平台的原因是… / 我希望观众…”Choose final aesthetic keywords for the series (e.g., “moody,” “upbeat,” “cinematic”)

* 为系列确定 3 个“风格关键词”（如：情绪化、轻快、电影感）Submit final version of 1 episode with edited script + visual plan

提交最终版视频（至少 1 集），附带定稿剧本与画面设计说明

# Final Products 项目成果 (Tier 2)

✔️ One revised script set with pacing & delivery notes

一份带节奏与表演提示的剧本集

✔️ One fully edited video (30–60 sec)

一段精剪短视频（30–60 秒）

✔️ Visual Design Worksheet with platform-specific annotations

一份详细的画面设计表

✔️ English Creator Statement (100–120 words)

一篇英文创作者反思陈述（100–120 词）
### Level 3: Media Creator as Artist and Analyst

第三阶梯：作为创作者与分析者的你（TOEFL 100）
**Format:** 线上一对一 Online 1-on-1
**Theme:** Make artistic, ethical, and emotional choices that define your creative voice

---

### Tier Goal 阶段目标

Students refine their complete digital mini-series with deliberate attention to tone, symbolism, audience psychology, and cultural relevance. They learn to analyze their own content like a filmmaker and reflect critically on how short-form media shapes both message and meaning.

学生将在完成短视频系列的基础上，进一步打磨语言风格、象征表达与文化定位，分析自己的创作逻辑与受众心理，同时思考短视频作为媒介如何影响信息传递与情感表达。

---

### 6-Class Breakdown – 线上 1 对 1 课程结构（6 节课）

---

### Session 1: What’s the Story *Underneath* the Story?

第一课：表象之下的故事核心

**Learning Goal 学习目标：**

Identify the emotional core and underlying themes across episodes

找出每一集背后的主题内核与情绪线索 **Tasks 任务内容：**

* Watch and reflect on an acclaimed short-form digital series

* 观看一个优秀的短视频系列并进行分析 Teacher prompts: “What connects each episode beyond plot?”

* 教师引导思考：“除了情节，每集之间还有什么共鸣？”Map your own emotional arc across 3 episodes

用“情绪曲线图”标记自己系列的整体情绪变化

---

### Session 2: Rebuild the Series Arc

第二课：重构叙事结构

**Learning Goal 学习目标：**

Edit the sequence of your series to build theme, contrast, and tone progression

调整系列顺序与结构以强化主题表达与风格递进 **Tasks 任务内容：**

* Reevaluate episode order: should one come first or last for impact?

* 重新审视三集的排列顺序：哪一集最能抓住观众？哪一集适合结尾？Add or revise opening/closing lines for stronger thematic unity

* 修改开头或结尾语句，使系列更具整体性 Teacher helps brainstorm transitions or
framing devices (e.g., recurring symbols)
教师协助构思集与集之间的转场或视觉符号衔接方式

---

## Session 3: Language & Symbolism Upgrade

第三课：语言与象征的升级表达

**Learning Goal 学习目标：**

Enhance word choice, visual cues, and symbols to deepen meaning
优化语言与象征细节，丰富内容层次与文化指向 **Tasks 任务内容：**

* Identify 2–3 key words or images that reappear or connect scenes
找出 2–3 个能贯穿系列的关键词或象征元素 Use metaphor or contrast in at least one script revision

* 至少为一集剧本加入比喻或对比手法 Create a “motif map” to track recurring phrases, shots, or ideas
制作“元素回环图”追踪重复的短语、镜头或主题

---

## Session 4: Viewer Psychology & Media Ethics

第四课：观众心理与媒介伦理

**Learning Goal 学习目标：**

Reflect on how algorithms, trends, and platform rules affect content choices
理解算法机制、流行趋势与平台规范如何影响创作决定 **Tasks 任务内容：**

* Discuss: How would this story change on another platform or in a longer format?
讨论：若换个平台或变成长篇，这个故事会怎么变化？Analyze a viral trend that uses misleading edits or emotional manipulation

* 分析一个存在误导性剪辑或情绪操控的流行视频 Write a 5-sentence commentary: “As a creator, I believe...”
用英文撰写 5 句评论：“作为创作者，我认为...”

---

## Session 5: Final Episode Polish

第五课：最终集打磨与技术检查

**Learning Goal 学习目标：**

Apply advanced polish to visuals, transitions, and pacing in final cut
在最终成片中完成画面、节奏与过渡的高级润色 **Tasks 任务内容：**

* Review each second of one episode together and annotate pacing/timing

* 与教师逐秒回看一集视频，标注节奏与时间线调整 Make final edits based on feedback (visual rhythm, text timing, etc.)

* 根据反馈做最终修改（节奏控制、字幕时机等）Add a thumbnail, title, or outro slide for platform upload readiness
添加平台适配的封面图、标题与结束页

---

## Session 6: Showcase + Creator Talk

第六课：最终展示与创作者分享

**Learning Goal 学习目标：**
Present final series with a reflective “creator talk” in English

用英文呈现完整系列并发表创作者说明 **Tasks 任务内容：**

* Deliver a 2-minute English creator talk:
“Here’s what I made, and here’s why I made it this way.”

* 完成 2 分钟英文创作者演讲：“我做了什么，为什么这样做。”Present full 3-episode series to teacher for final feedback

* 向教师展示完整三集系列并获得最终反馈 Optional: post online (private link) with description and tags

可选：将作品上传至平台并设置为私密链接，附带英文简介与关键词标签

修改完善的剧本，附主题说明与象征线索

英文创作者说明（书面+口头，约 2 分钟）
