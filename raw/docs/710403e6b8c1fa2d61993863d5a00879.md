# Session 1 – Poetry Themes & Imagery 诗歌主题与意象

## Learning Goals 学习目标

* Understand what constitutes a poetic theme and how imagery brings themes to life

* Learn to identify and analyze themes and corresponding imagery in classic poems

* Understand the basic structure and formal characteristics of poetry

* 理解诗歌主题的构成以及意象如何赋予主题生命力

* 学会识别和分析经典诗歌中的主题与对应意象

* 了解诗歌的基本结构与形式特征

## Student Task 学生任务

* Analyze themes and imagery in sample poems from classic and contemporary works

* Discuss how different poets use imagery to express similar themes

* 分析范例诗歌中的主题与意象运用

* 讨论不同诗人如何通过意象表达相似主题

## Homework after Session 1：

Collect 3-5 poems that resonate with you and identify their central themes and key imagery.

# Session 2 – Brainstorming Theme & Outline 脑暴主题与大纲

## Learning Goals 学习目标

* Learn brainstorming techniques for discovering personal and meaningful poem themes

* Develop skills in generating relevant imagery, expressions, and narrative threads

* Create a preliminary outline for your original poem

* 学习脑暴技巧，发现个人化且有意义的诗歌主题

* 培养构思相关意象、表达方式和叙事线索的能力

* 形成自己作品的初步大纲

## Student Task 学生任务

* Brainstorm potential themes based on personal experiences, observations, or emotions

* Generate imagery banks and word associations for chosen themes

* Draft a working outline with theme, central images, and possible structure

* 基于个人经历、观察或情感进行主题脑暴
* 为选定主题构建意象库和词汇联想
* 撰写包含主题、核心意象和可能结构的工作大纲

**Homework after Session 2 :**

Finalize your poem theme and detailed outline, including key images and a rough narrative or emotional arc.

# Session 3 & 4 - Poetry Techniques 诗歌技法讲解

## Learning Goals 学习目标

* Master common literary devices in poetry: metaphor, simile, personification, symbolism

* Understand sound devices: rhyme, rhythm, alliteration, assonance, consonance

* Learn how to select and apply techniques that align with your creative vision

* Practice techniques through in-class writing exercises

* 掌握诗歌中常见的文学修辞手法：隐喻、明喻、拟人、象征

* 理解音韵技巧：押韵、节奏、头韵、半谐音、谐音

* 学会选择与创作思路相契合的技法并加以精讲

* 通过随堂写作练习掌握技法运用

## Student Task 学生任务

* Analyze how classic poets employ specific literary and sound devices

* Experiment with different techniques in short writing exercises

* Select and apply appropriate techniques to your poem draft

* 分析经典诗人如何运用文学与音韵技巧

* 在短篇练习中尝试不同技法

* 为自己的诗歌选择并应用合适的技法

**Homework after Session 3 & 4 :**

Write the first draft of your poem, incorporating at least 3-4 literary devices and 2-3 sound techniques learned in class.

# Session 5 & 6 - Poetry Revision & Refinement 诗歌修改与优化

## Learning Goals 学习目标

* Learn systematic approaches to revising and refining poetry
* Understand how to strengthen imagery, tighten language, and enhance emotional impact

* Master techniques for improving rhythm, line breaks, and stanza structure

* Learn to give and receive constructive feedback on poetic works

* 学习修改与优化诗歌的系统方法

* 掌握加强意象、精简语言、增强情感冲击力的技巧

* 优化节奏、断句和节的结构

* 学会对诗歌作品给出建设性反馈

**Workshop Focus 常见问题聚焦**

* Imagery that feels generic or underdeveloped

* Inconsistent or ineffective use of sound devices

* Weak line breaks that disrupt flow or meaning

* Lack of emotional depth or thematic clarity

* 意象过于通俗或发展不足

* 音韵技巧使用不一致或效果不佳

* 断句不当影响节奏或意义

* 情感浅薄或主题不够清晰

**Student Task 学生任务**

* Revise key sections of your poem based on instructor feedback

* Strengthen imagery and refine language choices

* Polish rhythm, line breaks, and overall structure

* Reflect on the revision process and identify major improvements

* 根据老师反馈修改作品关键部分

* 加强意象表现并精细语言选择

* 优化节奏、断句和整体结构

* 反思修改过程，识别最大的写作提升点

**Homework after Session 5 & 6 :**

Complete the final revision of your poem, incorporating all feedback and refinements discussed in class.

**Final Outcome 课程最终成果**

* One polished original poem demonstrating mastery of theme, imagery, and poetic techniques
* A visible progression from initial concept to refined artistic expression
* Understanding of poetic craft that enables continued self-directed writing
* 一首完整的原创诗歌，展现对主题、意象和诗歌技法的掌握
* 从初始构想到精炼艺术表达的明显进步
* 对诗歌创作技巧的理解，能够继续进行独立创作

**NCT 作品反馈：**

After submitting your draft, the instructor will provide detailed written feedback on your poem, highlighting strengths and areas for further development. This feedback will be discussed in subsequent sessions to guide your revision process.

学生提交初稿后，老师将提供详细的书面反馈，突出优点和需进一步发展的方面。这些反馈将在后续课程中讨论，以指导修改过程。
