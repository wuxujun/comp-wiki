# 我眼中的小人物：记录被历史忽视的声音

## Future Leaders’ Thinking Camp: From Jobs to Musk

6课时(CT) + 1课时(NCT)

Illustration of diverse historical figures

## 凹 项目简介 Project Summary

在本项目的线上1对1课程中，学生将挖掘被历史忽视或边缘化的群体人物——例如女性、工人、移民或被殖民地区的人物。学生将选择一位“小人物”，研究其一生、贡献，以及为何未被主流历史充分记载。通过资料研究、同理写作和创意演绎，学生将创作一篇微型人物传记，并通过角色独白或情景演绎将其呈现出来。项目旨在培养学生的批判性思维、历史同理心及英文表达能力。

In this fully online 1-on-1 project, students will uncover the stories of overlooked or marginalized historical figures—such as women, laborers, immigrants, or people from colonized regions. Students will choose one such figure and explore their life, contributions, and the reasons they were left out of mainstream history. Through research, empathy-driven writing, and creative performance, students will craft a short biographical narrative and present it through a character monologue or scene performance. This project encourages critical thinking, historical empathy, and the development of both written and oral communication skills.

<mark>A Message to Parents</mark>

本项目鼓励学生从更包容和个体化的视角理解历史。通过关注鲜为人知的“小人物”，学生不仅培养了同理心，也更深入地理解了社会结构、不平等现象与历史书写的局限性。项目融合了学术研究与写作训练，同时通过角色扮演进行创造性表达，有助于提升学生的视角转换能力、沟通表达能力以及全球公民意识。

This project enables students to explore history from a more inclusive and personal angle. By focusing on lesser-known individuals, students develop empathy and a deeper understanding of social dynamics, inequality, and historical narrative. The project supports research and writing development while encouraging creative self-expression through performance. It is especially effective for building perspective-taking, communication skills, and global citizenship awareness in young learners.
