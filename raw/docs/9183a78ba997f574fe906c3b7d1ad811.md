About John Locke
John Locke 竞赛是由普林斯顿、牛津大学联合协办的、含金量超高的国际写作比赛之一，对申请英美名校心理学、神经科学或哲学等专业助力。John Locke共分7大学科领域，其中心理学是热门的参赛领域之一，适合对行为科学、认知研究或心理哲学有热情的学生，对学生的知识储备、学术调研能力、批判性写作能力都有极高要求。

I am delighted to announce today the release of the short list for the Psychology Category of the John Locke Institute 2022 Global Essay Competition. This year we received a record number of entries, of which 945 were in the Psychology category. The great majority of submissions were of a very high standard but only the top quintile was shortlisted.
Our first- and second-round examiners have now assessed and reassessed every essay, and have made a decision about which entries to shortlist for a prize. It is my very great pleasure to tell you that your essay was shortlisted. Congratulations!
I would like to invite you to come to Oxford for the awards ceremony and gala dinner at Wadham College on Saturday, 1 October, 2022, at which the winners and runners up will be announced.
If you come to Oxford for the prize-giving event I hope you will also join us for an invitation-only academic conference open only to shortlisted candidates. This event will begin with an informal dinner on the Friday evening, followed by a lecture by our director, Martin Cox. On the Saturday there will be a series of lectures and seminars led by academics from Oxford and Cambridge with special expertise in the topics of this year's essay question. On Sunday morning there will be an admissions briefing, which will be of particular interest to students hoping to study at Oxford or Cambridge.
