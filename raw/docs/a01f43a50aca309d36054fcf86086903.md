# 音乐学探索：世界音乐

## Discover in Music: Introduction to Musicology

9课时 / 9 sessions

Music notes illustration

## 课程概述 Overview

本课程将带领学生走进世界各地的音乐传统，探索其背后的文化故事与历史背景，通过音乐分析、跨文化研究、音频创作等实践，做出属于自己的第一份音乐项目。

This course introduces students to the global diversity of music traditions. Students will investigate the cultural and historical roots of music, analyze musical structures, and create their first personal music projects.

## 适合学员 Suitable for Students

* [x] 对音乐与文化感兴趣的学生
* [x] 未来计划申请音乐、人文、传媒专业的申请者
* [x] 渴望培养音乐听辨、跨文化理解与研究表达能力的学生

* Students interested in music and culture

* Aspiring applicants to music, humanities, or media programs

* Students eager to build skills in musical analysis, cross-cultural understanding, and research expression

## 课程模块 Syllabus

### 01 文化音乐探索与学习 Cultural Music Exploration and Study (4课时 / 4 lessons)

介绍不同地区的音乐类型，如韩国流行音乐、中国戏曲、拉丁美洲桑巴、中东音乐等，研究其历史背景、功能与发展，并学习基本的音乐分析方法。

Introduce music from different regions, such as Korean pop music, Chinese opera, Latin American samba, Middle Eastern music, etc. Students will examine the historical background, functions, and development of these styles, and learn basic methods of musical analysis.

### 02 个性化研究 Personalized Research (4课时 / 4 lessons)

学生根据兴趣选择研究地区，采用文献分析、采访和有可能的田野研究等研究方法，分析代表性音乐作品，探索该音乐的文化历史与发展趋势。

Students will choose a region of interest and conduct research using methods such as literature review, interviews, and possible fieldwork. They will analyze representative musical works and explore the cultural history and development trends of the chosen music.

### 03 成果展示与反馈 Presentation and Feedback of Achievements (1课时 / 1 lesson)

学生整理研究成果，以研究报告、多媒体展示的形式发表，增强实践能力，并通过展示与讨论进一步深化理解。
Students will organize their research findings and present them through research reports or multimedia presentations. This process enhances practical skills and deepens understanding through presentation and discussion.

## 课程亮点 Course Highlights

### 全球音乐鉴赏 Global Music Appreciation

* 探索中国古琴与文人精神、欧洲古典与交响逻辑、K-pop与全球流行文化等不同音乐风格

Explore a range of styles including Chinese guqin and scholar culture, European classical and symphonic logic, K-pop and global pop culture.

* 以音乐为切入点，结合乐谱、影像、文献展开深度研究
Use music as the entry point, integrating score, media, and texts to conduct interdisciplinary exploration.

### 学术与创意双轨并行 Blend of Academic and Creative Output

* 学习基础的音乐分析框架（旋律、节奏、织体、结构）和文化分析方法

Learn musical elements (melody, rhythm, texture, form) and frameworks for cultural analysis.

### Interdisciplinary icon 项目式跨学科研究 Interdisciplinary Project-Based Research

* 融合音乐学+人类学+媒体研究，建立从音乐出发的全球视野
Combine musicology + anthropology + media studies to build a global view through music.

* 结业成果可衍生为音乐论文、竞赛作品或创意项目集
Final projects can evolve into music research papers, competition submissions, or creative portfolios.
