# Beginner Course: Introduction to Film Analysis
# 初级课程：电影分析入门

## Lesson 1 (CT): Understanding Film as a Storytelling Medium

## 第 1 课（CT）：理解电影作为一种叙事媒介

**Course Content 课程内容：**

* Introduction to the fundamental elements of storytelling in film.

* 介绍电影叙事的基本要素。 Discussion on narrative structures such as three-act structure and character arcs.

* 讨论叙事结构，例如三幕式结构和人物成长弧线。 Activity: Identifying and mapping the story structure of a selected film.

课堂活动：识别并梳理一部选定电影的故事结构。

---

## Lesson 2 (CT): Cinematic Elements Overview

## 第 2 课（CT）：电影语言要素概览

**Course Content 课程内容：**

* Introduction to cinematography, sound, editing, and music in film.

* 介绍电影中的摄影、声音、剪辑和音乐。 Discussion on how these elements contribute to storytelling.

* 讨论这些要素如何服务于叙事。 Activity: Watching either a short film or a scene from a feature-length film and collaboratively mapping the defining cinematic elements.

课堂活动：观看一部短片或一部长片中的片段，并合作梳理其中关键的电影语言要素。

---

## Lesson 3 (CT): Elements of a Film Review

## 第 3 课（CT）：影评的基本构成

**Course Content 课程内容：**

* Overview of a film review’s key components: summary, analysis, and personal opinion.

* 概述影评的关键组成部分：剧情概述、分析与个人观点。 Examination of professional film reviews to identify strengths and weaknesses.

* 分析专业影评，识别其优点与不足。 Activity: Watch a short film and complete a film review worksheet to help students begin learning how to critically analyze films and structure film reviews.

课堂活动：观看一部短片并完成影评练习单，帮助学生开始学习如何批判性分析电影并组织影评结构。
# Lesson 4 (CT): Analyzing Perspectives in Film Criticism
# 第 4 课（CT）：分析影评中的不同视角

**Course Content 课程内容：**

* Understanding subjective vs. objective reviews.

* 理解主观影评与客观影评的区别。 Recognizing bias in professional film critiques.

* 识别专业影评中的偏见。 Activity: Comparing two contrasting reviews of the same film and identifying personal biases/preferences that result in different interpretations.

<u>课堂活动：比较同一部电影的两篇不同观点影评，并识别导致不同解读的个人偏好与偏见。</u>

---

# Lesson 5 (CT): Writing a Structured Film Review
# 第 5 课（CT）：撰写结构化影评

**Course Content 课程内容：**

* Step-by-step guide to constructing a complete film review.

* 分步骤学习如何完成一篇完整的影评。 Discussion of films chosen for final project; review of film review worksheets.

* 讨论期末项目选定的影片，并回顾影评练习单内容。 Project: Completing and finalizing a structured review of a feature-length film.

<u>项目任务：完成并定稿一篇针对长片的结构化影评。</u>

---

# Lesson 6 (CT): Summative Project
# 第 6 课（CT）：总结性项目

**Course Content 课程内容：**

* Presenting an in-depth review, incorporating feedback and critical analysis.

* 展示一篇深入影评，融合反馈意见与批判性分析。 Reflecting on progress and discussing key takeaways.

<u>回顾学习进展并讨论关键收获。</u>

---

# Lesson 7 (NCT): Final Project Review & Foundational Feedback
# 第 7 课（NCT）：期末项目审阅与基础反馈

**Course Content 课程内容：**

* Instructor conducts an independent review of the student’s final film review project.

* 教师独立审阅学生的期末影评项目。 Evaluation focuses on core competencies: structure (summary, analysis, opinion), clarity of argument, and use of film-specific evidence.

* 评估重点包括核心能力：结构（概述、分析、观点）、论点清晰度，以及电影文本证据的使用。 Written feedback provided to students highlighting strengths in organization and clarity.
* 向学生提供书面反馈，重点指出其在结构组织与表达清晰度方面的优点。 Areas for improvement may include over-reliance on summary, limited analysis, or lack of specific examples.

* 需要改进的方面可能包括：过度依赖剧情概述、分析不足，或缺少具体例子。 Instructor identifies 2–3 priority skill gaps based on student work.

* 教师根据学生作品识别出 2–3 项优先提升的技能短板。 Students receive a concise action plan outlining next steps for improving foundational film review writing skills. 学生将收到一份简明行动计划，明确后续如何提升基础影评写作能力。

# Intermediate Course: Exploring Film Genres & Themes

# 中级课程：探索电影类型与主题

## Lesson 1 (CT): Introduction to Film Genres

## 第 1 课（CT）：电影类型导论

**Course Content 课程内容：**

* Overview of major film genres and their defining characteristics.

* 概述主要电影类型及其定义特征。 Discussion on how different genres utilize unique storytelling techniques.

* 讨论不同类型如何运用独特的叙事方式。 Activity: Classifying movies based on genre and justifying selections.

课堂活动：根据类型对电影进行分类，并说明分类理由。

## Lesson 2 (CT): Genre Comparison & Review

## 第 2 课（CT）：类型比较与影评写作

**Course Content 课程内容：**

* Watching and analyzing two films of different genres.

* 观看并分析两部不同类型的电影。 Discussion on storytelling, character development, and cinematography.

* 讨论叙事方式、人物发展与摄影表现。 Project: Writing a genre comparison review focusing on style and themes.

项目任务：撰写一篇聚焦风格与主题的类型比较影评。

## Lesson 3 (CT): Films & Social Issues

## 第 3 课（CT）：电影与社会议题

**Course Content 课程内容：**

* Understanding how films reflect and challenge societal norms and issues.

* 理解电影如何反映并挑战社会规范与现实议题。 Identifying themes related to race, gender, politics, and class in cinema.
* 识别电影中与种族、性别、政治和阶级相关的主题。 Project: Writing a critical analysis on how a chosen film presents its social theme and its effectiveness in raising awareness.

项目任务：撰写批判性分析，探讨一部选定电影如何呈现其社会主题，以及其唤起公众意识的效果。

---

# Lesson 4 (CT): Ethics & Responsibility in Filmmaking

# 第 4 课（CT）：电影创作中的伦理与责任

**Course Content 课程内容：**

* Examining censorship, historical accuracy, and storytelling responsibility.

* 探讨审查制度、历史准确性与叙事责任。 Discussion on the role of filmmakers in shaping cultural narratives.

* 讨论电影创作者在塑造文化叙事中的作用。 Project: Writing an ethical critique of a selected film.

项目任务：为一部选定电影撰写伦理批评。

---

# Lesson 5 (CT): Global Perspectives in Film

# 第 5 课（CT）：电影中的全球视角

**Course Content 课程内容：**

* Examining films from different cultural backgrounds.

* 研究来自不同文化背景的电影。 Understanding how cultural contexts shape storytelling and audience reception.

* 理解文化语境如何影响叙事方式与观众接受。 Activity: Writing a short analysis comparing cultural representation in two films.

课堂活动：撰写一篇简短分析，比较两部电影中的文化呈现。

---

# Lesson 6 (CT): Review of Summative Project

# 第 6 课（CT）：总结性项目回顾

**Course Content 课程内容：**

* Reviewing final projects: portfolios containing all written analyses and self-reflection.

* 回顾期末项目：包含所有书面分析与自我反思的作品集。 Student presentations.

* 学生展示。 Discussions on platforms students can utilize to publish reviews.
讨论学生可以用于发布影评的平台。

---

# Lesson 7 (NCT): Analytical Depth Review & Critical Thinking Development

# 第 7 课（NCT）：分析深度审阅与批判性思维发展

**Course Content 课程内容：**

* Instructor conducts an independent review of the student's final portfolio (genre,
theme, and ethical analyses).

* 教师独立审阅学生的最终作品集（包括类型、主题与伦理分析）。 Evaluation focuses on depth of analysis, clarity of thematic interpretation, and effectiveness of comparative/critical reasoning.

* 评估重点包括分析深度、主题解读清晰度，以及比较与批判性推理的有效性。 Written feedback highlights strengths in argument development and thematic insight.

* 书面反馈将重点指出学生在论证展开与主题洞察方面的优势。 Areas for improvement may include surface-level analysis, insufficient comparison, or an unclear thesis.

* 需要改进的方面可能包括：分析停留于表面、比较不足，或中心论点不清。 Instructor assesses the student’s ability to move from description to interpretation and identifies gaps in critical thinking.

* 教师评估学生从描述走向解读的能力，并识别其批判性思维中的不足。 Students receive a targeted action plan for deepening analysis and strengthening argumentative writing.

学生将收到一份有针对性的行动计划，用于深化分析并加强论证性写作能力。

# Advanced Course: Multimedia Film Reviews & Publishing

# 高级课程：多媒体影评与发布

## Lesson 1 (CT): Exploring Multimedia Film Review Formats

## 第 1 课（CT）：探索多媒体影评形式

**Course Content 课程内容：**

* Understanding the differences between written and multimedia reviews.

* 理解文字影评与多媒体影评之间的差异。 Analyzing successful video reviews, podcasts, and blogs.

* 分析优秀的视频影评、播客和博客。 Activity: Identifying comparative strengths and weaknesses in different review formats.

<u>课堂活动：比较不同影评形式的优缺点。</u>

## Lesson 2 (CT): Writing for Multimedia Reviews

## 第 2 课（CT）：为多媒体影评写作

**Course Content 课程内容：**

* Structuring an engaging and persuasive script for video or podcast reviews.

* 为视频或播客影评构建有吸引力且有说服力的脚本。 Balancing critical analysis with audience engagement.

* 平衡批判性分析与观众吸引力。 Activity: Drafting a script for a multimedia review.
<u>课堂活动：起草一份多媒体影评脚本。</u>
## Lesson 3 (CT): Basics of Video & Audio Editing

## 第 3 课 (CT) : 视频与音频剪辑基础

**Course Content 课程内容：**

* Introduction to basic editing tools for video and podcasts.

* 介绍视频与播客常用的基础剪辑工具。 Hands-on practice with cutting, layering, and enhancing audio/visual elements.

* 动手练习剪切、叠加和优化音视频元素。 Activity: Editing a short sample review clip.

<u>课堂活动：剪辑一个简短的影评示例片段。</u>

---

## Lesson 4 (CT): Publishing & Presenting Reviews

## 第 4 课 (CT) : 发布与展示影评

**Course Content 课程内容：**

* Exploring different platforms for publishing multimedia reviews.

* 探索发布多媒体影评的不同平台。 Best practices for audience engagement and feedback.

* 学习吸引观众和获取反馈的最佳实践。 Activity: Recording first drafts for multimedia review final projects.

<u>课堂活动：录制多媒体影评期末项目的初稿。</u>

---

## Lesson 5 (CT): Creating a Multimedia Review

## 第 5 课 (CT) : 制作多媒体影评

**Course Content 课程内容：**

* Peer review of multimedia review final projects.

* 同伴互评多媒体影评期末项目。 Project: Producing and finalizing a multimedia review.

<u>项目任务：制作并完成一份多媒体影评。</u>

---

## Lesson 6 (CT): Summative Project

## 第 6 课 (CT) : 总结性项目

**Course Content 课程内容：**

* Final Project: Presenting polished multimedia film reviews.

* 期末项目：展示完善后的多媒体影评作品。 Review of key concepts and pathway for future review work.

<u>回顾核心概念，并梳理后续影评创作的发展路径。</u>
# Lesson 7 (NCT): Multimedia Project Evaluation & Publication Readiness
# 第 7 课（NCT）：多媒体项目评估与发布准备度

**Course Content 课程内容：**

* Instructor conducts an independent, asynchronous review of each student’s final multimedia film review project.

* 教师以异步方式独立审阅每位学生的最终多媒体影评项目。 Evaluation focuses on integration of content and form: quality of analysis, effectiveness of scripting, and execution of multimedia elements (video/audio/editing).

* 评估重点为内容与形式的整合：分析质量、脚本效果，以及多媒体元素（视频、音频、剪辑）的执行情况。 Written feedback highlights strengths in communication, creativity, and audience engagement.

* 书面反馈将重点指出学生在表达、创意与观众互动方面的优势。 Areas for improvement may include pacing, clarity of argument in the script, and technical execution.

* 需要改进的方面可能包括：节奏把控、脚本论点清晰度，以及技术执行。 Instructor assesses overall readiness for public-facing publication or portfolio inclusion.

* 教师评估作品是否已具备公开发布或纳入作品集的准备度。 Students receive a forward-looking action plan with suggested refinements and publishing next steps. 学生将收到一份面向未来的行动计划，包括优化建议与后续发布方向。

By the end of these courses, students will have developed a deeper appreciation for film, improved their critical thinking and writing skills, and gained experience in multimedia content creation.
完成这些课程后，学生将提升对电影的理解与欣赏能力，增强批判性思维与写作技能，并获得多媒体内容创作经验。
