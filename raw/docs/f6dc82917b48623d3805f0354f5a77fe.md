# 直播打游戏如何赚钱？流量变现机制解析
How do games make money? Exploring the streaming economy

<mark>6课时(CT) + 1课时(NCT)</mark>

Illustration of a person live-streaming a video game

## 项目简介 Project Summary

学生将通过分析游戏直播案例，深入探讨数字内容产业背后的商业逻辑。通过研究主播如何吸引观众、流量如何转化为收入，学生将学习成本分析、用户画像与商业变现的平衡。学生将运用数据分析与商业建模，完成一份个人“主播商业计划”，并模拟真实的商业路演。

Through case analysis of the streaming industry, students will explore the business logic behind digital content. Students will analyze revenue models, user engagement, and data-driven strategies to propose their own "Streamer Business Plan."

<mark>**A Message to Parents**</mark>

本项目旨在培养学生建立商业思维与现实世界问题的解决能力。通过沉浸式的行业分析，学生将提升以下能力：
* 理解数字产业盈利逻辑
* 分析流量与收入的转化关系
* 运用数据图表进行商业推理
* 用英语表达商业观点与策略
* 提升逻辑思维与演讲说服力

This project builds real-world financial awareness and critical thinking skills. Students will:

* Understand digital revenue streams

* Analyze the relationship between traffic and profit

* Use charts and data for business reasoning

* Communicate business ideas in English

* Develop structured thinking and presentation skills

## 适合学生 Target Students

* 难度将根据学生的英语水平进行调整

* 高年级学生将进行更深入的市场细分与盈利模型探讨

The difficulty level will be adjusted according to students' English proficiency.

Older students will engage in deeper market segmentation and economic modeling.

## 跨学科整合 Subjects Integrated

<mark>**经济学 | Economics**</mark>

直播经济、付费意愿、边际收益分析
Live-streaming economy, willingness to pay, marginal revenue analysis

<mark>**商业 | Business**</mark>

商业模式设计、目标观众分析、竞争策略
Business models, target group analysis, competitive strategy

<mark>**英语表达 | English**</mark>

行业词汇、商业计划书写作、英文路演演讲
Business vocabulary, business plan writing, English roadshow presentation

<mark>**数据可视化 | Data Visualization**</mark>

流量统计图、收入构成分析、增长预测
Traffic statistics chart, revenue composition analysis, growth forecast

## 项目成果 Final Deliverables

Streamer Business Plan icon **主播商业计划书 Streamer Business Plan**

学生将设计个人直播方案，涵盖内容定位、目标受众与运营策略。
Students will design individual live-streaming plans, covering content positioning, target audiences and operational strategies.

Revenue Model Dashboard icon **收入模型看板 Revenue Model Dashboard**

学生将可视化展示礼物打赏、广告赞助与周边销售的占比关系。
Students will visually display the proportion of gift rewards, advertising sponsorships and peripheral product sales.

Business Strategy Brief icon **英文商业简报 Business Strategy Brief**

完成一份正式的英文分析文档：“我的直播增长策略”。
Compile a formal English analysis document: My Live-Streaming Growth Strategy.

Business Pitch Presentation icon **英文商业Pitch演讲 Business Pitch Presentation**

进行 3-5 分钟的商业路演，展示计划并回答 Q&A。
Deliver a 3-5 minute business roadshow to present the plan and answer Q&A.
