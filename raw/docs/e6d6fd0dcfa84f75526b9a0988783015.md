# Level 1 (TOEFL 60)：读懂台词、模仿表达、用现代英文说人话

**关键词**：基础翻译 + 情绪识别 + 创意模仿

**Keywords**: Basic translation + Emotion recognition + Creative imitation

## 课程目标 English Goals：

* 理解莎士比亚角色基本信息

Understand basic facts about Shakespearean characters

* 掌握基础台词翻译和情绪识别

Learn to translate simple lines and identify emotions

* 尝试用现代英文重述并朗读角色语句

Try to paraphrase and read lines in modern English

## 最终成果 Final Product：

* 一段用现代英文改写的角色独白（6–8 句）

A short character monologue rewritten in modern English (6–8 lines)

* 一段音频或视频朗读（可选）

An optional audio or video recording of the reading

---

## Session 1 – Who Was Shakespeare? 了解莎士比亚

**学习目标**：了解莎士比亚和他的著名角色，用英文学习角色经典片段

**Learning Goal**: Understand Shakespeare and his famous characters; read short iconic lines in English

**具体任务：**

* 教师介绍莎士比亚的时代、代表作与文化影响；

The teacher introduces Shakespeare’s time, famous works, and cultural impact.

* 学生学习代表角色如 Hamlet、Macbeth 等，选择一个感兴趣的角色深入；

Students learn about key characters such as Hamlet and Macbeth, and choose one to explore.

* 使用“角色卡片模板”填写角色的姓名、出自哪部作品、角色身份描述；

Complete a character card template with name, play title, and basic description.

* 写下 3 个想了解该角色的问题（可中英混合），作为后续探究线索。

Write 3 questions about the character (in English or Chinese) for further investigation.

---

## Session 2 – Monologue Magic 台词魔法课：走入角色内心

**学习目标**：练习用关键词识别台词中的情绪

**Learning Goal**: Practice identifying emotions in short speeches using key words

**具体任务：**

* 教师提供节选台词（如 Juliet 的“What's in a name?”、Hamlet 的“To be or not to
be”），帮助学生理解句子结构；
The teacher provides famous quotes and helps students understand their meaning and structure.

* 引导学生精读剧本，每句标注对应情感，制作情绪卡；
Guide students in close reading and have them match each line with an emotion card.

* 课堂活动：将 3 句原文与 3 张情绪卡进行配对并朗读，用戏剧技巧表现人物
In-class activity: Match three original lines with emotion cards and read them aloud using dramatic expression.

## Session 3 – Learning Shakespearean Language

**学习目标**：练习把读懂、翻译莎士比亚语言（短句+情绪）
**Learning Goal**: Learn how to understand and translate Shakespearean English into short, emotional phrases

**具体任务**：

* 学生学习莎士比亚英语，并理解其意思；
Students learn about Shakespearean English and its meanings.

* 分组选定一段 3–5 句的台词，逐句用现代英文表达；
In groups, choose 3–5 lines and rewrite each one in modern English.

* 朗读比对原文与改写文，尝试表达语气差异。
<u>Read and compare the original and modern versions, trying to express tone changes.</u>

## Session 4 – Meet the Character 创建“角色档案”

**学习目标**：建立角色基础档案（姓名、愿望、变化）
**Learning Goal**: Create a basic character profile including name, goal, and changes

**具体任务**：

* 教师示范“Character Profile”填写方法：Name, Age, Wants, Feels, Problem；
Teacher models how to complete a character profile (Name, Age, Wants, Feels, Problem).

* 学生参考上节改写结果，绘制角色“情绪曲线”；
Students create an emotion chart based on the rewritten monologue.

* 手绘角色形象并标注关键情绪词汇；
Draw the character and add key emotional words.

* 你觉得这个人物最像哪个你认识的人？为什么？
Discuss: Who in your life is most like this character, and why?

## Session 5 – My Shakespeare Diary Entry 我的莎士比亚角色日记

**学习目标**：用第一人称写一篇英文小日记（我觉得…今天发生了…）
**Learning Goal**: Write a short first-person diary as the character, using feeling-based language

**具体任务**：

* 教师提供日记格式样板：时间+“I am Juliet. Today I felt... because...”；
Teacher provides a diary format sample using "I am... I felt... because...".

* 学生根据角色情境填写 6–8 句英文小段落，加入“I want/I am/I feel/I hope”等结构；
Students write 6–8 sentences using sentence starters such as “I want/I feel/I hope”.

* 教师点评段落，主要注重角色表现和英文表达
Teacher provides feedback, focusing on character clarity and sentence correctness.

# Session 6 – Acting Optional: My Mini Monologue! 小小演绎时间

**学习目标：**朗读一段翻译后台词，并可录制视频
**Learning Goal:** Practice reading a modernized line aloud with emotion; optional recording

**具体任务：**

* 教师示范语音训练：“重音”“断句”“表情读法”；
Teacher demonstrates speech techniques: stress, pauses, and emotional delivery.

* 学生选择 1–2 句自己翻译过的台词进行朗读练习；
Students practice reading 1–2 lines they previously translated.

* 学生提交录音/小视频，或在课堂上进行演讲展示。
Submit an audio/video recording or present aloud in class.

# Session7 - NCT

协助学生完成台词改编，确保其准确理解所选台词的内涵，并指导学生标注语音重音、情绪转换的关键节点，以提升台词表达的感染力和戏剧效果。

**Keywords:** Structural analysis + Modern reinterpretation+Emotion Expression

### 课程目标 English Goals：

* 理解人物语气变化与情感结构
Understand how a character's tone and emotions change

* 掌握古语翻译与现代重写技巧
Learn to translate Shakespearean English and rewrite it in modern form

* 创作具有现代语感的角色独白，并展示
Create and perform a modernized character monologue

### 最终成果 Final Product：

* 一篇完整的改写角色独白和日记
A fully rewritten modern character monologue or diary entry

* 一段配合语调与情绪展示的表演视频（可用作 audition）
A performance video that showcases tone and emotion (audition-ready)
# Session 1 – Shakespeare in 2024: Why Does It Still Matter? 2024 年的莎士比亚

**学习目标**：了解人物处境与语气变化
**Learning Goal**: Understand character situations and tone shifts

具体任务：

* 教师展示 Romeo、Lady Macbeth、Iago 等角色关键片段；

Teacher presents key scenes from characters such as Romeo, Lady Macbeth, and Iago.

* 分析练习：他们在这段台词里经历了哪些情绪变化？用了哪些情绪词（rage, desperate, excited 等）；

Practice analyzing emotional changes and vocabulary (e.g., rage, desperate, excited).

* 课后写一段 5 句评论：“Shakespeare’s characters are still relevant because…”

Write a 5-sentence response explaining why these characters still matter today.

# Session 2 – Language Decode: Translating Shakespeare 翻译莎士比亚

**学习目标**：翻译莎士比亚句子，学会简化比喻和用词替换
**Learning Goal**: Translate Shakespearean lines and simplify figurative language

具体任务：

* 分析原句中的比喻（如“a sea of troubles”）和典型古语（thou, thy, art）；

Analyze metaphors and archaic words like “thou,” “thy,” and “art.”

* 使用词汇替换表将 7 行原文翻译成现代英语，保持角色情感与语气；

Use vocabulary charts to translate 7 lines into modern English while keeping the tone.

* 分句展示翻译成果并解释语气保持策略。

Present line-by-line translations and explain tone-preserving choices.

# Session 3 – Build a Character File 制作角色档案

**学习目标**：引导用英文写出人物动机、阻力、性格关键词
**Learning Goal**: Use English to outline character goals, conflicts, and traits

具体任务：

* 使用“角色动机地图”表格，填写 What they want → What stops them → What they do；

Complete a motivation map: What the character wants → What blocks them → What they do.

* 绘制情绪变化线，标注不同场景下角色态度变化；

Draw an emotional arc to show how the character’s feelings shift over scenes.

* 用英文总结角色“关键词”（例如：ambitious, misunderstood, hopeful）

Summarize the character using descriptive English keywords.

# Session 4 – Monologue Rewrite OR Diary Entry 角色日记/独白撰写

**学习目标**：将角色的高光时刻转换为现代语言表达
**Learning Goal**: Turn a dramatic moment into a modern emotional expression
**具体任务：**

* 选择一个转折性台词片段（如“Is this a dagger...”）；

Choose a turning-point monologue (e.g., “Is this a dagger...”).

* 学生选择以“第一人称独白”或“私密日记”形式进行创作；

Write from the first-person perspective as a monologue or diary entry.

* 运用课上积累的动词与情感表达，写一篇角色独白

Use vocabulary and emotional phrasing learned in class to complete the piece.

* 教师反馈逻辑、词汇、语言节奏等写作技巧。

Teacher provides feedback on structure, vocabulary, and rhythm.

## Session 5 – Add Performance Voice 声音表现练习

**学习目标：**学习语调+表情+手势的英文表达与台词练习方法

**Learning Goal:** Learn how to express tone, emotion, and gesture in English performance

**具体任务：**

* 教师演示台词的停顿、语速变化、重音位置与表情配合；

Teacher models pacing, emphasis, and emotional delivery.

* 学生练习独白段落表演

Students rehearse and perform their monologue.

* 教师提供反馈并布置最终任务。

Teacher gives feedback and sets expectations for final presentation.

## Session 6 – Showcase: Meet My Shakespeare 展演：认识我的莎士比亚

**学习目标：**呈现一个“角色介绍+片段演绎”

**Learning Goal:** Present a character introduction and performance

**具体任务：**

* 每位学生准备一段 1 分钟的演读台词；

Each student prepares and delivers a 1-minute monologue.

* 展示角色语音语调控制（包含强调词、疑问句等）；

Focus on voice, intonation, stress, and punctuation.

* 完成演出视频或线上实时演出；

Submit a performance video or perform live online.

## Session 7 NCT

协助学生完成台词改编，确保其准确理解所选台词的内涵，并指导学生标注语音重音、情绪转换的关键节点，以提升台词表达的感染力和戏剧效果。

## Session 1 – Shakespearean Psychology: What Drives a Character? 莎士比亚心理学：角色动机是什么？

**学习目标**：分析台词中的潜台词与修辞技巧

**Learning Goal**: Analyze character psychology and rhetorical structure

### 具体任务：

* 精读选定人物独白（如“Tomorrow, and tomorrow, and tomorrow…”）；

Close-read a major monologue such as “Tomorrow, and tomorrow, and tomorrow…”

* 标注关键词、语气变化、内心冲突点；

Annotate tone shifts, keywords, and internal conflict moments.

* 辨析修辞策略（重复、排比、拟人、讽刺）；

Identify rhetorical strategies: repetition, irony, metaphor, parallelism.

* 用英文撰写 5 句分析评论（I think this shows…）

Write 5-sentence English commentary beginning with “I think this shows…”

# Session 3 – The Actor’s Toolkit: Who Are You On Stage? 莎士比亚演员的自我修养

**学习目标**：建立角色完整档案，包含冲突、地位、转变点

**Learning Goal**: Build a complete character file with arc and position

**具体任务**：

● 用引文支持的方式书写“动机-行为-结果”链条；

Use direct quotes to construct a motivation-behavior-outcome chain.

● 分析角色所处社会语境（性别、身份、阶层）；

Analyze the character's social context (gender, status, class).

● 以“If I were Macbeth…”完成一段英文角色反思短文。

Write a short reflection titled “If I were Macbeth…”

# Session 4 – Dramatic Rewrite + Literary Framing 重写文本+文学塑造

**学习目标**：重构独白结构，引入现代语境与开头结尾逻辑

**Learning Goal**: Rebuild a monologue with modern framing and literary flow

**具体任务**：

● 撰写一篇含“我是谁 → 我为何如此 → 我想要什么”逻辑结构的戏剧性演讲文；

Write a dramatic monologue with the logic: Who am I → Why am I like this → What do I want.

● 加入修辞过渡、反问句与文体递进；

Add rhetorical questions, transitions, and varied sentence types.

# Session 5 – Performance Refinement Lab 表演练习工作坊

**学习目标**：动态呈现语言节奏与文学韵律

**Learning Goal**: Perform with controlled literary rhythm and emotion

**具体任务**：

● 完成两次演读录像并提交教师；

Record two takes and receive peer or teacher feedback.

● 比较语调、节奏、停顿安排的表现效果；

Compare delivery style—intonation, pace, pause.

● 根据反馈修改用词与语速，控制口语表达风格。

Revise based on feedback to refine tone and delivery.
# Session 6 – The Shakespeare Showcase 莎士比亚剧场演出

**学习目标：**呈现台词与表达设计的结合

**Learning Goal:** Present a refined blend of language and character insight

**具体任务：**

* 全英文完成一段 2 分钟个人演读（含自我介绍、角色、演讲台词）；

Deliver a 2-minute solo performance including intro, character, and speech.

* 紧接 1 分钟反思演讲：“Why I chose to portray them this way”；

Follow with a 1-minute reflection: Why I chose to portray them this way.

* 参与 Q&A 环节，回答观众提问或发表他人点评。

# Session 7 NCT

协助学生完成台词改编，确保其准确理解所选台词的内涵，并指导学生标注语音重音、情绪转换的关键节点，以提升台词表达的感染力和戏剧效果。
