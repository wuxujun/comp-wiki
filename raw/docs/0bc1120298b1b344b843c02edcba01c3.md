# PBL-G02 CS-太阳神传说：跨文化神话探秘之旅

## 《太阳神传说：跨文化神话探秘之旅》Battle of the Sun Gods: Comparing World Myths 课程大纲与计划

### Syllabus 1: Beginner Level (TOEFL ~60)

适合初学者（托福约 60 分）

**Target Students:** Grades 5–8 students with basic English proficiency, able to understand simple sentences and express basic ideas.

目标学生：5–8 年级学生，具备基础英语能力，能理解简单句子并表达基本想法。

**Objective:** Build confidence in using English to describe myths and make simple comparisons while creating a visual or dramatic product.

目标：培养学生用英语描述神话故事并进行简单比较的信心，同时创作视觉或表演作品。

**Final Products:** Simple comparison chart, illustrated myth poster, short English reflection (3–5 sentences).

最终成果：简单比较表，神话插图海报，短篇英文反思（3–5 句）。

### Curriculum (6 Classes, 45 Minutes Each)

课程计划（6 课时，每课时 45 分钟）

**Learning Goals 学习目标：**

* Learn names and images of 3 sun gods (Apollo, Ra, Xihe)

* Recognize country + symbol for each god

* 学习 3 位太阳神的名字、形象和所属文化

**Student Task 学生任务：**

* Complete a “sun god ID card” with picture + country + one special power

* 制作“太阳神小卡片”：国家 + 图片 + 一项能力（中英可混）

### Session 2 – What’s the Story? 谁做了什么？

**Learning Goals 学习目标：**

* Learn basic story structure (who, what, why)
*   Practice short story summaries
*   用英文练习“神话故事三句话总结”

**Student Task 学生任务：**

*   Write a 3-sentence summary of one myth: “He is… He did… He helps…”
*   用“He is… He did… He helps…”造句总结一个神话

## Session 3 – Let’s Compare! 简单对比我能说

**Learning Goals 学习目标：**

*   Learn “similar / different” structures (He is like… / In China… but in Egypt…)
*   学会最基本的英文比较句型

**Student Task 学生任务：**

*   Complete a Venn diagram or T-chart comparing two gods
*   用图表法比较两个太阳神（外形、能力、文化）

## Session 4 – Draw or Act: Show the Myth!

用画或动作讲故事

**Learning Goals 学习目标：**

*   Choose between drawing or simple role-play
*   Learn phrases like: “I am Ra. I bring the sun every day.”
*   用英文图注或角色语句表达神话角色

**Student Task 学生任务：**

*   Draw 1 god and write 2 sentences, OR record a short intro line
*   画图并配 2 句英文，或录制一句角色台词

## Session 5 – If I Had a Sun Power 如果我也有太阳的力量

**Learning Goals 学习目标：**

*   Practice personal writing: “If I were a sun god, I would…”
*   学习英文虚拟表达（愿望/设想）

**Student Task 学生任务：**

*   Write a 4–5 sentence paragraph: “If I were a sun god, I would…”
*   写一小段英文“我自己的太阳神想象”
# Session 6 – Gallery & Sharing 展示和讲一讲

**Learning Goals 学习目标：**

* Practice oral sharing: introduce your drawing or reading your paragraph

* 用简单英文口语分享自己的作品

**Student Task 学生任务：**

* Read your paragraph and show your chart/drawing

* 朗读自己的段落，展示图表或插图

# Syllabus 2: Intermediate Level (TOEFL ~80)

适合中级学生（托福约 80 分）

**Target Students:** Grades 5–8 students with moderate English proficiency, able to form complete sentences and understand short paragraphs.

**目标学生：**5–8 年级学生，具备中等英语能力，能组成完整句子并理解短段落。

**Objective:** Develop comparative language skills and cultural analysis through creative projects and structured writing.

**目标：**通过创意项目和结构化写作，培养比较语言技能和文化分析能力。

**Final Products:** Detailed comparison chart, illustrated story or short skit, 5–7 sentence English reflection.

**最终成果：**详细比较表，插图故事或短剧，5–7 句英文反思。

**Curriculum (6 Classes, 45 Minutes Each)**

**课程计划（6 课时，每课时 45 分钟）**

**Class 1: Introduction to Mythology and Sun Gods**

* **Objective:** Understand the concept of myths and introduce sun gods.

* **目标：**理解神话概念，介绍太阳神。

* **Activities:** Watch a video on world myths (English with Chinese subtitles). Discuss: “Why do cultures have sun gods?” Learn comparative phrases: “more than,” “similar to.”

* **活动：**观看世界神话视频（英文带中文字幕）。讨论：“为什么文化中有太阳神？”学习比较短语：“多于”“类似于”。

* **Homework:** Write 3 sentences about why myths are important.

* **作业：**写 3 句关于神话重要性的句子。

**Class 2: Apollo and Greek Culture**

* **Objective:** Analyze Apollo’s myth and its cultural significance.

* **目标：**分析阿波罗的神话及其文化意义。

* **Activities:** Read a short Apollo story. Discuss: “What does Apollo tell us about
Greek culture?" Start a comparison chart with categories (e.g., role, symbols, culture).

* **活动**：阅读阿波罗短篇故事。讨论：“阿波罗告诉我们关于希腊文化的什么？”开始制作比较表（角色、象征、文化等）。

* **Homework**: Write 4 sentences about Apollo and Greek culture.

* **作业**：写 4 句关于阿波罗和希腊文化的句子。

## Class 3: Ra and Egyptian Culture

* **Objective**: Explore Ra’s myth and compare with Apollo.

* **目标**：探索拉的神话，与阿波罗比较。

* **Activities**: Teacher presents Ra’s story via slides. Students add Ra to the comparison chart. Practice: “Ra is similar to Apollo because…” Group discussion on Egyptian values.

* **活动**：老师通过幻灯片展示拉的故事。学生将拉加入比较表。练习：“拉与阿波罗相似因为……”小组讨论埃及价值观。

* **Homework**: Write 3 sentences comparing Ra and Apollo.

* **作业**：写 3 句比较拉和阿波罗的句子。

## Class 4: Xihe and Chinese Culture

* **Objective**: Understand Xihe’s role and complete the comparison chart.

* **目标**：理解羲和的角色，完成比较表。

* **Activities**: Read Xihe’s myth (bilingual). Discuss: “How does Xihe reflect Chinese culture?” Add Xihe to chart. Practice complex sentences: “Unlike Apollo, Xihe…”

* **活动**：阅读羲和神话（双语）。讨论：“羲和如何反映中国文化？”将羲和加入比较表。练习复杂句子：“不像阿波罗，羲和……”

* **Homework**: Write 4 sentences about Xihe and Chinese culture.

* **作业**：写 4 句关于羲和和中国文化的句子。

## Class 5: Creative Project Development

* **Objective**: Create an illustrated story or short skit comparing the gods.

* **目标**：创作比较神的插图故事或短剧。

* **Activities**: Students choose to illustrate a story or write a skit script (e.g., gods meeting and discussing their roles). Teacher provides sentence starters and feedback.

* **活动**：学生选择绘制插图故事或编写短剧剧本（如神明相遇讨论角色）。老师提供句式模板和反馈。

* **Homework**: Complete story or skit.

* **作业**：完成故事或剧本。

## Class 6: Presentation and Reflection

* **Objective**: Present projects and write a reflective paragraph.

* **目标**：展示作品，写反思段落。

* **Activities**: Students share projects via Zoom. Write 5–7 sentences: “I learned about… The myths are similar because…” Peer feedback using full sentences.

* **活动**：学生通过 Zoom 分享作品。写 5–7 句反思：“我学到……神话相似因为……”用完整句子进行同伴反馈。
*   **Homework**: None.
*   **作业**：无。

# Syllabus 3: Advanced Level (TOEFL ~100)

适合高级学生（托福约 100 分）

**Target Students**: Grades 5–8 students with strong English proficiency, able to write paragraphs and engage in discussions.

目标学生：5–8 年级学生，具备较强英语能力，能写段落并参与讨论。

**Objective**: Develop analytical and creative skills through in-depth comparison of myths and polished English deliverables.

目标：通过深入的神话比较和精炼的英文产出，培养分析与创造力。

**Final Products**: Comprehensive comparison chart, dramatized skit or detailed illustration, 150-word English reflection.

最终成果：全面比较表，戏剧化短剧或详细插图，150 字英文反思。

## Curriculum (6 Classes, 45 Minutes Each)
课程计划（6 课时，每课时 45 分钟）

**Class 1: Myths and Cultural Significance**

*   **Objective**: Analyze the role of myths in culture and introduce sun gods.

*   **目标**：分析神话在文化中的作用，介绍太阳神。

*   **Activities**: Watch a video on mythology’s role in society. Discuss: “How do myths shape cultural identity?” Introduce Apollo, Ra, Xihe with key themes.

*   **活动**：观看关于神话与社会角色的视频。讨论：“神话如何塑造文化身份？”介绍阿波罗、拉、羲和及其主题。

*   **Homework**: Write a 5-sentence paragraph on why myths matter.

*   **作业**：写 5 句关于神话重要性的段落。

**Class 2: Apollo and Greek Values**

*   **Objective**: Analyze Apollo’s myth and its cultural context.

*   **目标**：分析阿波罗神话及其文化背景。

*   **Activities**: Read a detailed Apollo story. Discuss: “What Greek values are shown in Apollo’s story?” Start a detailed comparison chart (e.g., role, symbols, values).

*   **活动**：阅读详细的阿波罗故事。讨论：“阿波罗故事展现了哪些希腊价值观？”开始制作详细比较表（角色、象征、价值观）。

*   **Homework**: Write a 100-word summary of Apollo’s myth.

*   **作业**：写 100 字的阿波罗神话总结。

**Class 3: Ra and Egyptian Values**

*   **Objective**: Explore Ra’s myth and compare with Apollo.

*   **目标**：探索拉的神话，与阿波罗比较。

*   **Activities**: Analyze Ra’s story via slides. Add to comparison chart. Practice advanced comparisons: “While Apollo represents..., Ra symbolizes...” Discuss Egyptian
worldview.

*   活动：通过幻灯片分析拉的故事。补充比较表。练习高级比较：“阿波罗代表……，而拉象征……”讨论埃及世界观。

*   **Homework: Write 5 sentences comparing Apollo and Ra.**

*   **作业：写 5 句比较阿波罗和拉的句子。**

**Class 4: Xihe and Chinese Values**

*   **Objective:** Analyze Xihe’s myth and finalize comparison chart.

*   **目标：**分析羲和神话，完成比较表。

*   **Activities:** Read Xihe’s myth. Discuss: “How does Xihe reflect Chinese values like harmony?” Complete chart. Practice: “Xihe differs from others because…”

*   **活动：**阅读羲和神话。讨论：“羲和如何反映和谐等中国价值观？”完成比较表。练习：“羲和与其他神的不同在于……”

*   **Homework: Write a 100-word summary of Xihe’s myth.**

*   **作业：写 100 字的羲和神话总结。**

**Class 5: Creative Project and Script Writing**

*   **Objective:** Create a detailed skit or illustrated narrative.

*   **目标：**创作详细的短剧或插图叙事。

*   **Activities:** Students write a skit where gods debate their roles or illustrate a narrative comparing myths. Teacher provides feedback on English clarity and creativity.

*   **活动：**学生编写神明辩论角色的短剧或绘制比较神话的插图叙事。老师对英语清晰度和创意提供反馈。

*   **Homework: Finalize skit or illustration.**

*   **作业：完成短剧或插图。**

**Class 6: Presentation and Reflective Writing**

*   **Objective:** Present projects and write a detailed reflection.

*   **目标：**展示作品，写详细反思。

*   **Activities:** Students present via Zoom. Write a 150-word reflection: “What I learned about culture… The myths teach us…” Peer feedback in detailed sentences.

*   **活动：**学生通过 Zoom 展示作品。写 150 字反思：“我学到的文化……神话教给我们……”用详细句子进行同伴反馈。

*   **Homework: None.**

*   **作业：无。**
