> - **简介**:: 
Music Producer (Music Producer) is a Subject Based Advisory program for music creation designed and initiated by the Thinktown music team. This product is aimed at students who have a certain foundation in music and have a strong interest in learning music composition, and hope to present their personal ideas or research results in the form of original music. Under the full professional guidance of the music academic advisor the finished composition will be published in the form of a single on a public music platform. This product can be used as an independent background improvement project to add points to the application, or as a module for other extracurricular activities.
