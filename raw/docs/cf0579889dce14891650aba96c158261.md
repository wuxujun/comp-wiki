# 像导演那样拍照：人像摄影故事创作

## Photograph Like a Director: Portrait Photography

## Story Creation

**AA Instructor 指导老师**

章放歌

**Original PBL topic 原始题目**

摆拍照片系列讲述个人故事

**Project Summary 项目简介**

学生将学习摆拍摄影的艺术技巧，如构图、光影运用、道具选择、人物姿态，来精心构建和传达一个个人故事（如“克服考试焦虑”、“探索梦想”）。项目重点在于如何通过视觉语言和图像序列，像电影一样讲述一个有情感、有情节的故事。

**Parent Introduction 家长说明**

您的孩子将学习像电影导演一样，通过摆拍摄影来讲述自己的故事。他们会掌握摄影艺术技巧（如光影、构图），并学会如何用一系列精心设计的照片，视觉化地表达情感和叙事。这不仅能培养他们的摄影技能和视觉讲故事能力，还能提升他们的自信和创意表达，并用英文清晰地阐述故事内容和艺术手法。

**Target Age Grade 适合年级**

G5-10 年级学生

**Subjects Integrated 跨学科整合**

摄影艺术、视觉叙事、电影艺术、个人发展

**Final Products 项目成果：**

* “我的故事”摆拍摄影作品集 (My Story Staged Photography Series)：一组数字照片，通过精心设计的摆拍和序列，讲述一个个人故事。

* 英文艺术叙事说明 (English Artistic Narrative Explanation)：解释每张照片的艺术意图和在故事中的作用。

**English Learning Outcomes 英语学习关联**

* 学习描述视觉元素、阐述叙事逻辑、表达情感和个人经历的英文表达；
* 练习撰写故事叙述和艺术说明。

**English Deliverables 英文产出**

* "My Story Staged Photography Series" (英文“我的故事”摆拍摄影系列)；

* English Artistic Narrative Explanation (英文艺术叙事说明)。

# 第一节课：基本主题与审美培养

第一节课前作业：看完约一个多小时的相机参数基础视频（例如光圈快门 iso 白平衡等），老师发视频链接。如果对相机各参数含义都理解，可以不看或者选择地看。

有过往摄影作品可以发给老师。

把自己用于拍照的设备型号告诉老师，相机或手机都可以，老师会去调研这个设备的摄影功能，从而在课上进行个性化的教学。

第一节课学习目标：找到基本的主题方向，了解基本的审美培养方法。

第一节课内容：

1. 头脑风暴自己最关心的问题，这个头脑风暴需要有一个框架：

    你关心的事情有什么关键词？

    你成长过程中印象深刻的事情？

    你有没有很想表达的情绪？

    你的梦想是什么？

    你在生活中最喜欢什么？

    你不喜欢什么？

    老师从头脑风暴中提取可能的主题，让学生选择

2. 根据学生所说的问题，选取大师作品案例来进行审美训练（滨田英明、秋山亮二，索尔莱特，薇薇安迈尔，布列松，Alex Webb 等）学生在每个案例中选取一个，老师带着细读，从前景、后景、用光、道具、光圈、表情等角度去剖析照片好的地方。帮助学生初步掌握赏析作品的方法。
第一节课作业：

1. 在老师给的摆拍叙事参考案例列表里面，选取 15 张以上符合自己拍摄主题的照片，涵盖至少 5 个以上的艺术家参考案例。可以用 eagle 软件分类。

2. 拍 3 张人物，自己觉得好看就行。

# Lesson 1: Basic Themes and Aesthetic Cultivation

Pre-Lesson 1 Homework: Watch about an hour or more of basic camera parameter videos (e.g., aperture, shutter, ISO, white balance, etc.). The teacher will provide video links. If you already understand the meanings of each camera parameter, you can skip or selectively watch.

If you have previous photography works, you can send them to the teacher.

Tell the teacher the model of the equipment you use for photography, whether it's a camera or a phone. The teacher will research the photography functions of this equipment to provide personalized teaching in class.

Lesson 1 Learning Objectives: Identify basic theme directions and understand basic methods for cultivating aesthetics.

Lesson 1 Content:

1. Brainstorm the issues you care about most, using a framework for this brainstorming:

What keywords describe the things you care about?
What memorable events from your growth process?
Do you have emotions you really want to express?
What are your dreams?
What do you like most in life?
What do you dislike?

2. Based on the issues mentioned by the student, select masterworks as case studies for aesthetic training (e.g., Hideaki Hamada, Ryoji Akiyama, Saul Leiter, Vivian Maier, Henri Cartier-Bresson, Alex Webb, etc.). The student selects one from each case, and the teacher guides a close reading, analyzing the strengths of the photo from perspectives such as foreground, background, lighting, props, aperture, expressions, etc. This helps the student initially master methods for appreciating works.

Lesson 1 Homework:

1. From the list of staged narrative reference cases provided by the teacher, select 10 photos that resonate with you, covering more than 3 artists.

2. Take 3 portraits that you think look good.
# 第二节课：主题细化与审美培养

第二节课学习目标：找到叙事摆拍主题，初步规划艺术表达方式，同时提升审美素养。

## 第二节课：

1. 分析学生选取的照片，细读它们表达的内容和情感，以及表达方式，还有构图

2. 根据 1 的推理，帮助学生细化摆拍叙事的主题。

3. 最后讲解学生的 3 张人物照优缺点，进行构图训练的命题作业（通常是让学生注意某个点，例如主要的色彩不超过 2 个）

## 第二节课作业：

1. 根据摆拍叙事主题，学生进行查资料调研，并对 potential 的拍摄地进行踩点，完成以下初步策划，需要画简易草图

    哪些场景可以表达你的主题（写 5 个）

    怎样的衣服、妆发适合表达你的主题（写 5 个）

    说起你的主题，你会想到什么颜色（写 3 个）

    哪些物品和你的主题有关（写 5-10 个）

    哪些情绪和你的主题有关（至少 2 个）

2. 人物构图训练 10 张（拍摄人物）

# Lesson 2: Theme Refinement and Aesthetic Cultivation

Lesson 2 Learning Objectives: Identify a narrative staged photography theme, preliminarily plan artistic expression methods, and enhance aesthetic literacy.

Lesson 2:

1. Analyze the photos selected by the student, closely reading their expressed content and emotions, as well as expression methods and composition.

2. Based on the inferences from 1, help the student refine the theme for staged narrative.

3. Finally, explain the strengths and weaknesses of the student's 3 portraits, and assign composition training exercises (usually focusing on a specific point, such as limiting main colors to no more than 2).

## Lesson 2 Homework:

1. Based on the staged narrative theme, conduct research and complete the
following preliminary planning:

What scenes can express your theme (write 5)?

What kinds of clothing are suitable for expressing your theme (write 5)?

What colors come to mind when thinking of your theme (write 3)?

What objects are related to your theme (write 5-10)?

What emotions are related to your theme (at least 2)?

2. Portrait composition training: 10 photos.

# 第三节课：拍摄计划与审美提升

第三节课学习目标：确定第一场拍摄计划，同时提升审美素养。

第三节课：

1. 老师讲解作业的优点和缺点

2. 老师根据作业优缺点，讲解适合学生现阶段学习的案例

3. 老师根据初步策划作业，与学生共同完成第一场拍摄计划（包括场景、道具、模特等），并给予技术指导

第三节课作业：

根据拍摄计划，联系模特，完成 10 张创作。不建议删除不满意的照片，如果有多于 10 张可以全都发给老师挑选。

# Lesson 3: Shooting Plan and Aesthetic Enhancement

Lesson 3 Learning Objectives: Determine the plan for the first shoot, while enhancing aesthetic literacy.

Lesson 3:

1. The teacher explains the strengths and weaknesses of the homework.

2. Based on the homework's strengths and weaknesses, the teacher explains case studies suitable for the student's current stage of learning.

3. Based on the preliminary planning homework, the teacher and student jointly complete the plan for the first shoot (including scenes, props, models, etc.), and provide technical guidance.

Lesson 3 Homework:
Complete 10 creations based on the shooting plan. It is not recommended to delete unsatisfactory photos; if there are more than 10, you can send all of them to the teacher for selection.

# 第四节课：作品复盘与表达提升

第四课学习目标：复盘第一批拍摄的作业，提升审美素养，深化艺术表达方式。

### 第四节课：

1. 老师讲解作业的优点和缺点

2. 老师和学生讨论这组摆拍组图深化方向

3. 老师根据作业优缺点，讲解适合学生现阶段学习的案例

4. 头脑风暴优秀作品的文字讲解

### 第四节课作业：

根据拍摄计划完成 10 张创作。不建议删除不满意的照片，如果有多于 10 张可以全都发给老师挑选。

# Lesson 4: Work Review and Expression Enhancement

Lesson 4 Learning Objectives: Review the first batch of shooting homework, enhance aesthetic literacy, and deepen artistic expression methods.

### Lesson 4:

1. The teacher explains the strengths and weaknesses of the homework.

2. The teacher and student discuss directions for deepening this set of staged photo series.

3. Based on the homework's strengths and weaknesses, the teacher explains case studies suitable for the student's current stage of learning.

4. Brainstorm the caption of selected works.

### Lesson 4 Homework:

Complete 10 creations based on the shooting plan. It is not recommended to delete unsatisfactory photos; if there are more than 10, you can send all of them to the teacher for selection.
# 第五节课：作品复盘与表达提升

第五节课学习目标：复盘第二批拍摄的作业，提升审美素养，深化艺术表达方式。

## 第五节课：

1. 老师讲解作业的优点和缺点

2. 老师和学生讨论这组摆拍组图深化方向

3. 老师根据作业优缺点，讲解适合学生现阶段学习的案例

4. 头脑风暴优秀作品的文字讲解

## 第五节课作业：

根据拍摄计划再完成 10 张创作。不建议删除不满意的照片，如果有多于 10 张可以全都发给老师挑选。

# Lesson 5: Work Review and Expression Enhancement

Lesson 5 Learning Objectives: Review the second batch of shooting homework, enhance aesthetic literacy, and deepen artistic expression methods.

## Lesson 5:

1. The teacher explains the strengths and weaknesses of the homework.

2. The teacher and student discuss directions for deepening this set of staged photo series.

3. Based on the homework's strengths and weaknesses, the teacher explains case studies suitable for the student's current stage of learning.

4. Brainstorm the caption of selected works.

## Lesson 5 Homework:

Complete another 10 creations based on the shooting plan. It is not recommended to delete unsatisfactory photos; if there are more than 10, you can send all of them to the teacher for selection.

# 第六节课：挑选作品与摄影后期
第六节课学习目标：复盘第三批拍摄的作业，挑选最终作品，学习必要的摄影后期技巧。

第六节课：

1. 确认最终作品，

2. 头脑风暴优秀作品的文字讲解

3. 老师给出后期建议和后期教学。

4. 后期摄影审美训练

第六节课作业：

完成已选主题的后期处理

# Lesson 6: Selecting Works and Post-Processing in Photography

Lesson 6 Learning Objectives: Review the third batch of shooting homework, select final works, and learn necessary photography post-processing techniques.

Lesson 6:

1. Confirm the final works.

2. Brainstorm the caption of selected works.

3. The teacher provides post-processing suggestions and teaches post-processing.

4. Post-processing photography aesthetic training.

Lesson 6 Homework:

Complete post-processing for the selected theme.

# 第七节课：后期提升与作品描述

第七节课目标：提升后期审美素养，完成作品描述大纲

第七节课：

1. 复盘学生的后期效果，讲解调整方向

2. 作品描述提纲头脑风暴
3. 作品排序讨论和确定。

## 第七节课作业

学生完成作品描述和提纲，给老师批改（1NCT）。

学生观看 Bilibili 作品集排版教学视频，以及老师推荐的作品集排版案例，用 Indesign 进行初步排版。

# Lesson 7: Post-Processing Enhancement and Work Description

Lesson 7 Objectives: Enhance post-processing aesthetic literacy and complete the outline for work descriptions.

## Lesson 7:

1. Review the student's post-processing effects and explain adjustment directions.

2. Brainstorm the outline for work descriptions.

3. Discuss and determine the sequencing of works.

## Lesson 7 Homework:

The student completes the work descriptions and outline, and sends them to the teacher for review (1 round of corrections).

The student watches Bilibili portfolio layout teaching videos, as well as teacher-recommended portfolio layout cases, and uses InDesign for preliminary layout.

# 第八节课：作品集排版优化

第八节课目标：让学生有能力完成符合其艺术叙事的作品集排版

## 第八节课

1. 讲解摄影作品集排版装帧的含义，讨论哪种最适合学生的作品最终呈现

2. 讲解学生现有排版的可优化之处。

3. 总结上课表现，提出接下来的艺术发展建议。

第八节课作业：完成作品集排版。

3. Summarize class performance and provide suggestions for future artistic development.

Lesson 8 Homework: Complete the portfolio layout.

## Chapter 1: Sailing

I yearn for an environment where I can be free and independent and settle down the unsettling aspects of growing up. I am my own helmsman in an unknown place, facing thrills and troubles, showing the meaning of my life. The bathtub is a wet space created by myself. It is a place where I can create the color, smell, and temperature of the water. Lying in it releases the stress from my body and fills me with a sense of security and a sense of connection to the world. The state when I am addicted to the bathtub is like a baby coming back to its parents' body, making up the anesthesia in an unknown place, setting me free.

This ritual is like a fish hatched in fresh water heading to the ocean, but only after the migration is the life journey complete. The place where we grow up may not be our ideal world, but it must be the cornerstone and reference for us to perceive the future world.
