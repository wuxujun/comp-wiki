# 直播打游戏如何赚钱？流量变现机制解析
How Do Gamers Make Money? Exploring the Streaming Economy

## AA Instructor 指导老师

王承璐

## Original PBL Topic 原始题目

直播经济背后的流量魔法
The Magic of Streaming

## Project Summary 项目简介

游戏主播是如何靠打游戏赚钱的？本课程将带领学生了解内容创作的核心机制：内容吸引流量，流量转化为收入。通过对游戏直播平台的调研、热门主播案例分析，以及虚拟收入路径的模拟，学生将构建“内容 → 流量 → 变现”逻辑模型，并通过数据图表与英文讲解，展示他们对直播经济的理解。
How do streamers earn money by playing games? In this project, students explore the core mechanics of content monetization — how engaging content brings traffic, and how traffic turns into income. They will research popular streamers, analyze business models, and simulate revenue paths to build a “Content–Traffic–Monetization” map. Students will present their findings using visuals and English communication.

## Parent Introduction 家长说明

这门课程以青少年熟悉的“游戏直播”为入口，帮助他们理解现代数字经济的真实逻辑。通过调研、数据整理、逻辑建模与英文表达，孩子不仅能探索“打游戏赚钱”的商业原理，也能反思自己作为观众的行为动机与心理反应。课程鼓励学生用结构化方式呈现复杂流程，适合线上授课，兼具趣味性与认知挑战。
This course uses the familiar world of game streaming to introduce students to the realities of the digital economy. Through research, data analysis, logical modeling, and English output, students not only understand how content creators earn revenue, but also reflect on their own motivations as viewers. The project promotes structured thinking and communication, making it ideal for online learning with a balance of fun and challenge.
