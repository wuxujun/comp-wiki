# PBL Film Review Writing Course for Grades 8–10
# 8–10 年级 PBL 电影评论写作课程

## Course Overview

## 课程概述

This immersive, project-based learning (PBL) course is designed to develop students' critical thinking, writing, and media literacy skills through the analysis and review of films. By engaging with films across various genres and cultures, students will learn to articulate their opinions thoughtfully and construct well-structured film reviews. The course will culminate in a portfolio of written reviews and multimedia presentations.

这是一门沉浸式的项目式学习（PBL）课程，旨在通过电影分析与影评写作，培养学生的批判性思维、写作能力和媒介素养。通过接触不同类型、不同文化背景的电影，学生将学习如何有逻辑地表达观点，并写出结构清晰、内容扎实的影评。课程最终将以书面影评作品集和多媒体展示作为成果呈现。

## Course Structure

## 课程结构

This program consists of three separate courses: Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced, with each course containing 6 lessons. The courses are conducted in small classes to encourage collaboration and in-depth discussions. Each course concludes with a hands-on summative project.

本项目共分为三个独立课程阶段：初级、中级和高级，每个阶段包含 6 节课。课程采用小班形式开展，以鼓励学生合作学习和深入讨论。每一阶段课程都将以一个实践型总结项目作为结课成果。

## Course Summaries

## 课程简介

**Beginner Course: Introduction to Film Analysis**

**初级课程：电影分析入门**

This course provides foundational knowledge in film critique and review writing. Students will explore storytelling elements, the structure of film reviews, and key cinematic techniques. By analyzing short films and reviewing professional critiques, they will develop the skills needed to write structured and insightful reviews. The course concludes with a final project where students compile their written reviews into a portfolio and present an in-depth film critique.

本课程为学生提供电影评论与影评写作的基础知识。学生将学习电影叙事要素、影评结构以及核心电影语言技巧。通过分析短片和阅读专业影评，学生将逐步掌握撰写结构完整、观点深入影评的能力。课程最后，学生将整理自己的书面影评作品，形成个人作品集，并完成一次深入的电影评论展示。
# Intermediate Course: Exploring Film Genres & Themes

**中级课程：探索电影类型与主题**

Expanding on the beginner course, this course delves into film genres and thematic analysis. Students will examine different film genres, explore how films address social issues, and discuss ethical considerations in filmmaking. Through comparative reviews and cultural film analysis, they will deepen their critical understanding of cinema’s social, cultural, and political elements. The final project involves creating a comprehensive thematic and genre-based portfolio with a self-reflection component.

在初级课程的基础上，本课程将进一步深入电影类型和主题分析。学生将研究不同的电影类型，探讨电影如何回应社会议题，并讨论电影创作中的伦理问题。通过类型比较影评和文化电影分析，学生将加深对电影中社会、文化和政治维度的理解。课程最终成果为一份围绕主题与类型展开的综合性作品集，并包含学生的自我反思内容。

# Advanced Course: Multimedia Film Reviews

**高级课程：多媒体电影评论**

This course focuses on transitioning from written film reviews to multimedia formats. Students will explore video reviews, podcasts, and blog formats, learning how to script and edit multimedia content. They will practice recording and presenting their reviews, culminating in a final project where they produce and publish a polished multimedia film review, incorporating both written and video/audio elements.

本课程聚焦于从传统书面影评过渡到多媒体影评形式。学生将探索视频影评、播客和博客等不同媒介形式，学习如何撰写脚本并编辑多媒体内容。他们将在课程中练习录制与展示自己的影评，最终完成一个综合性项目，制作并发布一份成熟的多媒体电影评论作品，结合文字与视频/音频表达。

Each course is structured to build on prior knowledge, ensuring a progressive learning experience in film analysis and media literacy.

每个阶段课程都建立在前一阶段知识基础之上，确保学生能够在电影分析与媒介素养方面获得循序渐进的学习体验。
