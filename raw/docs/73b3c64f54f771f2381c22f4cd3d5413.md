# 电影评论写作：

# 透过电影理解世界，用文字表达观点

**Film Review Writing: Understanding the World through Film and Learning to Express Ideas through Writing**

7课时/阶段 7 sessions/level

Film camera illustration

## 课程概述 Overview

本课程系列旨在通过电影分析的视角，提升学生的批判性思维、学术写作能力和传媒方向的学术背景。学生将探索电影叙事技巧、镜头语言以及主题升华，最终完成一系列文字或多媒体组成的电影评论作品集。

This course series is designed to build students' critical thinking, academic writing, and media literacy skills through the lens of film analysis. Students will explore storytelling techniques, cinematic language, and thematic depth across a variety of films, ultimately crafting a portfolio of written and multimedia film reviews.

## 适合学员 Suitable for Students

* 对电影、写作或媒体研究有兴趣的学生
* 未来有意申请电影、人文学科、社会科学、传媒或艺术类专业的学生
* 希望提升分析类写作能力、批判性思维能力与数字内容创作能力的学生
* Students passionate about film, writing, or media studies
* Future applicants to film, humanities, social sciences, media, or arts programs
* Students seeking to strengthen analytical writing, critical thinking, and digital content creation skills

## 课程模块 Syllabus

### 基础班 Beginner Level

* 理解电影叙事及视听语言
Understanding Film Narrative and Visual Language

* 掌握评论核心要素及分析视角
Mastering Core Elements and Analytical Perspectives of Film Criticism

* 影评写作实践与反馈
Film Review Writing Practice and Feedback

### 进阶班 Intermediate Level

* 学习电影类型及对比分析
Studying Film Genres and Comparative Analysis

* 探索电影中的社会议题、伦理及全球化视角
Exploring Social Issues, Ethics, and Global Perspectives in Cinema

* 制作影评作品集
Creating a Film Review Portfolio

### 高级班 Advanced Level

* 了解多媒体影评格式及结构
Understanding Multimedia Film Review Formats and Structures

* 熟悉音视频剪辑技术
Mastering Audio-Visual Editing Techniques

* 完成多媒体影评制作及发布
Completing and Publishing Multimedia Film Reviews

## 课程亮点 Course Highlights

### 沉浸式探索体验 Engaging & Exploratory

* 从影评者的角度出发，探索不同类型与文化的电影如何讲述故事、反映观点以及激发观众思考
Adopt the perspective of a cultural critic and explore how films across genres and cultures tell stories, reflect values, and provoke thought.

* 分析电影的关键元素——叙事结构、摄影、音效、剪辑——并探讨这些手法如何赋予影片意义
Analyze key cinematic elements—narrative structure, cinematography, sound, editing—and discuss how they shape meaning.

### Pencil icon 学术写作能力提升 Academic Writing Skills Enhancement

* 学习如何写出清晰、有说服力、结构完整的电影评论
Learn how to write clear, persuasive, and structured film reviews through guided drafting and feedback.

* 完成多篇500-800字的分析评论，并制作多媒体评论作品（结合音频/视频的展现形式）
Craft a series of 500-800 word analytical reviews and a final multimedia review using audio/visual presentation formats.

### Globe icon 跨学科项目式学习 Interdisciplinary Project-Based Research

* 融合文学、视觉艺术、社会学与伦理学等学科概念，用更丰富和深入的方式解读电影
Integrate concepts from literature, visual arts, sociology, and ethics to interpret film in rich, layered, intellectual ways.

* 最终项目可以用于投稿，参加纽约时报影评写作比赛（NYT Review）、美国学术艺术与写作奖（SAWA）等竞赛
Final projects may be adapted for submission to competitions like the NYT Review Writing Contest or the Scholastic Arts & Writing Awards.

## 师资介绍 Teaching Team

Portrait of Jack Dwyer
**Jack Dwyer**
毕业于汉密尔顿学院，主修世界政治，专攻国际发展方向
B.A. in World Politics with a specialization in International Development from Hamilton College.

加入新课堂前，Jack 曾深耕于电影与电视行业，担任创意开发专员，评估剧本、电视剧试播集、小说及舞台剧作品，并与编剧和制片人合作进行原创内容开发。加入新课堂后，他已指导数百位学生，致力于帮助学生提高他们的批判性思维、写作与多媒体表达能力，并帮助毕业生们获得麻省理工大学、杜克大学、约翰霍普金斯大学、韦尔斯利学院、纽约大学、西北大学等顶尖院校的录取。

Before beginning his career at Thinktown in 2020, Jack spent several years working in the film and television industry. In his work as a Creative Development Associate, he evaluated material such as screenplays, television pilots, novels, and stage plays and worked with writers and producers on narrative development for original material. Since joining Thinktown, Jack has mentored hundreds of students, helping them hone their critical thinking, writing, and multimedia skills and gain admission to top-tier universities such as MIT, Duke, Johns Hopkins, Wellesley College, NYU, and Northwestern.
