# Pet Diaries Writing PBL Client Connection Guide (CCG)

## Question 1: What academic subjects does this course cover?

## Primary Learning Opportunities

This course is a creative writing course focused on storytelling from a pet's perspective, covering genres like diary writing, fiction, and personal narrative, while developing core English writing skills.

Students learn key techniques like narrative structure, character development, dialogue, sensory detail, and emotional expression through model texts an guided writing activities.

## Interdisciplinary Learning Opportunities:

In addition to English and writing, the course also introduces **interdisciplinary learning opportunities** depending on the type of story a student chooses.

## Interdisciplinary Learning Opportunities

Depending on their topic, students may also engage in light research and real-world connections, such as:

* **Science:** animal behavior, habitats, instincts

* **Social Studies:** roles of working animals (police, rescue, service)

* **Ethics:** adoption, rescue, responsible ownership

* **Media & Literature:** analyzing or rewriting stories from books/films

* **Health:** basic veterinary care and animal wellbeing

This allows students to connect writing with real-world knowledge, making their work more meaningful and informed.

**Parent takeaway:** This course combines creative writing with real-world knowledge, building both strong writing skills and broader academic awareness.
# Question 2: What skills will students develop after completing the course?

## Writing Skills

* **Idea Development & Outlining:** generate and organize story ideas before drafting

* **Narrative Structure:** clear beginning, middle, end with conflict and resolution

* **Character & Dialogue:** build expressive characters and natural interactions

* **Descriptive & Emotional Writing:** “show, not tell” using sensory detail

* **Revision & Editing:** improve clarity, flow, and structure through feedback

## Research Skills

* **Targeted Research:** explore topics like animal roles, behavior, or real-world settings

* **Application:** integrate details to make stories more realistic and engaging

## Creative & Analytical Skills

* **Perspective-Taking:** build empathy through a pet’s point of view

* **Creativity & Observation:** develop original ideas from real-life inspiration

***

**Short-term benefits:**

Stronger writing and English expression, improved structure, and development of research skills

**Long-term benefits:**

**Students build skills that support:**

* improved academic writing and in-school performance

* improved test results, grades and overall GPA

* future application essays and advanced coursework

* creative portfolios and competition submissions

Students also gain confidence and stronger personal writing voice.

# Question 3: Does this class have any prerequisites?
# What students are a good fit?

* This course is designed for Grades 5–10 and is fully personalized based on each student’s level (grade and TOEFL).

* Younger students may be considered if they show strong interest, readiness, and willingness to learn.

* Students will not be overwhelmed—instruction and pacing are adjusted to each individual.

## Recommended Level

TOEFL 60+ (flexible based on writing sample)

## Who is this course ideal for?

**Students who:**

* Enjoy storytelling, creativity, or animals

* Want to improve English writing in an engaging way

* Prefer personalized 1-on-1 instruction and feedback

* Are looking for meaningful activities during breaks, weekends, or lighter schedules

**It is also beneficial for students interested in:**

* Humanities, psychology, or communications

* Biology, animal science, or pre-vet pathways

* Media, writing, or creative fields

# Question 4: What will students produce in this course?

Students will complete one or more polished pet stories, with the option to create a short collection or chapbook.

**Each piece includes:**

* A clear introduction and structured narrative

* Developed scenes, dialogue, and sensory detail

* A strong conclusion

* A revised and polished final draft
## Feedback & Revision Process

**Students receive ongoing, high-touch support:**

* Live instruction and real-time feedback

* Multiple draft submissions and revisions

* Clear, actionable feedback for improvement

* Guidance to help students become independent editors

## Thinktown Writing Approach

**This course mirrors Thinktown’s application essay methodology:**

* Instruction + revision + continuous feedback

* Ongoing support via WeChat/WeCom

* Focus on long-term skill development, not just final output

## Outcome Value

**Students gain both a polished final product and a repeatable writing process that prepares them for:**

* summer program applications

* college essays

* advanced academic writing
