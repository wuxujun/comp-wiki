**PBL Project Name 项目名称**

《从“幸运咖”到“瑞幸”：便宜背后的赚钱逻辑》
How Cheap Coffee Makes Big Money: Business Logic Behind Luckin Coffee

• **Project Summary 项目简介**

In this online PBL course, students will explore how a coffee brand can earn profits even when selling at low prices. Using real-world examples like Luckin and Starbucks, they will learn core business concepts including pricing strategies, cost structures, and profit formulas. Through simulations and visualized analysis, students will build pricing tables, predict break-even points, and make their own pricing proposals. The project will be conducted fully online with guided 1-on-1 or small group sessions.

本课程为线上 PBL 项目，学生将探讨为何“卖得便宜”也能赚到钱。以瑞幸咖啡为例，通过真实商业案例引导，学生将掌握定价策略、成本结构与盈利模型等关键概念，并通过模拟与数据分析，自主设计价格表与盈利预测图表，最终形成英文策略简报与 Pitch 展示。适合 5–10 年级，线上 1v1 或小组授课均可开展。

• **Parent Introduction 家长说明**

This project empowers students to think like young entrepreneurs. By simulating real business decisions, they will strengthen data analysis skills, logical reasoning, and English communication. It’s a valuable preparation for future business competitions and international programs. The course encourages confidence-building, critical thinking, and a practical understanding of how businesses succeed.

本课程通过商业模拟训练孩子的逻辑思维、数据分析与英文表达能力，帮助他们建立商业敏感度、提升自信，并为未来参与商业竞赛和国际课程打下坚实基础。学生将以“模拟创业者”的视角设计价格与策略，在真实情境中提升综合素养。

• **Target Age Grade 适合年级**
Grades 5–10 | 适合 5–10 年级

**Business / Entrepreneurship 商业与创业** **Economics & Math 经济学与数学建模** **English Communication & Writing 英语表达与写作** **Data Visualization & Presentation 数据可视化与演讲**

* A customized **Price Simulation Table** with cost-revenue-profit analysis
* A visual **Profit Model Chart** (bar or pie chart)
* An English brief: **“How Would I Price It?” Strategy Report**
* A 2-minute **Business Pitch Presentation** with Q&A
* 【价格模拟表】：涵盖多种定价情景下的盈亏计算
* 【盈利结构图表】：柱状图或饼图展示成本-利润比例
* 【英文策略简报】：阐述定价逻辑与利润预测

To accommodate different levels from Grades 5–10 and encourage multiple modes of communication, this project includes a variety of English deliverables. These range from short reflective writing and data description to structured reports, video scripts, and oral presentations. All deliverables are designed to enhance logical reasoning, vocabulary usage, and confidence in English communication.

为适配 5–10 年级学生的表达水平，本项目设计了多样化的英文产出任务，覆盖写作、图表解说、口语表达与多媒体创作等不同形式。任务具有分层性，既有简短反思和关键词训练，也有完整报告与英文演讲，帮助学生在逻辑思维、词汇运用和表达信心方面全面提升。

**1. Strategy Report《英文策略简报》**

“How Would I Price It?” strategy report (150–200 words): Includes pricing rationale, simulation data, elasticity reasoning, and final recommendation.《我的定价策略》英文简报（150–200 词）：包含定价原因、模拟数据、弹性分析与最终建议。

**2. Business Reflection Mini-Journal《商业反思迷你日志》**

4–6 short entries (each 3–5 sentences) reflecting learning outcomes and decisions after key lessons. 课程阶段性反思（每篇 3–5 句），记录学习重点与商业判断。

**3. Chart-Based Writing《图表+英文解释段落》**

Describe visual data (bar/pie charts) in 100–150 words using sentence frames and comparative language. 运用句型描述盈利图表（如：“The green part shows the profit, which increases when...”）。

**4. Illustrated Glossary《商业词汇手册/卡片》**

Student-created glossary of 10–15 terms (e.g., margin, elasticity, revenue) with definitions and examples. 自制商业术语词卡，包含定义与例句，便于后续复用于演讲或写作中。

**5. Business Pitch Presentation《英文商业 Pitch 演讲》**

1.5–2-minute English pitch with chart explanation and sales forecast; includes a Q&A segment. 1.5–2 分钟英文演讲，展示价格方案与盈利预测，并接受简单提问挑战。

**6. Short Video Script《微短视频脚本》**

A 10–12 sentence video script explaining pricing logic, cost control, and projected outcome. 编写英文口播稿并录制，介绍自己设计的价格与盈利方式。

**7. Dialogue Simulation《商业情境英文对话》**

Pair activity: Simulate a vendor-customer conversation involving pricing, recommendation, and objection handling. 情境对话练习，例如 “Why is your coffee so cheap?” / “Because we optimized the cost structure.”
