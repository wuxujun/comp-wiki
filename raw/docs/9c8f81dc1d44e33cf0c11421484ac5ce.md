# Topic Setup Final

## 1. PBL Project Name 项目名称:

自然密码：艺术与野性中的分型
Nature's Code: Fractals in Art & the Wild

## 2. AA Instructor 指导老师:

朱宸(Chen)

## 3. Original PBL Topic 原始英文题目:

Fractal Geometry and Natural Art

## 4. Project Summary 项目简介:

Math is more than just abstract numbers; it's a language for describing both nature and art. Students will explore fractal patterns in the natural world and in art, using math to explain how these complex shapes are formed. The curriculum covers the fundamental concepts of fractals, including self-similarity, iterative processes, and their clever applications in the real world. Students will complete a mathematical portfolio and deliver a presentation in English.
数学不仅仅是抽象的数字，更是描述大自然和美学的语言。学生们将探索自然界和艺术中的分形图案，尝试用数学来解释这个世界的形成。课程内容包括分形的基本概念、自相似性、迭代步骤，以及在现实世界中的巧妙应用。学生们将完成一篇数学作品集，并用英文演讲。

## 5. Parent Introduction 家长说明:

This project will give students a new perspective on math. They will learn to explore the world with a rigorous mathematical mindset, developing their observation skills, logical thinking, and ability to apply math. The final project report will train their academic writing and public speaking skills.
本项目让学生对数学有全新的认识。学生将学会如何用严谨的数学思维去探索世界，培养他们的观察力、逻辑思维和数学运用能力。最终的项目报告将锻炼他们的学术写作和演讲能力。

## 6. Target Age Grade 适合年级：

Grades 5-10
## 7. Subjects Integrated 跨学科整合：

Mathematics (Fractal Geometry, Ratios & Proportions, Sequences, Iteration) + Biology (Natural Forms, Biological Patterns, Plant Growth) + Art & Design (Visual Arts, Digital Design)

数学（分形几何、比例与比率、数列、迭代）+ 生物学（自然形态、生物模式、生长规律）+艺术（视觉艺术、数字设计）

## 8. Final Products 项目成果：

一份数学作品集

一个 5-7 分钟的课题报告

A mathematical portfolio

A 5-7 minute oral presentation of the project report

## 9. English Learning Outcomes 英语学习关联：

**Math Vocabulary**: Master academic math terms related to fractals, iteration, ratios, and dimension.

**Academic Writing**: Learn to write well-structured, evidence-based academic report.

**Oral Expression**: Practice public speaking skills to clearly and confidently present complex math concepts and research findings.

**数学词汇**: 掌握相关的数学学术词汇。

**学术写作**: 学习撰写数学学术报告。

**语音表达**: 训练口语表达能力，清晰、自信地展示数学概念和研究成果。

## 10. English Deliverables 英文产出

Mathematical portfolio/report

Presentation (spoken in English)

数学作品集/研究报告（英文）

课题报告（英文口语）
