# 像导演那样拍照：人像摄影故事创作

## Photograph Like a Director: Portrait Photography Story Creation

AA Instructor 指导老师

章放歌

Original PBL topic 原始题目

摆拍照片系列讲述个人故事

### Project Summary 项目简介

学生将学习摆拍摄影的艺术技巧，如构图、光影运用、道具选择、人物姿态，来精心构建和传达一个个人故事（如“克服考试焦虑”、“探索梦想”）。项目重点在于如何通过视觉语言和图像序列，像电影一样讲述一个有情感、有情节的故事。

### Parent Introduction 家长说明

您的孩子将学习像电影导演一样，通过摆拍摄影来讲述自己的故事。他们会掌握摄影艺术技巧（如光影、构图），并学会如何用一系列精心设计的照片，视觉化地表达情感和叙事。这不仅能培养他们的摄影技能和视觉讲故事能力，还能提升他们的自信和创意表达，并用英文清晰地阐述故事内容和艺术手法。

Target Age Grade 适合年级

G5-12 年级学生

Subjects Integrated 跨学科整合
摄影艺术、视觉叙事、电影艺术、个人发展

Final Products 项目成果：

●“我的故事”摆拍摄影作品集 (My Story Staged Photography Series)：一组数字照片，通过精心设计的摆拍和序列，讲述一个个人故事。

●英文艺术叙事说明 (English Artistic Narrative Explanation)：解释每张照片的艺术意图和在故事中的作用。

English Learning Outcomes 英语学习关联

●学习描述视觉元素、阐述叙事逻辑、表达情感和个人经历的英文表达；

●练习撰写故事叙述和艺术说明。

English Deliverables 英文产出

●"My Story Staged Photography Series" (英文“我的故事”摆拍摄影系列)；

●English Artistic Narrative Explanation (英文艺术叙事说明)。

## LEVEL 1：摄影零基础学生 （成果：一组至少 5 张作品和作品描述）

第一节课前作业：看完约一个多小时的相机参数基础视频（例如光圈快门 iso 白平衡等），老师发视频链接。如果对相机各参数含义都理解，可以不看或者选择地看。

有过往摄影作品可以发给老师。

把自己用于拍照的设备型号告诉老师，相机或手机都可以，老师会去调研这个设备的摄影功能，从而在课上进行个性化的教学。

第一节课学习目标：培养摄影的最初审美。
第一节课内容：通过赏析布列松、Saul Leiter、薇薇安迈尔等大师作品，学习摄影的线条审美，以及主体物的创意位置，从而具备基本的构图审美。

第一节课作业：拍摄 20 张生活中的任何线条，从而练习线条审美基础。

第二节课学习目标：培养对摄影色彩的审美。

第二节课内容：老师在讲解作业后，会根据学生作业所呈现出的特点、优缺点，来使用一系列在色彩方面值得学习的摄影大师作品，与学生进行讨论和头脑风暴式的赏析，帮助学生感悟到值得学习的地方。

第二节课作业：以“色彩审美”为核心，在生活中拍摄 20 张自己觉得色彩好看的照片，从而练习色彩审美基础。

第三节课学习目标：学习摄影大师对人物的叙事表达。

第三节课内容：老师在讲解作业后，会根据学生作业所呈现出的特点、优缺点，来使用一系列在人物拍摄表达方面具有创意，而且适合这位学生学习的优秀摄影作品，与学生进行讨论和头脑风暴式的美学赏析，帮助学生感悟到值得学习的地方，并对学生感兴趣的摄影方式进行技术指导。

第三节课作业：针对学生在课上最感兴趣的点（例如人物的性格神态，或者是人物与环境的关系），进行半命题的人物拍摄作业布置，不少于 20 张照片。

第四节课学习目标：进行初步的作品策划头脑风暴

第四节课内容：在评讲作业后，老师给学生讲解 1-2 个有助于改善本次作业问题的优秀摄影师案例。然后，开始头脑风暴本次摄影创作主题，找到学生想要创作的方向，并且根据这个方向进行视觉图像的头脑风暴。

第四节课作业：在老师推荐的人像叙事摄影案例列表里，找出 20 张接近自己想表达的创作主题的摄影案例。同时，拍摄 3 张主题不限的人像摄影来保持摄影感觉。

第五节课学习目标：进行摄影作品策划

第五节课内容：在评讲作业后，老师与学生探讨这 20 张摄影案例与想表达主题的关系，帮助学生在审美与观念之间找到思想的桥梁，并进行主题的细化，梳理出为了表达这个主题可以有怎样的拍摄要点，学生需要在课上产出 3 个大致的摄影构图草图。

第五节课后作业：学生根据课上讨论的主题想法，联系愿意被拍摄的模特，自己自由组织道具，
进行 20 张以上的摄影尝试。

第六节课学习目标：进行摄影作品集的技术深化、图像元素扩充

第六节课内容：老师根据学生目前的作业，来教给学生如何充分利用道具、光影、虚化、环境等元素来表达学生原本构想的主题。

第五节课后作业：学生根据课上讲解的内容，做出一个摄影组图策划案，包含草图、道具、光影、色彩、线条、想表达的含义等，在老师审核过后，可以进行拍摄，拍摄数量不少于 50 张，所有的照片都可以交给老师，不需要删除自己不满意的照片。

第七节课学习目标：进行作品的最终优化。

第七节课内容：老师根据学生目前作业的优缺点，讲解相关的审美案例，来辅导学生进行第三轮拍摄的策划。

第七节课后的作业：完成第三轮拍摄，拍摄数量不少于 50 张

第八节课学习目标：进行作品挑选与作品描述

第八节课内容：进行作品的最终挑选，辅导作品描述。

第八节课后的作业：完成作品描述，老师会用 1NCT 进行批改。

# English Version

## LEVEL 1: For Students with No Prior Photography Experience (Outcome: A series of at least 5 photographs with accompanying descriptions)

**Pre-Class Assignment for the First Lesson:**

* Watch approximately one hour of introductory videos on basic camera parameters (e.g., aperture, shutter speed, ISO, white balance). The instructor will provide the video links. If you already understand the meaning of each camera parameter, you may skip this or watch selectively.

* If you have any past photography work, you may send it to the instructor.

* Inform the instructor of the model of your photography equipment (camera or phone). The instructor will research its photographic capabilities to provide personalized instruction during the class.

**Lesson 1: Learning Objective:** To cultivate an initial aesthetic sense in photography.

* **Content:** By analyzing the works of masters such as Henri Cartier-Bresson, Saul Leiter, and Vivian Maier, students will learn about the aesthetic of lines in photography and creative positioning of the main subject, thereby developing a fundamental compositional aesthetic.
* **Homework:** Shoot 20 photographs of any lines found in daily life to practice the
fundamentals of line aesthetics.

**Lesson 2: Learning Objective:** To develop an aesthetic appreciation for color in photography.

*   **Content:** After reviewing the homework, the instructor will use a series of works by master photographers, notable for their use of color, to engage in discussion and brainstorming with the student. This will be tailored to the strengths and weaknesses observed in the student's work, helping the student to grasp key learning points.

*   **Homework:** Focusing on "color aesthetics," shoot 20 photos of scenes in daily life that you find to have beautiful colors, in order to practice the fundamentals of color appreciation.

**Lesson 3: Learning Objective:** To learn how master photographers express narrative through portraits.

*   **Content:** After reviewing the homework, the instructor will select outstanding and creative portrait photography examples that are suitable for the student's learning style. This selection will be based on the characteristics, strengths, and weaknesses of the student's work. The instructor and student will engage in a discussion and brainstorming-style aesthetic analysis to help the student identify valuable techniques and receive technical guidance on photographic methods of interest.

*   **Homework:** Based on the aspect that most interested the student in class (e.g., the personality and expression of the subject, or the relationship between the subject and the environment), a semi-structured portrait photography assignment will be given. A minimum of 20 photos is required.

**Lesson 4: Learning Objective:** To conduct an initial brainstorming session for a photo project.

*   **Content:** After critiquing the homework, the instructor will present 1-2 cases of outstanding photographers whose work can help address issues from the recent assignment. Then, a brainstorming session will begin for the main creative theme of the photo project, identifying a direction the student wishes to pursue and brainstorming visual imagery related to this direction.

*   **Homework:** From a list of narrative portrait photography examples recommended by the instructor, find 20 images that are close to the creative theme you want to express. Additionally, shoot 3 portrait photos on any theme to maintain your photographic practice.

**Lesson 5: Learning Objective:** To plan the photography project.

*   **Content:** After critiquing the homework, the instructor and student will discuss the relationship between the 20 reference photos and the intended theme, helping the student build a conceptual bridge between aesthetics and ideas. The theme will be further refined, outlining key shooting points to express it. The student is required to produce 3 rough compositional sketches during class.

*   **Homework:** Based on the theme discussed in class, the student will contact a model, organize their own props, and conduct a test shoot of over 20 photographs.

**Lesson 6: Learning Objective:** To technically deepen and expand the visual elements of the photo series.
*   **Content:** Based on the student's current work, the instructor will teach how to fully utilize elements such as props, light and shadow, blur, and the environment to express the originally conceived theme.

*   **Homework:** Based on the content covered in class, the student will create a project plan for a photo series, including sketches, props, lighting, color, lines, and intended meaning. After the instructor reviews and approves the plan, the student can proceed with the shoot, taking no fewer than 50 photos. All photos, including those the student is not satisfied with, can be submitted to the instructor.

**Lesson 7: Learning Objective:** To conduct final optimization of the project.

*   **Content:** Based on the strengths and weaknesses of the student's current work, the instructor will explain relevant aesthetic examples to guide the student in planning a third round of shooting.

*   **Homework:** Complete the third round of shooting, with a minimum of 50 photos.

**Lesson 8: Learning Objective:** To select the final works and write the project description.

*   **Content:** Final selection of the photographs and guidance on writing the project description.

*   **Homework:** Complete the project description. The instructor will use 1NCT for revisions.

# LEVEL 2: For Students Proficient with Camera Parameters but Seeking to Improve Aesthetics (Outcome: A series of at least 10 edited photographs with an accompanying project description)

**Pre-Class Assignment for the First Lesson:**

*   Browse cnu.cc, find ten portrait photographs you like, and send the links to the instructor.

*   If you have any past photography work, you may send it to the instructor.

*   Inform the instructor of the model of your photography equipment (camera or phone). The instructor will research its photographic capabilities to provide personalized instruction during the class.

**Lesson 1: Learning Objective:** Aesthetic Enhancement Topic 1 (One topic will be chosen from: lines, color, subject simplicity, color blocking in the frame, or diverse shooting angles, based on the student's current work).

*   **Content:** Understand the student's preferences in portrait photography. Simultaneously, cultivate the student's aesthetic sense to address the primary issue in their current work.

*   **Homework:** Based on the Aesthetic Enhancement Topic 1 discussed in class, shoot over 20 photos on any subject.

**Lesson 2: Learning Objective:** Aesthetic Enhancement Topic 2 (A different topic from
Lesson 1 will be chosen from: lines, color, subject simplicity, color blocking in the frame, or diverse shooting angles, based on the student's current work).

*   **Content:** After reviewing the homework, continue to cultivate the student's aesthetic sense and resolve the secondary issue in their work.

*   **Homework:** Based on the Aesthetic Enhancement Topic 2 discussed in class, shoot over 20 photos on any subject.

**Lesson 3: Learning Objective:** To conduct an initial brainstorming session for a photo project.

*   **Content:** After critiquing the homework, a brainstorming session will begin for the main creative theme of the photo project, identifying a direction the student wishes to pursue and brainstorming visual imagery related to this direction.

*   **Homework:** From a list of narrative portrait photography examples recommended by the instructor, find 20 images that are close to the creative theme you want to express. In the assignment, you must specify what aspects of these images reflect your intended theme. Additionally, shoot 3 portrait photos on any theme (street or staged photography are both acceptable).

**Lesson 4: Learning Objective:** To discuss the project proposal.

*   **Content:** The instructor and student will jointly analyze how to draw inspiration from the 20 reference photos in terms of scene, props, light and shadow, color, composition, and imagery, so that these elements can be relevant to the student's shoot. The instructor will provide a preliminary introduction to props that can be used in photography.

*   **Homework:** Create a complete photography project proposal, including scene, props, light and shadow, color, composition, and imagery, with hand-drawn sketches.

**Lesson 5: Learning Objective:** To refine the project proposal.

*   **Content:** Discuss the feasibility and artistic merit of the proposal, provide technical guidance, and select relevant outstanding photography examples to enhance aesthetic understanding.

*   **Homework:** Contact a model, go to the corresponding location, and complete a shoot of no fewer than 50 photos.

**Lesson 6: Learning Objective:** Photo series optimization.

*   **Content:** Discuss the strengths and weaknesses of the shoot, explain them in detail, and use relevant examples to help the student find ways to optimize their shooting.

*   **Homework:** Conduct a second round of shooting, with no fewer than 50 photos.

**Lesson 7: Learning Objective:** Photo editing instruction.

*   **Content:** Confirm the final selection of works, provide basic photo editing guidance, and brainstorm ideas for the descriptions of outstanding photos.

*   **Homework:** Complete the editing for the final selected works.

**Lesson 8: Learning Objective:** Editing refinement, project description brainstorming, and basic portfolio layout instruction.

*   **Content:** Discuss directions for optimizing the edits and brainstorm an outline for the project description.
*   **Homework:** Complete the photo editing and project description. The instructor will use 1NCT to revise the project description.

# LEVEL 3: For Students with an Excellent Foundation in Photography (Outcome: A portfolio including a series of edited photographs and a project description, with no fewer than 15 works)

**Pre-Class Assignment for the First Lesson:**

*   Browse cnu.cc, find ten portrait photography series you like, and send the links to the instructor.

*   If you have any past photography work, you may send it to the instructor.

*   Inform the instructor of the model of your photography equipment (camera or phone). The instructor will research its photographic capabilities to provide personalized instruction during the class.

**Lesson 1: Content:**

1.  **Brainstorming Personal Themes:** This session will be structured around a framework:

    a. What are the keywords for issues you care about?

    b. What are some memorable events from your personal growth?

    c. Are there any emotions you strongly wish to express?

    d. What is your dream?

    e. What do you like most in life?

    f. What do you dislike? The instructor will extract potential themes from this brainstorming session for the student to choose from.

2.  Based on the brainstorming list, ask the student what kind of images come to mind.

3.  Review the portrait photography examples the student selected from cnu.cc to discuss the desired style, understand the basic theme the student wants to express, and brainstorm a direction for a staged narrative theme.

*   **Homework:** Based on the brainstorming results, undertake a free-form creative photography session of no fewer than 20 images.

**Lesson 2: Learning Objective:** Fundamental knowledge of creative staged photography.

*   **Content:** After critiquing the homework, the instructor will explain creative techniques involving props, blur, environment, and visual symbolism, as well as methods for collecting aesthetic materials and finding references.
*   **Homework:** From the portrait photography examples provided by the instructor,
find 20 reference works that can express the theme you want to create.

**Lesson 3: Learning Objective:** To discuss the project proposal.

* **Content:** The instructor and student will jointly analyze how to draw inspiration from the 20 reference photos in terms of scene, props, light and shadow, color, composition, and imagery, so that these elements can be relevant to the student's shoot. The instructor will provide a preliminary introduction to props that can be used in photography.

* **Homework:** Create a complete photography project proposal, including scene, props, light and shadow, color, composition, and imagery, with hand-drawn sketches.

**Lesson 4: Learning Objective:** To refine the project proposal.

* **Content:** Discuss the feasibility and artistic merit of the proposal, provide technical guidance, and select relevant outstanding photography examples to enhance aesthetic understanding.

* **Homework:** Contact a model, go to the corresponding location, and complete a shoot of no fewer than 50 photos.

**Lesson 5: Learning Objective:** Photo series optimization.

* **Content:** Discuss the strengths and weaknesses of the shoot, explain them in detail, and use relevant examples to help the student find ways to optimize their shooting.

* **Homework:** Conduct a second round of shooting, with no fewer than 50 photos.

**Lesson 6: Learning Objective:** Photo editing techniques.

* **Content:** Learn how different styles and emotional tones in editing affect the aesthetics and narrative of a photograph.

* **Homework:** Complete the first round of editing attempts.

**Lesson 7: Learning Objective:** Overall project optimization.

* **Content:** Critique the student's editing and provide technical explanations. Brainstorm the project description.

* **Homework:** The student will adjust the edits and complete the project description. The instructor will use one NCT session for revisions.

**Lesson 8: Learning Objective:** Learning to create a portfolio.

* **Content:** Confirm the final selection of works and explain how portfolio layout affects the narrative.

* **Homework:** Complete the portfolio.
