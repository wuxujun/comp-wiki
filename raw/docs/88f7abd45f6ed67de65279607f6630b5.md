# 历史人物剧本创作 | Historical Figure Playwriting
S&C

## Lesson 1 | Why Adapt Historical Figures? | 为什么要改编历史人物？

**学习目标 | Learning Objective :**

理解历史人物改编的意义，认识历史题材戏剧与影视作品的基本特点。

Understand the value of adapting historical figures and recognize key features of historical drama and film.

**学生任务 | Student Tasks :**

* 教师介绍历史人物改编的重要性与经典案例，如 *Hamilton*、*Les Misérables* 等

Introduction to the importance of historical adaptation and examples such as *Hamilton* and *Les Misérables*

* 观看 2–3 分钟相关片段，观察历史人物如何被戏剧化呈现

Watch a short clip and observe how a historical figure is dramatized

* 回答讨论：“Why do people still tell stories about historical figures today?”

Discuss: “Why do people still tell stories about historical figures today?”

* 完成一个简短写作练习：选择一个你感兴趣的历史人物，并写出原因（不少于 3 句）

Complete a short writing task: choose a historical figure you are interested in and explain why in at least 3 sentences

---

## Lesson 2 | How to Research a Historical Figure | 如何研究历史人物？

**学习目标 | Learning Objective :**

学习搜集和整理历史人物资料的方法，区分事实与戏剧化表达。

Learn how to research a historical figure and distinguish between fact and dramatic interpretation.

**学生任务 | Student Tasks :**

* 教师示范如何从文章、纪录片、访谈或图片中提取人物信息

Learn how to collect information from articles, documentaries, interviews, and visuals

* 整理人物基本信息：时代背景、身份、重要经历、冲突与转折

Organize key facts: time period, identity, major experiences, conflicts, and turning points

* 完成“人物研究表”

Complete a character research worksheet

* 课堂讨论：哪些信息最适合被写进剧本？

Discuss which facts are most useful for dramatic storytelling

---
# Lesson 3 | What Makes a Good Story? | 什么样的剧情才好看？

**学习目标 | Learning Objective：**

理解戏剧冲突和剧情推进的基本逻辑，学会从历史中寻找可戏剧化的内容。

Understand dramatic conflict and plot development, and learn how to find theatrical potential in history.

**学生任务 | Student Tasks：**

* 教师讲解“好看的剧情”通常包含哪些元素：目标、障碍、冲突、转折

Learn the key elements of a strong story: goal, obstacle, conflict, and turning point

* 分析一个历史人物故事中的戏剧冲突

Analyze the dramatic conflict in a historical figure’s story

* 头脑风暴：哪些历史事件最适合发展成舞台情节？

Brainstorm which historical events can become strong scenes

* 课后作业：选择一个你感兴趣的历史时段，并简要说明其吸引你的原因

Homework: choose a historical period you are interested in and explain why it stands out to you

# Lesson 4 | Starting the Script | 开始写剧本

**学习目标 | Learning Objective：**

了解剧本和小说的区别，学习如何在剧本中呈现和塑造历史人物。

Understand the difference between a script and a novel, and learn how to present a historical figure in dramatic form.

**学生任务 | Student Tasks：**

* 教师讲解剧本与小说在语言、结构和表达方式上的不同

Learn the difference between scriptwriting and prose writing

* 阅读简短剧本片段，观察人物如何通过台词和行动呈现

Read a short script excerpt and observe how character is revealed through dialogue and action

* 尝试把一段人物介绍改写成剧本场景

Rewrite a short character description as a dramatic scene

* 开始构思自己的主人公形象：他/她想要什么？面临什么问题？

Start shaping the main character: What does this person want? What problem do they face?

# Lesson 5 | Building the Outline | 形成剧本大纲

**学习目标 | Learning Objective：**

形成初步剧本大纲，理解人物发展与剧情发展的关系。

Create a script outline and understand the relationship between character development and plot development.
**学生任务 | Student Tasks :**

●教师讲解剧本大纲的基本组成：开端、发展、转折、结尾

Learn the basic structure of an outline: beginning, development, turning point, and ending

●梳理人物成长线与剧情推进线

Map both the character arc and the plot arc

●完成剧本大纲草稿

Draft a script outline

●课堂分享并获得教师反馈

Share the outline in class and receive feedback

---

# Lesson 6 | Revising the First Draft | 修改初稿：台词与潜台词

**学习目标 | Learning Objective :**

学习如何修改剧本初稿，理解台词与潜台词的区别和作用。

Learn how to revise a first draft and understand the function of dialogue and subtext.

**学生任务 | Student Tasks :**

●教师讲解什么是“台词”和“潜台词”

Learn the difference between dialogue and subtext

●分析示例片段：人物真正想表达的是什么？

Analyze a sample scene and identify what the character really means

●修改自己剧本中的一段对话，使其更自然、更有张力

Revise one dialogue section to improve tension and clarity

●根据教师反馈完善初稿内容

Revise the first draft based on feedback

---

# Lesson 7 | Writing the Final Draft | 撰写剧本终稿

**学习目标 | Learning Objective :**

完成剧本终稿的主体写作，增强结构完整性与人物表达。

Complete the main writing of the final draft and strengthen both structure and characterization.

**学生任务 | Student Tasks :**

●根据前几课的研究、剧情设计和修改建议继续完成剧本

Continue writing the script using previous research and revisions●检查场景之间的逻辑连接和节奏

Review scene flow, pacing, and coherence●完善人物语言风格与情感表达

Refine character voice and emotional expression●提交终稿初版供教师审阅

Submit a near-final version for instructor review

---
# Lesson 8 | Completing the Final Script | 完成剧本终稿

## 学习目标 | Learning Objective :

完成历史人物剧本终稿，并对整体内容进行整理与完善。

Complete the final historical figure script and polish the work as a whole.

## 学生任务 | Student Tasks :

* 根据反馈修改终稿中的结构、人物或语言问题
Revise structure, characterization, and language based on feedback

* 检查格式、场景标题、角色名称和台词书写是否规范
Check script format, scene headings, character names, and dialogue presentation

* 完成最终版本并提交
Complete and submit the final version

* 进行简单回顾：这位历史人物最值得被记住的是什么？
Reflect: What makes this historical figure worth remembering?

---

# NCT | Written Feedback and Revision | 终稿书面反馈与修改（非面授课）

## 学习目标 | Learning Objective :

对剧本终稿进行书面审阅、修改和润色，在教师反馈基础上提升结构完整性、人物塑造和语言表达。

Review, revise, and polish the final script based on written instructor feedback, with a focus on structure, characterization, and language.

## 学生任务 | Student Tasks :

* 使用“Revision Checklist”检查剧本的结构、人物逻辑、历史背景清晰度和语言准确度
Use a revision checklist to review structure, character logic, historical clarity, and language accuracy

* 根据反馈修改终稿：优化剧情连接、强化人物冲突、润色台词表达
Revise the script based on feedback: improve plot flow, strengthen character conflict, and polish dialogue

* 提交修改后的终稿，接受教师进一步书面反馈
Submit the revised final draft for additional written feedback
