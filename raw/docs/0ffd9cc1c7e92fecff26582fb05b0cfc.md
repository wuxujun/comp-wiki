# paw prints icon Short Pet Diaries Writing Pre-Meeting Writing Task

## “A Day My Pet Changed Something”

**Length:** 150–250 words
**Time:** 20–30 minutes
**Write in English only.**

---

### writing hand emoji Prompt:

Write a short story about one day when your pet (or a pet you imagine) changed something important.

It could change:

* Your mood
* A friendship
* A mistake you made
* A family moment
* Or something unexpected

Please include:

1. A clear beginning (Where are you? What is happening?)

2. At least 2 sentences that show the pet’s thoughts or feelings

3. At least 2 lines of dialogue

4. An ending that shows what changed

---

## star icon Optional Challenge (If You Want to Try)

You do not have to do this — but if you’d like to challenge yourself:

* Add 1 short paragraph from the pet’s point of view
* Include at least 3 sensory details (what the pet sees, hears, smells, or feels)
* Use at least 2 strong verbs instead of simple words like “went,” “said,” or “made”
* Add 1 sentence of reflection at the end (What did you learn?)
