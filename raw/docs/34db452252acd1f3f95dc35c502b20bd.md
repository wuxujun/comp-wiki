# 《城市探险奥德赛：探索纽约市、纽约上州与长岛》

Urban Odyssey: Exploring NYC, Upstate New York, and Long Island

**AA Instructor 指导老师:**

Erin Hunt

**Project Summary 项目简介:**

In this fully online 1-on-1 project, students embark on a journey to explore New York City and Upstate New York through a combination of research, creative writing, and cultural investigation. Students will select locations from curated “Adventure Menus” spanning neighborhoods, streets, landmarks, natural retreats, events, and hidden gems. They will engage in guided observation, multimedia research, and descriptive and narrative writing to produce detailed travel guidebook entries or creative “choose your own adventure” stories. Through weekly homework and structured lessons, students will synthesize facts, personal reflections, and historical/cultural context into polished, multi-page projects.
该项目为线上 1 对 1 远程形式，学生将通过研究、创意写作和文化探索踏上探索纽约市及上州 的旅程。学生将从精心设计的“探险菜单”中选择地点，包括社区、街道、地标、自然景观、活 动及隐藏宝地。他们将通过指导性观察、多媒体研究以及描述性与叙事性写作，创作详细的旅 行指南条目或创意“自行选择冒险”故事。通过每周作业和结构化课程，学生将整合事实、个人 反思以及历史/文化背景，形成完整的多页项目作品。

**Parent Introduction 家长说明:**

This project immerses students in the history, culture, and landmarks of NYC and Upstate New York, fostering skills in research, critical thinking, and creative writing. By combining exploration, multimedia resources, and reflective writing, students enhance observation, analytical, and narrative skills while learning to organize and communicate information effectively. The project encourages independent learning, cultural curiosity, and the ability to synthesize personal insights with factual research. It culminates in a polished travel guidebook or creative story that demonstrates both content mastery and storytelling ability.
本项目让学生深入了解纽约市及上州的历史、文化和地标，培养研究能力、批判性思维和创 意写作技能。通过探索、多媒体资源和反思性写作，学生将提升观察力、分析能力和叙事能力， 同时学习有效组织与表达信息。本项目鼓励独立学习、文化好奇心，并培养将个人见解与事实 研究结合的能力，最终完成一份精致的旅行指南或创意故事，展示内容掌握与叙事能力。
**Target Age Grade 适合年级:**

Grades 5–10
5–10 年级

**Subjects Integrated 跨学科整合:**

History / Geography + Cultural Studies / Social-Emotional Learning + English Language Arts / Creative Writing + Research & Personal Reflection
历史 / 地理 + 文化研究 / 社交情绪学习 + 英语语言艺术 / 创意写作 + 研究与个人反思

**Final Products 项目成果:**

* A digital travel guidebook or multi-page creative story with at least 5 detailed entries covering neighborhoods, streets, landmarks, natural sites, events, or hidden gems.
* A polished final project (digital or printable) including maps, photos, illustrations, and optional embedded multimedia.
* 一份数字版旅行指南或多页创意故事，包含至少 5 篇详尽条目，涵盖社区、街道、地标、自然景点、活动或隐藏宝地。
* 精致的最终作品（数字或可打印版），包含地图、照片、插图及可选多媒体内容。

**English Learning Outcomes 英语学习关联:**

* Develop advanced descriptive and narrative writing skills for travel and cultural storytelling.
* Conduct independent research and integrate factual and historical information into written work.
* Practice comparative, persuasive, and reflective writing structures.
* Expand vocabulary related to geography, history, culture, tourism, and personal expression.
* Enhance oral presentation skills through sharing and discussing favorite discoveries.

* 培养用于旅行和文化叙事的高级描述性和叙事性写作能力。
* 独立进行研究，并将事实与历史信息融入写作。
* 练习比较性、说服性及反思性写作结构。
* 扩充与地理、历史、文化、旅游及个人表达相关的词汇量。
* 通过分享与讨论最喜欢的探索内容，提高口语表达能力。

**English Deliverables 英文产出:**

* Structured travel guidebook or creative story entries (5 sections, 250–450 words each depending on grade) with research, personal reflection, and historical/cultural context.
● Optional illustrations, photos, maps, or multimedia elements embedded in the digital project.

● Individual entries revised based on teacher feedback to demonstrate clarity, descriptive precision, and storytelling skill.

● 结构化旅行指南或创意故事条目（5 个部分，每篇 250–450 字，根据年级水平），包含研究内容、个人反思及历史/文化背景。

● 数字项目中可嵌入插图、照片、地图或多媒体元素。

● 基于教师反馈修改后的各条目，展示清晰度、描述精准性和叙事能力。
