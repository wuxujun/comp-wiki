# The Art of Persuasion: Argumentative Writing Made Clear

## 说服的艺术：掌握议论文写作

**AA Instructor 指导老师:**

Zhang Zhaofei 张肇飞

## Project Summary 项目简介:

This course is a structured, skills-based exploration of argumentative writing designed to help students learn how to express opinions clearly, support them with strong reasons, and persuade readers using evidence and logic. Through step-by-step instruction, students will learn how to develop debatable claims, organize reasons, collect evidence, and apply persuasive strategies such as logic, credibility, and emotional appeal.

Lessons are built around real-world and student-relevant topics (such as school rules, technology use, mental health, and social issues), allowing students to practice argumentation in meaningful contexts. Each class focuses on a core argumentative skill, followed by targeted homework assignments that reinforce learning through short writing tasks, analysis, or reflection. By the end of the course, students will not only write stronger arguments but also think more critically and communicate more effectively.

本课程是一门以技能为核心、循序渐进的议论文写作课程，旨在帮助学生学会清晰表达观点、用有力理由支撑立场，并通过证据与逻辑说服读者。学生将系统学习如何提出可辩论的主张（claim）、展开多角度理由、收集与分析证据，并运用逻辑性、可信度和情感诉求等说服策略。课程围绕贴近学生生活的真实议题（如校园规则、科技使用、心理健康与社会问题等）展开，让学生在有意义的语境中练习论证写作。每一节课聚焦一个核心写作技能，并配有相应的课后作业，通过短写作、分析或反思巩固所学。课程结束时，学生不仅能写出更有说服力的文章，也将具备更成熟的批判性思维与表达能力。

## Parent Introduction 家长说明:

This course helps students move beyond simply stating opinions and teaches them how to think carefully, reason logically, and communicate persuasively. By learning how to structure arguments, evaluate evidence, and consider multiple perspectives, students develop critical thinking skills that are essential for academic success across subjects.

In addition to writing improvement, the course strengthens students' ability to analyze information, question assumptions, and express ideas confidently and respectfully in a formal writing style. These skills are highly
transferable to school assignments, writing section of standardized tests like TOFEL & IG EFL, debates, presentations, and future academic writing projects and contests. Regular homework after each class ensures steady practice and visible progress, while also encouraging independent thinking and responsibility.

本课程帮助学生从“表达个人观点”进阶到“有逻辑、有证据地说服他人”，培养严谨思考与清晰表达的能力。通过学习论证结构、证据分析和多角度思考，学生将掌握对学业发展至关重要的批判性思维技能。

除写作能力提升外，课程还强化学生分析信息、质疑假设以及自信而理性地用学术写作风格表达观点的能力。这些能力可广泛应用于学校写作任务、标化考试的写作模块、课堂讨论、演讲展示及未来的学术写作项目和竞赛中。每节课后的针对性作业确保学生持续练习、稳步进步，同时培养独立思考与学习责任感。

## Final Products 项目成果:

# English Learning Outcomes 英语学习关联:

Develop the ability to write clear, specific, and debatable claims.
Learn to organize arguments logically using reasons and sub-points.
Analyze and evaluate different types of evidence, including data, facts, logic, and personal experience.
Practice addressing counterarguments to strengthen overall persuasion.
Build academic vocabulary related to argumentation, logic, and social issues.
Gain confidence in expressing opinions clearly and respectfully in written English.
培养提出清晰、具体且可辩论主张的能力。
学会用逻辑结构组织论点与分论点。
分析与评估不同类型的证据，包括数据、事实、逻辑推理和个人经历。
练习回应反方观点，增强论证深度与公平性。
扩充与论证、逻辑和社会议题相关的学术词汇。
增强用英文清晰、理性表达观点的自信心。

# English Deliverables 英文产出:

Weekly homework assignments after each class, including short paragraphs, claim–reason–evidence exercises, evidence analysis, counterargument practice, or reflection tasks (length adjusted by grade level).
One final argumentative essay integrating skills learned throughout the course (e.g., 800–1500 words depending on grade level).
每节课后的英文书面作业，包括短段落写作、主张—理由—证据练习、证据分析、反驳练习或反思任务（字数根据年级调整）。
一篇期末综合议论文，整合课程中学习的全部论证技能（例如根据年级水平 800–1500 字）。
