# 自然密码：艺术与野性中的分型
**Nature's Code: Fractals in Art & the Wild**

<mark>7课时/阶段 7 sessions/level</mark>

## 项目简介 Project Overview

数学是宇宙写给人类的“情书”，而这个项目就是教你如何破译宇宙的密码！项目从一个有趣的问题（“一片雪花能绕地球一周吗？”）出发，核心在于教授学生：如何将自然界中复杂的、看似随机的形态（如海岸线、树枝）转化为严谨的数学公式，进行定量分析。

本项目共7课时，其中6课时为授课环节，1课时为非授课环节，用于项目总结与成果指导。

这是一个高度跨学科的项目，让孩子从根本上理解数学在日常生活中的应用价值。

Mathematics is the "love letter" written by the universe to humankind—and this project teaches students how to decode it. Starting from a curious question ("Can a snowflake circle the Earth?"), students will explore how to transform complex and seemingly random natural patterns—like coastlines or tree branches—into precise mathematical formulas for quantitative analysis.

The program consists of 7 sessions in total — 6 client-facing sessions for instruction and inquiry, and 1 non-client-facing session dedicated to project consolidation and instructor feedback.

This highly interdisciplinary program helps students truly understand the practical value of mathematics in everyday life.

## 课程模块 Syllabus

### Level 1  TOEFL 60+

课程核心：发现和描述自然界的重复图案，理解自相似和迭代的直观概念。
数学技能侧重：观察、比例、简单数列的记录与分析。

**Course Focus:** Discover and describe repeating patterns in nature; understand intuitive ideas of self-similarity and iteration.
**Math Focus:** Observation, ratios, and analysis of simple numerical sequences.

### Level 2  TOEFL 80+

课程核心：系统性地分析分形属性，用代数语言描述迭代规律，并概念性理解分形悖论。
数学技能侧重：数列的代数表达式、指数运算、几何级数（概念）、分形维度（概念）。

**Course Focus:** Analyze fractal properties systematically, describe iteration patterns using algebraic expressions, and conceptually grasp the fractal paradox.
**Math Focus:** Algebraic representation of sequences, exponential operations, geometric series (concepts), fractal dimension (concepts).

### Level 3  TOEFL 100+

课程核心：严谨地证明分形悖论，进行高级定量分析，并将分形理论应用于复杂的科学建模。
数学技能侧重：几何级数求和、极限、对数、分形维度公式计算。

**Course Focus:** Rigorously prove the fractal paradox, conduct advanced quantitative analysis, and apply fractal theory to complex scientific modeling.
**Math Focus:** Summation of geometric series, limits, logarithms, and fractal dimension calculation.
