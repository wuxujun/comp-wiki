# 改变人心的历史之歌：音乐与历史的交汇
**Songs That Shaped History: Music and Historical Change**

<mark>6课时(CT) + 1课时(NCT)</mark>

Musical notes and records illustration

## 课程概述 Overview

本课程将带领学生从音乐出发，走进不同时代的重要历史事件，探索歌曲如何记录时代情绪、传播社会理念、鼓舞群体行动，并在历史进程中发挥独特作用。学生将通过音乐分析、历史研究、文本解读与项目展示，完成属于自己的音乐 × 历史跨学科项目。

This course invites students to explore major historical events through music. Students will examine how songs record public emotions, spread social ideas, inspire collective action, and play unique roles in shaping historical moments. Through musical analysis, historical research, textual interpretation, and project presentation, students will complete their own interdisciplinary music-and-history project.

## 适合学员 Suitable for Students

* 对音乐、历史或人文社科感兴趣的学生
* 未来计划申请音乐、人文、历史、传媒或文化研究方向的学生
* 希望培养跨学科研究、文本分析与表达能力的学生
* Students interested in music, history, or the humanities
* Students considering future applications in music, humanities, history, media, or cultural studies
* Students eager to build skills in interdisciplinary research, analysis, and presentation

## 课程模块 Syllabus

### 01 历史中的音乐与社会表达 2课时
**Music as Historical Expression** 2 lessons

介绍音乐在历史中的不同角色，如战争时期的鼓舞歌曲、社会运动中的抗议歌曲以及民族认同中的文化音乐。学生将学习如何从历史背景、歌词内容、音乐风格与传播方式等角度分析歌曲，并理解音乐如何成为时代声音。

Introduce the roles of music in history, such as wartime songs, protest songs in social movements, and music tied to national identity. Students learn to analyze songs through historical context, lyrics, musical style, and circulation.

### 02 个性化主题研究 2课时
**Personalized Research** 2 lessons

学生根据兴趣选择一个历史时期、社会运动或代表性歌曲，围绕“音乐如何反映并影响历史”展开研究。研究方法包括文献阅读、歌词分析、音乐案例比较与展示设计等。

Students will choose a historical moment, social movement, or representative song to investigate how music reflects and influences history.

### 03 成果展示与反馈 2课时
**Presentation and Feedback** 2 lessons

学生整理研究成果，以研究报告或多媒体展示形式进行发表。通过展示与讨论，提升历史分析、跨学科整合与学术表达能力。
Students present their research through reports or multimedia presentations and receive feedback.

## 课程亮点 Course Highlights

### 音乐 × 历史双线探索
**Music and History in Dialogue**

* 通过代表性歌曲理解重要历史时期，如战争、社会运动、身份认同与文化变迁
Explore major historical moments—war, social movements, identity formation, and cultural change—through music.

* 从音乐切入历史，让学生看到：历史不仅写在书里，也唱在歌里
From music to history, allowing students to see that history is not only written in books but also sung in songs.

### 学术分析与现实表达并重
**Academic Analysis with Real-World Relevance**

* 学习基础音乐分析方法：旋律、节奏、风格、歌词与情感表达
Learn musical elements (melody, rhythm, texture, form).

* 建立历史分析框架，理解音乐与政治、社会、文化之间的关系
Learn historical analysis methods to connect music with social and political contexts.

### 项目式跨学科研究
**Interdisciplinary Project-Based Research**

* 融合音乐学 + 历史 + 文化研究，建立更完整的人文视角
Combine musicology, history, and cultural studies to develop a broader global perspective.

* 结业成果可延伸为研究论文、展示项目、竞赛选题或后续背景提升项目
Final projects can evolve into research papers, presentation projects, competition topics, or subsequent background enhancement programs.

## 可研究主题示例 Sample Research Topics

* 战争时期的音乐如何鼓舞公众情绪
How music inspired the public during wartime

* 抗议歌曲如何推动社会运动
How protest songs supported social movements

* 不同时代的流行音乐如何反映青年文化与社会价值
How popular music reflects youth culture and social values across time

* 音乐如何塑造民族认同与集体记忆
How music shapes national identity and collective memory
