# 《未来漫游者：数字生活与心理探索》
Future Wanderers: Digital Life & Psychological Exploration

## AA Instructor 指导老师：

Mengqi Du

## Original PBL Topic 原始题目：

数字游民：新型生活方式
Digital Nomads: A New Lifestyle

## Project Summary 项目简介：

In this fully online 1-on-1 or small group PBL project, students will explore what it means to live freely, move flexibly, and stay emotionally strong in a rapidly changing digital world. By stepping into the shoes of a "Future Wanderer"—a person who blends global exploration with digital connectivity—they will reflect on their own desires for independence, flexibility, and belonging. Students will study key psychological traits of adaptability, self-motivation, and emotional resilience, and simulate future challenges such as facing uncertainty, starting over in new environments, or navigating virtual relationships. Through creative writing, interactive discussions, and practical tool design, they will build a future lifestyle vision and a personalized mental adaptability toolkit.

该项目以线上 1 对 1 的远程形式开展，引导学生从青少年视角出发，模拟“未来漫游者”这一概念人物——一个结合数字连接与全球探索的生活方式。学生将反思自己对独立性、自由与归属感的渴望，学习关键的心理素质（如适应力、自我驱动、情绪韧性），并设想可能面对的未来挑战（如搬家、孤独感、不确定性）。他们将通过创意写作、互动对话和实用工具设计，完成未来生活构想与一套适应力工具箱的产出。

## Parent Introduction 家长说明：

This project empowers students to reflect deeply on their personal values, motivations, and goals for the future. While imagining possible lifestyles shaped by digital tools and global mobility, they will explore key psychological traits that support mental well-being in an uncertain world. Through guided English learning, self-expression, and cross-cultural thinking, your child will strengthen emotional intelligence, resilience, and creative communication—essential skills for navigating the future.

本项目将帮助学生深入思考自己的价值观、内在动机与未来方向。在构想数字化、全球流动背景下的未来生活时，学生将探索有助于心理健康的核心心理素质，如适应力、自主性和情绪管理能力。通过引导式英文表达与跨文化思维的训练，孩子将在项目中发展情绪智力、心理韧性与创造性沟通能力，为未来做好准备。
## Target Age Grade 适合年级：

Grades 5–10（建议按英语能力分组）
Grades 5–10 (grouped by English proficiency)

## Subjects Integrated 跨学科整合：

心理学 + 英语语言艺术 + 社会学 + 创意写作 + 全球文化 + 个人发展

Psychology + English Language Arts + Sociology + Creative Writing + Global Culture + Personal Development

## Final Products 项目成果：

* "My Future Wanderer" Psychological Profile & Lifestyle Vision

一份未来自我心理画像与生活构想：学生将构建一个虚拟未来身份，包括生活方式设想、心理特征、自我价值观和动机分析。

* "Adaptability Toolkit for the Future"

一套实用“适应力工具箱”：为未来可能遇到的心理挑战（如孤独、环境变动、跨文化交际）设计的英文策略指南，可采用图解、流程卡、建议清单、情绪支持卡片等形式呈现。

## English Learning Outcomes 英语学习关联：

* Build English vocabulary related to emotions, personality traits, and digital life.

* Learn to describe internal motivations, psychological states, and future goals clearly in English.

* Develop narrative and reflective writing skills through journaling and scenario simulations.

* Improve critical thinking and problem-solving communication in group discussions.

* Practice using English to express emotional needs, personal strategies, and future identity.

* 扩充与情绪、人格特征和数字生活相关的英语词汇；

* 学会用英文清晰表达内在动机、心理状态和未来目标；

* 通过写作模拟场景与日志反思，提升叙事与反思性写作能力；

* 在小组讨论中锻炼批判性思维和解决问题的表达能力；

* 练习用英文表达自我情绪需求、应对策略与未来身份设想。

## English Deliverables 英文产出：

* A 800–1500 word digital profile & lifestyle essay (or narrated PPT) titled: "**My Future Wanderer: Who I Might Become and How I'll Thrive**"

* 一篇 800–1500 字英文未来生活画像与设想（或配音 PPT），标题为“我的未来漫游者：我可能成为谁以及我将如何茁壮成长”
可能成为谁，我将如何成长”。

● A 500–1000 word written toolkit in English titled: **“Adaptability Toolkit for a Changing World”**, including strategies, visual tools, and tips for mental well-being.

● 一份 500–1000 字英文“适应力工具箱”，包括具体策略、视觉工具和情绪调节建议，支持学生未来心理健康发展。
