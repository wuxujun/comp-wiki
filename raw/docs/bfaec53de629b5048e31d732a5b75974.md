# Urban Odyssey: Exploring NYC & Long Island

纽约探险：探索纽约市与长岛

## How This Works

## 课程运作方式

* Choice + structure: Students always choose but within presets that match skill level and scope.

* 选择与结构：学生总是有选择，但选择内容基于预设，符合他们的技能水平和学习范围。

* Writing grows progressively in length, depth, and format—from simple listings to polished articles.

* 写作会逐步提升长度、深度和形式——从简单列表到精炼文章。
* Teacher scaffolds key vocabulary, reading passages, visual prompts, and clear writing frames each class.

* 教师在每节课中搭建核心词汇、阅读材料、视觉提示和清晰的写作框架。
* Personalization increases agency and excitement while ensuring educational outcomes.

## Adventure Menu (Choose 5 to Explore):

### 探险菜单（选择 5 个地点进行探索）：

* NYC: Times Square, Empire State Building, Yankee Stadium, Rockefeller Center, Central Park

* 纽约市：时代广场、帝国大厦、洋基球场、洛克菲勒中心、中央公园

## Lesson Breakdown (45 mins):

### 课程环节安排（45 分钟）：

* Warm-up & Intro (7 mins): Discuss students’ prior knowledge and places they want to visit; show photos.

* 热身与导入（7 分钟）：讨论学生已有的知识以及他们想参观的地方；展示图片。 ● Location Exploration (20 mins): Teacher presents 5 locations with photos, history, fun facts, and vocabulary. Encourage questions and discussion.

* 地点探索（20 分钟）：教师展示 5 个地点的照片、历史、趣闻及相关词汇。鼓励提问和讨论。 ● Vocabulary & Sentence Practice (10 mins): Introduce and practice key vocabulary linked to locations. Model simple descriptive sentences, student repeats and practices orally.

* 词汇与句型练习（10 分钟）：介绍并练习与地点相关的核心词汇。示范简单描述句型，学生口头跟读并练习。 ● Wrap-up & Homework Instructions (8 mins): Explain homework assignment.

* 总结与作业讲解（8 分钟）：说明作业要求。
