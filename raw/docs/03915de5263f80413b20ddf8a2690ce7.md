# 旋律时光机：
# 我的音乐故事播客
# Melodies and Memories: My Story Through Music Podcast

<mark>6课时(CT) + 1课时(NCT)</mark>

Illustration of a retro radio with musical notes

##      项目简介 Project Summary

本项目将引导学生围绕一段与音乐相关的情绪记忆，构思并完成一期播客节目。他们将在老师的引导下进行旋律联想、情绪词汇积累、故事结构构建等练习，并最终用英文完成一篇播客脚本，并配音制作播客音频。课程将通过“音乐触发记忆——情绪表达——播客讲述”这一学习路径，融合听觉审美与语言表达，帮助学生实现从生活体验到英文表达的跨越。

This project guides students to reflect on an emotional memory connected to music and produce a podcast episode based on that moment. Through guided exercises on melody association, emotional vocabulary building, and storytelling structure, students will draft an English podcast script and record an audio version. The learning pathway—Music evokes memory → Emotion expression → Podcast storytelling—combines auditory appreciation with English language production, bridging real-life experience and narrative expression.

<mark>A Message to Parents</mark>

该项目不仅提升了孩子的英语表达能力，更激发了他们对情绪与记忆的觉察力。在项目中，孩子将学会用英文讲述真实的自己，锻炼情感表达力、语言组织力和公众表达信心，并通过播客这一形式获得创造与成就感。

This project enhances students' English language skills while deepening their emotional awareness and self-reflection. By narrating personal stories in English, students build their emotional expression, narrative structure, and public speaking confidence. The podcast format also fosters creativity and a sense of accomplishment.
