# 《青少年新闻行动：用采访写出时代观察》

**Teen Journalism Project: Reporting Our World**

**AA Instructor 指导老师:**

Sara M.

**Project Summary 项目简介:**

Students explore youth-centered issues of education and teen health, conduct peer interviews, and write 500–750 word English news articles with clarity and accuracy.
学生将针对教育、青少年健康等年轻人关注的话题进行采访和写作，撰写 500-750 字的英文新闻稿，提高事实表达能力和媒体素养。

**Parent Introduction 家长说明:**

This project empowers students to engage with real-world issues and practice thoughtful journalism. It enhances research, interviewing, and public communication skills.
本项目鼓励学生通过真实议题锻炼研究与表达能力，提升公共写作的社会责任感。

**Target Age Grade 适合年级:**

Grades 6–10
6–10 年级

**Subjects Integrated 跨学科整合:**

Journalism + Cultural Studies + Informational Writing
新闻写作 + 文化观察 + 信息表达

**Final Products 项目成果:**

Original News Article + Interview Transcript + Headline
英文新闻稿 + 采访摘要 + 标题设计稿

**English Learning Outcomes 英语学习关联:**

Develop interview skills, structure, headline writing, professional tone, and source-based reasoning in English nonfiction.
提高非虚构类英文写作的采访技巧、逻辑结构、客观语气和信息整合能力。
