# 《一周生存预算挑战：住在美国不同州要花多少钱？》

How Much to Survive? A One-Week Budget Challenge Across U.S. States

## AA Instructor 指导老师

徐含青

## Project Summary 项目简介

In this PBL project, students will role-play as teenagers planning to live in different U.S. states (e.g., Texas, California, New York, North Carolina). They will explore the cost of living in each state, including housing, food, transportation, and entertainment. By comparing budgets and lifestyles, they will create visual presentations and explain their ideal living location in English.

在本项目中，学生将模拟自己即将搬去美国某个州（如德州、加州、纽约、北卡等）生活。他们需要了解各州的生活成本，如住房、食品、交通和娱乐等，比较后制作可视化预算图，并用英文说明自己的选址理由。

## Parent Introduction 家长说明

This course helps students develop real-world awareness of budgeting and regional economic differences while integrating English communication, critical thinking, and data skills. Students will also gain exposure to basic economics and personal finance concepts relevant to their future education and daily life.

本课程帮助学生了解真实世界中的生活预算和地区经济差异，同时提升英文表达、批判性思维和数据分析能力。项目还引导学生初步接触经济与个人财务的基本概念，对未来学习与生活有现实指导意义。

## Target Age Grade 适合年级

G5–G10（教学内容与任务可按年级层级调整难度）

建议以 G6–G8 为主力段落，G9–G10 可加入更多经济学分析内容

## Subjects Integrated 跨学科整合

* Economics 经济学：成本结构、预算概念、生活支出对比

* Geography 地理：美国区域差异、气候与生活方式

* English 英语表达：生活类词汇、表达偏好、结构化演讲

* Data Visualization 数据可视化：预算图表、价格分析展示

## Final Products 项目成果

1. Budget Comparison Infographic – One-week cost of living chart
一周生活预算对比图表（电子版）

2. English Oral Presentation (Video) – “Where Would I Live and Why?”
英文演讲视频：我为什么选择这个州？

3. Vocabulary Flashcards – Key terms in budgeting and daily expenses
关键词词卡（如：utilities, groceries, rent, income tax 等）

## English Learning Outcomes 英语学习关联

* Build vocabulary related to American lifestyle and budgeting
掌握美国生活与预算相关词汇

* Practice using logical structures for preference and comparison
运用逻辑句型表达选择与比较理由

* Develop presentation and speaking confidence in English
训练英文演讲与表达自信

## English Deliverables 英文产出

1. PPT (in English) – Comparing living costs across states
英文 PPT：各州生活成本对比

2. Speech Script (in English) – Explaining the choice of location
英文演讲稿：解释选址原因

3. Vocabulary Set – 10–15 key budgeting words
预算词汇学习小词卡
