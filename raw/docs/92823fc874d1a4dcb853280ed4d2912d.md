# 041026 Personal Storytelling PBL

**Session 1 - Project Introduction**

* Introduces the class and project format

* Identifies key features of narrative: a narrative arc, a motivation, reflection, causality

* Identifies features of a strong topic: specific, personal, interesting

* Discusses how the two example stories exemplify successful narrative structures and interesting topics

* Complete a topic brainstorming activity to generate topic ideas

**Student task:**

* Complete a topic brainstorming worksheet to generate at least 3 topic ideas.

**Session 2 - Topic Brainstorming**

* Participate in a brainstorming discussion to choose and develop a story topic

* Introduce types of external and internal conflicts and types of narrative change, using examples from the sample stories, and applying these concepts to the student's topic

* Introduce the idea of a 1-sentence summary to sum up the central narrative of a story

**Student task:**

* Complete a brainstorming freewrite and write a 1-sentence summary

**Session 3 - Introductions**

* Introduce the characteristics of a strong introduction and different types of introductions

* Analyize example introductions from real stories

* Brainstorm ideas for student's introduction

**Student task:**

* Draft 1-paragraph introduction
**Session 4 - Structure and Outline**

* Introduce key narrative terms: inciting incident, climax, resolution

* introduce types of narrative structures, including chronological, braided, flashback, opening cliffhanger, and callback

* Analyze the narrative beats of the sample stories

* Introduce the 'narrative stepping stone' format of the outline

**Student task:**

* Draft outline

**Session 5 - Outline Workshop**

* Introduce key terms: scene, summary, reflection

* Understand the perfect of illustrative details and reflection

* Receive feedback on outline

* Practice performing a short part of the story

**Student task**

* Revise outline according to feedback

**Session 6 - Conclusions**

* Introduce the characteristics of a successful conclusion

* Analyze example conclusions from real stories

* Brainstorm ideas for student's conclusion

**Student task**

* Draft conclusion paragraph

**Session 7 - Revision Workshop**

* Introduce narrative concept: show vs tell

* Introduce and complete revision strategies

* Practice performing story

**Student task:**

* Revise full draft and practice performing it all the way through
**Session 8 - Performance Workshop**

* Introduce performance elements, like pauses, emphasis, repetition, asides, and variance in tone

* Discuss performance styles of the sample stories

* Receive feedabck on story performance

**Student task:**

* Record practice performance

**NCT**

Deliverable: feedback on student performance

Student's final project: podcast-style reporting of the student story
