# 《莎士比亚：一位角色的现代重生》

*Rewriting Shakespeare: Bringing a Character to Life Online*

**AA Instructor 指导老师:**

Sara Wu , Julie Zhang

**Project Summary 项目简介:**

In this fully online project, students explore one Shakespeare character and bring them into the modern world. They will select monologues, analyze tone and emotion, translate selected lines into modern English, and create a personal “character file” with backstory, motivation, and emotional arc. They will then write a modern diary entry or reimagine a monologue, and optionally record a short performance to share with peers.

该项目为线上远程形式，学生将深入研究莎士比亚剧作中的一位角色，从台词分析其情绪与动机，再用现代英文进行改写与表达。他们将完成一份角色档案，撰写角色日记或改编独白，并可选择录制英文演绎视频，在线上平台进行展示。

**Parent Introduction 家长说明:**

This project helps students build deeper understanding of Shakespeare’s language and characters while developing their public speaking, empathy, and writing skills—all through digital tools. It transforms complex classical content into a fun, interactive, and expressive challenge suited for teens.

本项目通过线上形式让学生接触莎士比亚文学，结合角色演绎与现代表达，激发孩子对英文原著的兴趣，并提升语言分析、表达自信与表演能力。内容经典，形式灵活，适合青少年在家远程完成。

**Target Age Grade 适合年级:**

Grades 5–10

5–10 年级

**Subjects Integrated 跨学科整合:**

English Literature + Creative Writing + Theatre

英文文学 + 创意写作 + 戏剧表达（线上演绎）

**Final Products 项目成果:**
* Shakespeare character profile sheet (motivation, emotion chart, favorite quotes)

* Modernized monologue or diary entry

* Optional recorded performance (Zoom, Flipgrid, or Padlet)

* 莎士比亚角色档案（动机图、情绪线、台词赏析）

* 改写版独白（英文）

* 英文演绎视频录制

**English Learning Outcomes 英语学习关联:**

* Understand and interpret Shakespearean English through simplified analysis

* Learn to paraphrase complex literary language into modern English

* Practice expressive tone, character perspective, and dramatic voice in writing and speaking

* 掌握莎士比亚英文的语义与情绪表达

* 学习将经典句式翻译为现代英文并保留情感内核

* 在英文写作与口语中训练角色语调、第一人称表达与表演语感

**English Deliverables 英文产出:**

* Modern-English reinterpretation of one speech or diary-style writing

* English character summary or quote reflection

* Optional: English video monologue or “Meet the Character” introduction

* 一篇改写英文独白或角色日记

* 一份英文人物档案或台词解读

* 可选英文视频演绎或角色介绍
