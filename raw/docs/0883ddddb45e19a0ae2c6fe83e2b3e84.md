# PBL Project Name 项目名称

* 中文：歌声中的时代：一首改变人心的历史之歌
* English: Songs in Time: One Song That Moved a Generation

---

## 2. AA Instructor 学术指导老师

* 中文：吴海鹏
* English: Paul Wu

---

## 3. Original PBL Topic 原始英文题目

* 中文：音乐如何改变人心与时代
* English: How Music Can Move Hearts and Change History

---

## 4. Project Summary 项目简介

* 中文：

本项目将引导学生走进一首影响历史的歌曲，从音乐构成、歌词内容到社会文化背景，全面探索音乐的力量。学生将在老师指导下学习基础音乐词汇、分析旋律与情感、了解歌曲背后的故事与时代背景。通过比较不同风格的音乐（歌剧、摇滚、流行、电影音乐、音乐剧等），学生将理解歌曲如何传递情感与影响社会。最终，他们将制作一份英文创作成果，如**歌单解说音频或数字小册**，向观众介绍一首“改变时代”的歌曲。课程路径为：**认识歌曲 → 理解背景 → 文化解读 → 创作输出**，融合听觉欣赏与语言表达。

* English:

This project guides students to explore the power of a historically significant song by analyzing its music structure, lyrics, and cultural context. Under teacher guidance, students will learn essential music vocabulary, study melody and emotion, and uncover the story behind the song’s impact. By comparing different genres (opera, rock, pop, film scores, musicals, ect), students will understand how songs communicate emotion and influence society. The project culminates in an original English deliverable—such as a **playlist commentary audio or a digital booklet**—that presents “one song that moved a generation.” The learning pathway: **Song discovery → Context exploration → Cultural interpretation → Creative production** combines auditory appreciation and language expression.

---

## 5. Parent Introduction 家长说明

* “歌声中的时代”项目不仅提升孩子的英语能力，更拓展他们的文化视野。在学习过程中，孩子会接触不同音乐风格，理解一首歌如何承载故事与时代情感，并通过创作和展示获得自信与成就感。项目将帮助孩子提高英语听说读写能力、培养跨学科思维，并激发他们对艺术与历史的兴趣。
* The “Songs in Time” project enhances English skills while broadening cultural
horizons. Students explore diverse music genres and discover how songs carry stories and reflect their times. By creating and presenting their own work, students gain confidence and a sense of achievement. The project develops English listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills, fosters interdisciplinary thinking, and sparks interest in arts and history.

## 6. Target Age Grade 适合年级

* 适合 5–12 年级学生

* Suitable for students in Grades 5–12

## 7. Subjects Integrated 跨学科整合

* 音乐欣赏 + 历史文化 + 英语表达 + 创意写作/数字创作

* Music Appreciation + History & Culture + English Communication + Creative/Digital Writing

## 8. Final Products 项目成果

英文数字创作，内容包括：

* “改变时代的一首歌”英文解说稿

* **数字展示**（如歌单解说音频、互动小册或简短视频）

English creative output including:

* “One Song That Moved a Generation” commentary script

* **Digital showcase** (playlist commentary audio, interactive booklet, or short video)

## 9. English Learning Outcomes 英语学习关联

* 中文：学生将在项目中提升以下英语技能：

* 学习音乐相关核心词汇与表达

* 理解并总结历史与文化背景

* 撰写简短英文解说和评论

* 练习口语表达与音频讲解

* English:

* Students will develop the following English skills: Acquire key music-related vocabulary and expressions

* Understand and summarize historical and cultural context

* Write concise English commentary and analysis

* Practice oral delivery and audio narration
# 10. Deliverables 产出

项目中要求学生以英文完成以下成果：

* 解说稿件（文字）

* 音频/视频解说展示

* 英文标题与简短介绍文案

* Students are expected to produce the following in English: Commentary script (written)

* Audio or video presentation

* English title and tagline for their showcase
