# 《历史人物剧本创作》

# Historical Figure Playwriting: Bringing the Past to the Stage

**AA Instructor 指导老师:**

Sara Wu , Julie Zhang

---

**Project Summary 项目简介:**

In this project, students explore the life of a historical figure and transform real events into a dramatic script. They will research the character’s background, time period, motivations, and conflicts, then learn how to shape these materials into a compelling story for the stage. Through character analysis, plot building, dialogue writing, and revision, students will create an original script inspired by history. By the end of the project, they will complete a polished historical figure play script that combines factual research with dramatic imagination.

在本项目中，学生将选择一位历史人物，基于真实历史背景与人物经历，发展并创作一部原创剧本。学生将学习如何研究人物所处的时代、关键事件、核心矛盾与人物动机，并在此基础上理解如何将历史材料转化为具有戏剧张力的舞台故事。通过人物分析、剧情设计、剧本结构学习、台词写作与多轮修改，学生最终将完成一部兼具历史基础与戏剧表达的历史人物剧本作品。

---

**Parent Introduction 家长说明:**

This project helps students connect history, literature, and creative writing in a meaningful and engaging way. Instead of only memorizing historical facts, students learn to interpret the inner world of a real historical figure, understand the forces shaping their decisions, and reimagine their story through dramatic writing. The project strengthens research, analytical thinking, structured writing, and creative expression, while also helping students develop empathy, perspective-taking, and a deeper appreciation of how history can be told through art.

本项目将历史学习、文学理解与创意写作结合起来，让学生不只是“记住历史”，而是学会“理解历史中的人”。在创作过程中，学生需要深入思考历史人物所面临的时代环境、内在冲突与选择，从而将人物真实经历转化为有感染力的戏剧情节。这个过程不仅能提升学生的研究能力、逻辑分析能力和英文写作能力，也能培养他们的同理心、视角转换能力，以及用艺术方式表达复杂历史主题的能力。对于喜欢历史、文学、戏剧或写作的学生来说，这是一个兼具学术性和创造力的项目。

# Subjects Integrated 跨学科整合:

History + English Literature + Creative Writing + Theatre
历史 + 创意写作 + 戏剧表达

## Final Products 项目成果:

* Historical figure research sheet (background, time period, major events, motivations, conflicts)

* Script outline with character arc and plot arc

* Original historical play script

* Optional staged reading or script presentation

* 历史人物研究档案（时代背景、关键事件、人物动机、核心冲突）

* 剧本大纲（人物发展线与剧情发展线）

* 原创历史人物剧本终稿

## English Learning Outcomes 英语学习关联:

* Develop the ability to research and summarize historical information in English

* Learn to transform factual material into dramatic storytelling

* Practice scriptwriting skills such as scene structure, dialogue, and character voice

* Strengthen analytical reading, narrative organization, and revision skills through writing

* 学习用英文整理与表达历史人物资料

* 掌握如何将真实历史内容转化为戏剧情节

* 训练剧本写作能力，包括场景设置、人物语言和对话表达

* 在创作与修改过程中提升英文阅读分析、结构组织与写作表达能力

## English Deliverables 英文产出:

* English historical figure profile or research summary

* English script outline

* Original English play script based on a historical figure

* Optional: script reading recording or presentation reflection

* 一份英文历史人物研究档案

* 一份英文剧本大纲

* 一部基于历史人物创作的英文原创剧本
