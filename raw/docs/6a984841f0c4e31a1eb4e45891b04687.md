宾大沃顿商业领导力项目是由全美顶尖商学院--沃顿商学院主办的高含金量暑期课程，旨在帮助学生发掘自身的领导力，培养团队协作和沟通交流的能力。该项目是全美最顶尖的商科类夏校之一，含金量非常高!面向优秀且有实力的11年级学生，每年仅招收120人，竞争激烈!录取的学生不仅需要超高的学术成就，而且还要有出色的领导力。
Leadership in the Business World is a three-week immersive program that provides students with a strong foundation in business fundamentals and organizational strategy. Participants engage in team-based projects, simulations, and debates while learning to analyze internal and external business environments using essential management frameworks. The program culminates in a capstone case competition, allowing students to apply their skills in a real-world business scenario.

All participants who complete the program will earn a Wharton Global Youth Certificate of Completion.
