# 我最爱的科技产品变变变
Watch It Change:
My Favorite Tech

<mark>6课时(CT) + 1课时(NCT)</mark>

Illustration of technology evolution timeline

## 项目简介 Project Summary

在这个项目 中，学生将选择一个他们熟悉并喜爱的科技产品(例如手机、耳机、照相机、音乐播放器、电子游戏机等)，研究它从最早的样子到现在的变化过程，并大胆想象它未来可能的模样。通过查找资料、整理信息、搭建时间线，学生将用英文制作一份属于自己的“科技变化故事展”。

In this project, students will pick a tech item they love—like a phone, camera, music player, or game console—and trace how it has changed over time. Then, they'll create their own visual and written English showcase telling the story of this gadget's journey and imagining what's next.

<mark>**A Message to Parents**</mark>

这个项目鼓励孩子从熟悉和喜爱的事物出发，通过“我最爱的科技产品”来提升信息查找能力、英文表达力和数字内容创作能力。他们将在查资料、做图表、设计展示页的过程中积累实用英语表达，同时发展科技理解力与逻辑思维。本项目可完全远程操作，适合线上学习和展示。

This project encourages students to explore technology they already love. As they research, organize, and express ideas about their chosen gadget in English, they'll build vocabulary, timeline skills, and digital design confidence. The entire project can be completed remotely and shared online.
