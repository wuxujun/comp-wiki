PBL Gene Editing and In-depth Reading of Cutting-edge Papers-Biology

PBL Gene Editing and In-depth Reading of Cutting-edge Papers-Biology

原标题：《从基因突变到 CRISPR 治疗：以镰状细胞贫血为例，读懂一篇前沿生物医学论文》

From Genetic Mutation to CRISPR Therapy: Reading a Biomedical Research Paper through the Lens of Sickle Cell Disease

---

## ✅ 项目定位说明

### 项目切入点

以 **镰状细胞贫血（sickle cell disease）** 这一首个被发现的分子病为真实案例，从“一个单核苷酸突变（SNP）如何导致疾病”出发，引导学生理解基因突变、蛋白功能异常与疾病表型之间的关系，并进一步认识 CRISPR-Cas9 基因编辑技术如何被用于开发已获 FDA 批准的疗法。课程不要求死记技术细节，而是围绕 **“基因问题 → 疾病机制 → 治疗思路 → 科研证据”** 的逻辑链，帮助学生真正读懂一篇前沿生物医学论文。

### 学术顾问(AA)角色

AA 将帮助学生把《新英格兰医学杂志》（NEJM）等高水平论文拆解为学生可理解的部分：疾病背景、研究问题、CRISPR 治疗策略、关键实验图表、研究结论与局限性。技术概念（PCR、Western blot、测序、细胞模型）只作为“读懂图表工具”进行讲解，重点训练 **学术文献阅读、专业文献检索、图表解读、科研逻辑思维和英文学术表达**。

### Project Summary 项目简介（中英）

本项目以 **镰状细胞贫血（sickle cell disease）** 为真实教学案例。学生将首先理解：一个单核苷酸突变如何导致血红蛋白结构异常，进而引发红细胞镰变、贫血和多器官损伤。在此基础上，认识 CRISPR-Cas9 如何作为一种基因编辑工具，被用于修复或调控疾病相关基因——该疗法已于 2023 年获得 FDA 批准。

课程不会单独讲解 PCR、Western blot、测序等技术，而是将其作为 **读懂论文图表和实验证据的工具**。学生将在老师引导下，拆解一篇高质量论文的研究背景、研究问题、关键实验、主要图表、结论和局限性。项目结束时，**8-9 年级学生完成学术海报（poster）和论文口头报告（paper presentation）；10-12 年级学生完成一篇迷你综述（mini review）及论文口头报告（paper presentation）**，以上是一般情况，具体以学生水平为准。

*This project uses sickle cell disease as a real-world case study. Students will first understand how a single nucleotide mutation leads to abnormal hemoglobin structure, red blood cell sickling, anemia,*
and organ damage. They will then explore how CRISPR-Cas9 is used as a gene-editing tool to correct or regulate disease-related genes—a therapy approved by the FDA in 2023.
Techniques like PCR, Western blot, and sequencing are introduced only as tools to interpret figures and evidence. Under the instructor‘s guidance, students will break down a NEJM paper’s background, research question, key experiments, main figures, conclusions, and limitations. By the end: **Grades 8-9** produce an academic poster and presentation; **Grades 10-12** complete a mini-review paper and presentation. The above is the general rule, subject to students' actual ability.

# Parent Introduction 家长说明（中英）

本项目从“一个基因突变如何导致疾病”这一基础问题入手，以镰状细胞贫血为真实案例，帮助学生建立从基因、蛋白、疾病到基因治疗的科学思维路径。学生将学习阅读英文科研论文、理解实验图表、提取核心观点，并完成英文学术展示。

**本项目共 9 课时，根据学生年级（8-9 年级侧重海报+展示，10-12 年级侧重综述论文）设置不同产出要求，确保每个学生都能在适合的挑战度中成长。**

* **8-9 年级学生**：完成一份英文学术海报 + 5-8 分钟口头展示（含图表解读）。学术海报可直接作为美本申请的补充材料（Arts Supplement / Portfolio）。

* **10-12 年级学生**：完成一篇 800-1500 词英文迷你综述（mini review paper）+ 完整论文总结表 + 8-10 分钟展示及 Q&A。

项目成果可作为学术作品集核心素材，并为未来参加 **iGEM（国际基因工程机器大赛）、ISEF（国际科学与工程大奖赛）、Brain Bee（国际脑科学大赛）** 等顶级竞赛或生物医学方向申请奠定坚实基础。通过本项目，学生不仅巩固课内遗传学和分子生物学知识，更提前掌握大学水平的科研阅读与学术表达能力。

This project starts with a fundamental question—how can a single gene mutation cause disease?—using sickle cell disease as a real-world case study. Students will build a clear scientific thinking pathway: from genes and proteins to disease mechanisms and gene therapy. They will learn to read English research papers, interpret experimental figures, extract key findings, and deliver academic presentations in English.

**The project consists of 9 sessions with differentiated output requirements based on grade level (Grades 8-9 focus on poster + presentation; Grades 10-12 focus on a mini-review paper), ensuring that every student grows at an appropriate level of challenge.**

* **Grades 8-9**: Produce an academic poster in English + a 5-8 minute oral presentation with figure interpretation. The poster can be submitted directly as supplementary material (Arts Supplement/Portfolio) for U.S. college applications.

* **Grades 10-12**: Complete a 800-1500 word mini-review paper in English + a full paper summary sheet + an 8-10 minute presentation with Q&A.

The project outcomes serve as core assets for a student's academic portfolio and lay a solid foundation for future participation in top-tier competitions such as **iGEM, ISEF, Brain Bee**, or applications in biomedical sciences. Through this project, students not only reinforce their knowledge of genetics and molecular biology but also gain college-level skills in scientific reading and academic communication.

## Prerequisites 先修要求（中英）

* **生物基础**：已完成或正在学习遗传学，了解基因、突变、蛋白功能等基本概念

* **英语基础**：老托福 95+（阅读 23+）或新托福总分 5+或雅思总分 7+（或同等水平）

* **不需要** 任何实验操作经验或编程基础

* **Biology**: Have taken or be taking genetics, familiar with basic concepts of genes, mutations, and protein function

* **English**: Old TOEFL 95+ (Reading 23+) or New TOEFL total score of 5+ or IELTS total score of 7+ (or equivalent level)

* **No prior lab or programming experience required**

* 掌握遗传学、疾病机制和 CRISPR 相关的核心英文词汇

* 练习阅读英文科研论文，提取背景、问题、方法和结论
* 学习描述实验图表的学术表达：“*The figure shows...*”, “*The result suggests...*”, “*This experiment was designed to...*”

* 训练 Q&A 环节中用英文回答研究设计、实验结果和论文局限性相关问题

* Master key English vocabulary in genetics, disease mechanisms, and CRISPR

* Practice reading English research papers and identifying background, research question, methods, and conclusions

* Learn academic expressions for describing figures: “The figure shows…”, “The result suggests…”, “This experiment was designed to…”

* Practice explaining complex scientific concepts in clear, organized English

* Develop ability to answer questions about study design, results, and limitations during Q&A
