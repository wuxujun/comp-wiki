> [!info] 背景 
> - **主办方**:: Social Justice Watcher
> - **创办时间**::
> - **官网地址**::https://www.socialjusticewatcher.org/
> - **简介**:: 
社会公正奖Social Justice Awards（SJA）是由青少年非盈利组织Social Justice Watcher举办的写作、演讲与艺术大赛，旨在通过写作、演讲与艺术创作，引导青少年关注社会不平等现象，促使青年人思考并通过自身行动改变世界。每年比赛共分春季赛与秋季赛两场，每场主题不同，参赛学生可在写作、演讲、艺术三个类别中选择一个或多个类别，分别投递作品参赛。
> - **奖项设置**::每个类别都设有一等奖、二等奖、三等奖及入围奖，所有类别作品中也将评选出特别大奖，收获由American Scholastic Convention（ASC）提供的美国顶尖大学教授科研项目指定课题奖学金。

超越人性的正义:推进动物权利
Justice Beyond Humanity: Advancing Animal Rights

常规选题(20岁以下学生可选)
1.“Until he extends the circle of his compassion to all living things, man will not himself find peace."-Albert Schweitzer. How do you understand thisquote?
2.Should scientific innovation be slowed if it causes harm to animals, even when it benefits humans?
3.How should responsibility for animal treatment be distributed among individuals, corporations, andgovernments?
4.Share a personal story or observation about a time when you noticed how an animal's life was shaped by human choices or systems. What did the experience teach you about responsibility or
compassion?
Junior选题(15岁以下学生可选)
Why do you think people often treat different animals in different ways?
