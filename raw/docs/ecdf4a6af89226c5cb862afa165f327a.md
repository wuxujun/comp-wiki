# Personal Storytelling

**Question 1: What academic subjects does this course cover?**

This course is an introduction to the basics of a **nonfiction creative writing** genre called **personal narrative**, in which students tell stories and reflect on their own personal experiences. Students will learn the principles of **narrative writing** and will analyze examples stories from the genre, practicing their **English listening** skills. They will then use these principles to complete their own personal story, developing **English speaking and writing** skills.

**Question 2: What skills will students develop after completing the course?**

**Writing Skills:**

* **Narrative Structure:** Students will learn to create stories with a strong beginning and ending, an organizing narrative arc with a central internal and external arc, and a clear change and theme.

* **Supporting Details:** Students will choose strong illustrative details to craft vivid scenes and bring their stories to life

* **Personal Reflection:** Students will have the opportunity to reflect on a meaningful experience, what they learned, and how they changed. They will learn how to incorporate these reflections naturally into the structure of the essay rather than tacking them on at the end

**Performance Skills:**

* **Spoken Fluency:** Students will gain practice presenting and speaking English conversationally

* **Performance Style:** Students will learn performance techniques like pauses and emphasis to build audience engagement and enhance their story.

**Short-term benefits:**
**Improved English writing, speaking, and listening; understanding of the personal narrative genre and how to craft a story**

**Long-term benefits:**
Students gain confidence in self-expression (useful for high school and college and interviews) and personal reflection. Topics of personal narrative stories can be further developed into application essays.

## Question 3: Does this class have any prerequisites? What students are a good fit?

This course is suitable for students in grades 6-10.

**New to creative writing:** This class is designed for students with no prior experience with creative writing. It covers the basics of what students need to know to create their first personal narrative.

**60+ TOEFL:** Students should have a minimum TOEFL score of 60. Instruction and course material are entirely in English, so students should be comfortable reading English-language texts and participating in discussions in English

**Looking to improve clarity in their English-language speaking and writing:** The class includes both writing and speaking / performance, so this is a great fit for students looking to improve their skills in conversation and presentation.

**Interested in multimedia formats like podcasts:** This course helps students express their ideas through an audio format, great preparation for multimedia projects or podcast competitions like the *New York Times*.

## Question 4: What will students produce in this course?

Students will produce a **5-minute recording** of a nonfiction personal narrative story, including:

* An attention-grabbing **introduction**

* A detailed **middle** of the story, with scenes, details, and reflection

* A thoughtful **conclusion**

* An **outline** which they will use to help them perform the story

## Question 5: What can students do to continue exploring this topic

**Future Competitions:** Students may adapt or expand their article for submission to
* **New York Times Podcast**
* **Scholastic Art and Writing Award for Personal Narrative writing**
* **New York Times Tiny Memoir**

**Personalized extension:** Interested students could expand their project into
* **A personal website or podcast**
* **A creative writing portfolio**
