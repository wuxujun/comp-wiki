项目简介 Project Summary
该项目为全程线上1对1形式，学生将踏上一段跨文化的
神话探索之旅，了解世界各大文明如何通过神话塑造和崇
拜太阳。在互动式故事讲述、视觉化资料和创意活动的引
导下，学生将认识三位传奇太阳神：希腊的阿波罗、古埃
及的拉以及中国的羲和。课程将帮助学生掌握神话相关词
汇、描述与比较句型，并提升叙事写作能力，同时加深跨
文化认知。学生不会被动阅读神话，而是积极创作：绘制
“太阳神身份证”卡片，概述神祇的形象、起源与能力；用
简洁的英文撰写神话故事摘要；制作对比图表，分析不同
文化的价值观异同。
In this fully online 1-on-1 project, students will embark on a
cross-cultural journey to uncover how civilizations across the
world imagined and celebrated the Sun through mythology.
Guided by interactive storytelling, visual resources, and
creative tasks, they will explore three legendary figures: Apollo
from Greek mythology, Ra from ancient Egypt, and Xihe from
Chinese legend. Students will master myth-related vocabulary,
descriptive and comparative sentence structures, and develop
narrative writing skills, while also deepening their cultural
awareness.
Instead of passively reading myths, students will actively
create: designing "Sun God ID Cards" to summarize each
deity's appearance, origin, and powers; crafting concise
English summaries of each myth; and building comparison
charts to analyze similarities and differences in cultural values.
A Message to Parents
本项目为学生提供一次将文学、历史、艺术与英语学习相
结合的独特体验。通过神话探索，他们不仅会扩充词汇、
提升写作能力，还会培养跨文化比较、分析性思维与创意
表达的能力。研究、设计和展示的过程将强化学生有逻辑
地组织信息与清晰表达观点的能力。最终的数字成果将成
为其学术成长与艺术创造力的有形记录。
This project offers a rare opportunity for students to combine
literature, history, art, and English language learning in a
single, engaging experience. Through myth exploration, they
will not only expand vocabulary and improve writing but also
develop skills in cultural comparison, analytical thinking, and
creative expression. The process of researching, designing,
and presenting will strengthen their ability to structure
information logically and communicate ideas clearly. The final
digital products will serve as a tangible record of their
academic growth and artistic creativity.

comparison charts, and creative reinterpretations of
three sun gods.

"Cultural Reflection Essay"
一篇50-150词的英文反思，探讨神话揭示的人类

reveal about human imagination and cultural identity.
