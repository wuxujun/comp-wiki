> - **简介**:: 
The New York Times Annual Student Podcast Contest, initiated by The New York Times, is a competition open to global students aged 10-19. The podcast contest encourages students worldwide to submit original podcast episodes on various topics and in different styles. The aim is to expose students to a broader media audience through the creation of innovative and diverse podcast content.
> - **奖项设置**::In the 2024 edition of this competition, over 1100 high school students from around the world submitted original podcast content of five minutes or less. From this pool of over 1100 participants, The New York Times podcast editors, along with professionals in the podcast industry and education scholars across the United States, selected 11 winners, 12 runners-up, and 35 honorable mentions. The probability of winning was 1%.

In the 2023 edition of this competition, over 1100 high school students from around the world submitted original podcast content of five minutes or less. From this pool of over 1100 participants, The New York Times podcast editors, along with professionals in the podcast industry and education scholars across the United States, selected 11 winners, 14 runners-up, and 29 honorable mentions. The probability of winning was 1%.
