# 数位叙事创作营：面向新世代的新媒体表达
Digital Storytelling Studio: New Media for a New Audience

<mark>6课时(CT) + 1课时(NCT)</mark>

Digital media icons: list, TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts, Reels

## 项目简介 Project Summary

学生将学习如何把电影叙事转化为新媒体内容，如 TikTok、YouTube Shorts、Instagram Reels。他们会分析爆款短视频和网络剧，学习如何抓住观众注意力、掌握节奏与风格。随后，学生将构思一套短视频系列(3 集，每集 30-60 秒)，撰写脚本并用基础剪辑软件完成。

Students explore how cinematic storytelling adapts to new digital platforms like TikTok, YouTube Shorts, or Instagram Reels. They study examples of viral storytelling and short-form web series, analyzing hooks, pacing, and platform-specific style. Each student develops a concept for a short "digital series" (3 episodes, 30-60 seconds each), writes scripts, and produces the videos using simple editing tools.

## <mark>A Message to Parents</mark>

这个项目让孩子在创意写作与新媒体表达中双重提升。他们将学习如何为当下流行的短视频平台设计故事，练习简洁剧本写作、视觉设计与剪辑。不仅能创作有趣的视频，更能理解媒体如何影响生活，提升数字表达与媒介素养。

This project blends creativity with modern communication skills. Your child will learn how to craft stories for today's digital platforms, practicing concise scriptwriting, visual design, and editing. Beyond fun and engaging videos, this project helps them understand how media works in our everyday lives and equips them with creative digital literacy.
