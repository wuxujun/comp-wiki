# 073025 旋律时光机：我的音乐故事播客-李昕怡

1. PBL Project Name 项目名称：

* 中文：旋律时光机：我的音乐故事播客

* English: Melodies and Memories: My Story Through Music Podcast comment icon

2. AA Instructor 学术指导老师：

* 中文：李昕怡

* English: Xinyi Li

3. Original PBL Topic 原始英文题目：

* 中文：旋律与记忆：我的音乐自述播客

* English: Melodies and Memories: A Personal Music Memoir Podcast

4. Project Summary 项目简介：

* 中文：

本项目将引导学生围绕一段与音乐相关的情绪记忆，构思并完成一期播客节目。他们将在老师的引导下进行旋律联想、情绪词汇积累、故事结构构建等练习，并最终用英文完成一篇播客脚本，并配音制作播客音频。课程将通过“音乐触发记忆——情绪表达——播客讲述”这一学习路径，融合听觉审美与语言表达，帮助学生实现从生活体验到英文表达的跨越。

* English:

This project guides students to reflect on an emotional memory connected to music and produce a podcast episode based on that moment. Through guided exercises on melody association, emotional vocabulary building, and storytelling structure, students will draft an English podcast script and record an audio version. The learning pathway—Music evokes memory → Emotion expression → Podcast storytelling—combines auditory appreciation with English language production, bridging real-life experience and narrative expression.

5. Parent Introduction 家长说明：

* 中文：

“旋律时光机：我的音乐故事播客”项目不仅提升了孩子的英语表达能力，更激发了他们对情绪与记忆的觉察力。在项目中，孩子将学会用英文讲述真实的自己，锻炼情感表达力、语言组织力和公众表达信心，并通过播客这一形式获得创造与成就感。

* English:

This project enhances students' English language skills while deepening their emotional awareness and self-reflection. By narrating personal stories in English, students build their emotional expression, narrative structure, and public speaking
confidence. The podcast format also fosters creativity and a sense of accomplishment.

## 6. Target Age Grade 适合年级：

* 中文：适合 5 至 8 年级学生

* English: Suitable for students in Grades 5–8

## 7. Subjects Integrated 跨学科整合：

* 中文：音乐欣赏 + 情绪表达 + 播客写作 + 英语表达

* English: Music Appreciation + Emotional Expression + Podcast Writing + English Communication

## 8. Final Products 项目成果：

* 中文：英文播客脚本 + 英文播客音频

* English: English Podcast Script + Podcast Audio Recording

## 9. English Learning Outcomes 英语学习关联：

* 中文：

学生将在项目中提升以下英语技能：

用英文描写情绪和感受

构建具有起承转合的故事结构

撰写口语化播客脚本

练习英文语音语调与朗读技巧

* English:

Students will develop the following English skills during the project:

Describing emotions and feelings in English

Structuring a narrative with clarity and flow

Writing natural and engaging podcast scripts

Practicing pronunciation, intonation, and oral fluency

## 10. English Deliverables 英文产出：

* 中文：

项目中要求学生以英文完成以下成果：

播客英文脚本

播客音频配音（英文朗读）

英文标题与节目标语文案

* English:

Students are expected to produce the following in English:
English podcast script
English voice recording of the podcast
English title and tagline for their episode
