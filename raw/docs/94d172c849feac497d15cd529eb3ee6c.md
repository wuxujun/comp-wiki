# 为什么瑞幸咖啡可以卖得比星巴克便宜很多，却仍然能够盈利？
Why can Luckin Coffee sell coffee much cheaper than Starbucks and still remain profitable?

6课时(CT) + 1课时(NCT)

Luckin Coffee vs Starbucks illustration with charts and coins

## 项目简介 Project Summary

学生将通过分析真实商业案例（Luckin Coffee 与 Starbucks），理解企业如何通过定价策略、成本结构、规模效应和数字化运营实现低价与盈利的平衡。
学生将通过数据分析与商业模拟，完成定价实验和盈利预测，并最终提出自己的商业策略。

Through case analysis and business simulations, students will learn key concepts including pricing strategy, cost structure, economies of scale, and digital business models. Students will build pricing simulations, analyze profit models, and propose their own business strategy.

<mark>A Message to Parents</mark>

本PBL课程旨在帮助学生建立商业思维与现实世界问题解决能力。通过模拟企业决策情境，学生将提升以下能力：
* 理解企业如何赚钱
* 学会分析价格与成本之间的关系
* 运用数据和图表进行商业分析
* 用英语表达商业观点和策略
* 提升逻辑表达与公开演讲能力

This project builds real-world financial awareness and critical thinking skills. Students will:

* Understand how businesses generate profit

* Analyze the relationship between price and cost

* Use charts and data for business reasoning

* Communicate business ideas in English

* Develop structured thinking and presentation skills

## 适合学生 Target Students

* 难度将根据学生的英语水平进行调整
* 高年级学生将进行更深入的商业分析与策略讨论

* The level of difficulty will be adjusted according to students' English proficiency.

* Older students will engage in deeper strategic and economic analysis.
