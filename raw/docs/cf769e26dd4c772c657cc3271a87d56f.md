# 《运动心力量：青少年运动心理探索之旅》

Sports & Mind Power: A Teen's Journey into Exercise Psychology

**AA Instructor 指导老师:**

Cory

**Project Summary 项目简介:**

In this fully online 1-on-1 project, students will embark on a journey to explore the profound connection between physical activity and mental well-being. While engaging in self-directed offline exercise, students will learn to keenly observe their physical sensations and emotional responses. They will delve into practical mental strategies for peak performance, such as effective goal setting, visualization, and positive self-talk. Through structured exercise journaling and reflective writing, students will document their experiences, analyze their psychological states, and articulate their personal growth.

该项目为线上 1 对 1 远程形式，学生将踏上一段探索体育活动与心理健康之间深刻联系的旅程。在自主进行线下运动的同时，学生将学会敏锐地观察自己的身体感受和情绪反应。他们将深入学习针对巅峰表现的实用心理策略，例如高效的目标设定、可视化训练和积极的自我对话。通过结构化的运动日志和反思性写作，学生将记录他们的经历，分析他们的心理状态，并清晰表达他们的个人成长。

**Parent Introduction 家长说明:**

This project offers a unique opportunity for students to develop a holistic understanding of their physical and mental selves. By integrating movement, psychology, and writing, students will significantly enhance their self-awareness, emotional regulation, and resilience. They will gain practical tools for improving focus, motivation, and performance not only in sports but also in academic and daily life challenges. Furthermore, the project will cultivate critical thinking, analytical and narrative writing skills, and the ability to apply psychological concepts in real-world contexts, fostering a healthier and more reflective approach to personal growth.

本项目为学生提供了一个独特的机会，让他们全面理解自己的身体和心理。通过整合运动、心理学和写作，学生将显著提升自我意识、情绪调节能力和心理韧性。他们将获得提高专注力、动力和表现的实用工具，这些工具不仅适用于运动，也适用于学业和日常生活的挑战。此外，本项目还将培养批判性思维、分析性和叙事性写作能力，以及在现实情境中应用心理学概念的能力，从而促进学生以更健康、更具反思性的方式实现个人成长。

## 策略一：积极自我对话

Strategy 1: Positive Self-Talk

### 什么是积极自我对话？

积极自我对话就是你在心里对自己说的话。它能帮助你建立自信，保持专注，并在遇到困难时坚持下去。

### What is Positive Self-Talk?

Positive self-talk is the inner voice you use to talk to yourself. It helps build confidence, maintain focus, and persevere

### 如何运用？

*   **消极 → 积极：** 把“我做不到”变成“我可以尝试！”

*   **具体化：** “我能坚持到终点！”而不是“我不会放弃。”

*   **现在时：** “我现在很专注。”而不是“我应该专注。”

### How to Use It?

*   **Negative → Positive:** Change "I can't do it" to "I can try!"
